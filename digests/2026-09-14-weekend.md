It was the index's **fifth straight losing week**. India VIX (the volatility
gauge) rose **4.15%** over the week, pointing to rising trader nervousness.
On Friday alone, Sensex slipped 120.83 points (−0.16%) and Nifty fell 79.70
points (−0.34%) to close just below the 23,400 mark.

## Global Cues — the big story is oil
- **Geopolitical shock:** Saudi Arabia shut its critical 1,200km East-West
  crude pipeline (which bypasses the Strait of Hormuz) after drone attacks
  launched from Iraq on 10 September, followed by further attacks near the
  Strait of Hormuz. The pipeline carries roughly **4% of global oil supply**.
- **Crude prices spiked hard:** Brent crude jumped over 3.5% to above
  **$107–108/barrel**; WTI approached **$103/barrel**. US diesel prices
  crossed **$6/gallon**, a record high. This is a fast-moving, still-unfolding
  story worth tracking over the weekend into Monday's open.
- **Gold** stood at **$4,461/oz**, benefiting from safe-haven demand as
  geopolitical risk rose.
- **USD/INR** was at **95.56** as of Friday — a weaker rupee alongside costlier
  crude is a combination that typically pressures Indian equities and raises
  inflation concern.
- **Wall Street, by contrast, rallied Friday** on cooler inflation data: the
  Dow gained 509 points (+0.98%) to 52,573.29, the S&P 500 rose 0.86% to
  7,656.98, and the Nasdaq climbed 0.96% to 26,333.04 — a positive global cue
  that sits in tension with the oil-driven risk-off mood.

## Macro & Policy
- **RBI** held the repo rate at **5.25%** at its August review — the fourth
  consecutive pause. The **next MPC meeting is 5–7 October 2026**, which
  markets will watch closely for any shift in tone given the oil-driven
  inflation risk.
- **SEBI**: from 1 September 2026, every new single-holder demat account and
  mutual fund folio must carry either a nomination or a signed opt-out
  declaration (per a SEBI circular dated 29 May 2026).
- **RBI**: the special FCNR(B) dollar-rupee swap window closed on 31 August;
  fresh NRI FCNR(B) deposits from September no longer get the concessional
  pricing that had pushed rates to roughly 6–7%.
- **RBI + SEBI jointly launched "Demat 2.0"** at the Global Fintech Fest
  2026 in Mumbai — a pilot combining tokenised securities, distributed
  ledger technology and central bank digital currency to test faster
  issuance, holding, trading and settlement of corporate bonds.

## FII / DII Activity
- FIIs were **net sellers** over the week — cumulative net outflows of
  roughly **₹1,795 crore** — continuing a cautious stance amid global
  volatility.
- **DIIs stepped up buying**, providing an offsetting cushion: for example,
  net cash-segment buying of **₹1,968 crore on 11 September** alone, after a
  much larger **₹8,930 crore** net buy on 4 September (DIIs bought ₹19,254
  crore and sold ₹10,324 crore that day) even as FIIs sold ₹3,112 crore.
- Net read: domestic flows are currently the market's main support against
  persistent foreign outflows.

## What to Watch This Week (15–19 September)
1. **Crude oil trajectory** — the Saudi pipeline shutdown and Strait of
   Hormuz tensions are the single biggest swing factor for Indian markets
   right now, via inflation, the rupee, and the current account.
2. **Technical levels** — Nifty broke below the 23,500 support zone; expected
   trading range is **23,000–23,800**, with support near **22,800** and
   resistance at **24,000–24,200**. A reclaim of 23,700–23,900 would help
   confirm a recovery.
3. **FII flow trend** — whether foreign outflows persist or ease as the oil
   shock evolves, and whether DIIs can keep absorbing the selling.
4. **IPO-heavy calendar** — four new issues open this week alone; primary
   market appetite is a useful read on broader sentiment.
5. **Run-up to the October RBI policy** — commentary ahead of the 5–7 October
   MPC meeting will be watched for early signals given rising oil-driven
   inflation risk.
6. **Global cues** — continued divergence between a resilient Wall Street and
   a jittery, oil-sensitive Indian market bears watching into the week.
