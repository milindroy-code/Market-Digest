Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Long Straddle

Long Straddle ka matlab hai same strike aur same expiry par ek call aur ek put dono khareedna. Aap profit karte hain agar underlying koi bhi direction mein ek bada move karta hai — aap volatility par bet laga rahe hote hain, kisi specific direction par nahi. Cost dono options ka combined premium hota hai, jo aapka maximum loss bhi hai agar stock bilkul flat reh jaaye. Traders ise binary events se pehle use karte hain — jaise ek Fed decision ya earnings call — bilkul waisa hi setup jaisa kal (3 November) ke US midterm elections create kar rahe hain. Yeh sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, ek high-volatility tag ke saath. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
