Explainer: Realty Aur FMCG — Do Bilkul Alag Sectors — Ek Hi Din Top Kaise Rahe?

Aaj Nifty Realty (+2.75%) aur Nifty FMCG (+2.34%) dono top performers rahe — do sectors jo business model ke hisaab se ek-dusre se bilkul alag hain. Inki wajah bhi alag-alag thi, ek hi macro trigger nahi. Realty typically ek "rate-sensitive" sector hai: iske developers heavily debt-financed hote hain, aur homebuyers bhi loans par depend karte hain. Jab US Fed apna rate hold karta hai (ek hike ki jagah) toh global bond yields ke range-bound rehne ki possibility badhti hai, jisse capital-intensive, debt-heavy businesses ko relief milta hai. FMCG ki story alag hai: ye ek "defensive" sector hai jiska margin crude-linked input costs (packaging, transport, raw materials) se juda hota hai — Thursday ke softer Brent crude ($114, -1.4%) ne direct cost relief diya, aur Budhwar ke IT-led selloff ke baad investors earnings-visibility wale consumption names mein bhi rotate hue. Lesson: jab index-level rally dikhti hai, har sector ka apna alag "why" ho sakta hai — ek single headline cause dhoondna hamesha accurate nahi hota.

Risk-Reward Ratio

Koi bhi trade enter karne se pehle, compare karo ki aap kitna lose kar sakte ho (stop-loss tak ki distance) us hisaab se ki aap kitna gain kar sakte ho (target tak ki distance).
