Explainer: Hawkish Fed Minutes Ke Din Bhi Sirf IT Hi Kyun Doob Gaya

Aaj subah ke hawkish US FOMC minutes ne pura market hila diya, lekin close tak sirf Nifty IT hi red mein raha jabki baaki poora market 1%+ upar band hua. Iski wajah simple hai: Indian IT majors (TCS, Infosys, HCL Technologies, Tech Mahindra) apna revenue mostly US/Europe se earn karte hain — unka business US rate cycle, US corporate IT-spending budgets aur dollar-rupee rate se directly juda hota hai. Jab Fed "higher for longer" signal deta hai, toh US companies apna tech-spending budget tight karte hain — yeh IT stocks ke liye ek direct earnings-risk hai, na ki sirf sentiment ka asar. Doosri taraf, Auto aur Bank jaise domestic-demand-driven sectors ka link US rates se zyada indirect hai — unke liye zyada important hai India ki apni consumption, credit growth aur FII inflows, jo aaj bhi strong rahe. Yahi wajah hai ki ek hi "hawkish Fed" headline do bilkul alag reactions de sakti hai — ek hi din mein IT doob sakta hai aur Auto/Bank surge kar sakte hain.

The Iron Butterfly

Iron condor ka ek tighter cousin: at-the-money call aur put dono sell karo, aur protection ke liye further out-of-the-money call aur put buy karo. Yeh sabse zyada payout deta hai agar underlying middle strike par hi finish ho — ek condor ke comparison mein higher potential reward deta hai ek aisi market ke liye jo bahut still rehne ki expect ki jaaye, lekin iske against trade-off hai ek narrower profitable range. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "range-bound" tag ke saath — aaj ka session khud iska ek direct mismatch hai: Nifty/Sensex dono 1%+ ki trending rally mein band hue, range-bound nahi — isko transparently flag kiya ja raha hai, kyunki rotation sequence follow kiya jaata hai, har din ke market-condition ke hisaab se cherry-pick nahi. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
