The Iron Butterfly

Iron Condor ka ek tighter cousin: ek at-the-money call aur put sell karo, aur protection ke liye usse aur door out-of-the-money call aur put buy karo. Sabse zyada payout tab milta hai jab underlying exactly middle strike par close ho — condor se higher potential reward deta hai un markets ke liye jo bahut still rehne ki ummeed ho, lekin profitable range narrower hota hai. Bilkul aaj jaisa din, jahan Sensex-Nifty dono tight range mein flat hain, na koi gap-up na koi crash. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, trade placement ki recommendation nahi.
