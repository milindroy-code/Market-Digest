Explainer: Open Offer Actually Kaam Kaise Karta Hai

Jab Tata Steel jaisa koi acquirer kisi listed company mein 25%+ stake khareedta hai, SEBI ke Takeover Code (SAST Regulations, 2011) ke mutabiq usse baaki public shareholders ko bhi ek "mandatory open offer" deni padti hai — kam se kam additional 26% stake ke liye, ek fixed price par. Process kuch defined steps mein chalta hai: pehle ek public announcement, phir SEBI ke paas ek "draft letter of offer" file hota hai; SEBI ke comments ke baad ek "identified date" (record date jaisi concept) fix hoti hai jo decide karti hai kaun eligible hai; phir ek formal "letter of offer" eligible shareholders ko bheja jaata hai; aur phir ek ~10-working-day ki "tendering window" khulti hai jismein shareholders apne shares offer price par bech sakte hain (ya nahi bhi bech sakte, optional hai). Poora process typically announcement se lekar payment tak kayi hafton ka hota hai. Tata Steel-APL Apollo deal ke liye yeh formal timeline — record date, letter of offer ki date — is run mein kahin announce nahi hui thi; jaise-jaise filings aayengi, is digest mein update kiya jayega.

Bull Call Spread

Bull Call Spread mein tum ek lower-strike call khareedte ho aur simultaneously ek higher-strike call bechte ho, same expiry ke saath — isse tumhara cost aur maximum profit dono capped ho jaate hain ek outright call khareedne ke comparison mein, jo ek moderately-bullish view ko sasta express karne ka tarika banata hai, unlimited upside ke badle ek lower breakeven ke liye. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "uptrend" tag ke saath — aaj khud koi trading session nahi hai, lekin yeh concept ek forward-looking frame deta hai: agar Budhwar ko Somwar ke selloff se ek recovery bounce aata hai, toh Bull Call Spread hoti hai us move ko cheaply capture karne ka ek tarika, bina unlimited-upside exposure liye. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ya Budhwar ke move ki prediction nahi.
