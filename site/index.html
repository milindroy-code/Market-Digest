Explainer: Pyramiding vs. Averaging Down

Jab Tech Mahindra jaisa stock ek hi din mein 7.75% chadh jaata hai, toh ek purana trading dilemma phir se saamne aata hai: kya aage aur buy karein ya jo gains ban chuke hain unhe lock karein? Iska jawab is baat par depend karta hai ki aap "averaging down" kar rahe hain ya "pyramiding." Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur paisa daalna taaki average cost kam ho jaaye — risky hai, kyunki aap ek aise trade mein add kar rahe hain jo already aapke against ja raha hai. Pyramiding iska opposite hai: ek winning position mein add karna jab woh aapke favor mein move kar raha ho. Zyada tar experienced traders pyramiding ko prefer karte hain, kyunki ismein market khud aapki thesis ko confirm kar raha hota hai, na ki aap usse fight kar rahe hote hain. Aaj ka IT rally exactly aisa hi ek setup hai jahan yeh concept relevant hota hai.
