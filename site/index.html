Explainer: Crude Oil Chadhne Se Indian Stock Market Kyun Hilta Hai

India apni crude oil zaroorat ka ~85% import karta hai, isliye jab Brent jaisa global benchmark chadhta hai — jaisa aaj Gulf shipping attacks aur Iran war stalemate ki wajah se hua — iska asar seedha teen channels se Indian markets tak pahunchta hai. Pehla: import bill badh jaata hai, jo current account deficit ko widen karta hai aur rupee par pressure daalta hai. Doosra: mehenga crude seedha petrol-diesel aur transportation costs badhakar retail inflation (CPI) ko upar dhakelta hai, jisse RBI ke rate-cut ki umeedon par asar padta hai. Teesra: oil-sensitive sectors (aviation, paints, tyres, FMCG logistics) ke input costs badh jaate hain, jo unki margins ko squeeze karte hain — yahi wajah hai ki aaj jaisa din aata hai jab ek pure macro trigger (crude) poore market ko IT-led gap-up se seedha red territory mein le jaata hai, bina kisi company-specific news ke.
