Explainer: Markets Confirmed Bure News Se Pehle Hi Kyun Rally Kar Sakte Hain

Kal Asia aur Europe dono strongly upar band hue — Dalal Street ke liye bhi ek relief-signal — sirf is wajah se ki ek proposed US tariff ab shayad pehle-flagged 25%/17% se kam, kahin 15-20% ke aas-paas aa sakta hai. Note karne wali baat: yeh koi confirmed, final number nahi hai, phir bhi market ne ise already price-in kar diya. Aisa isliye hota hai kyunki markets hamesha "worst-case" ko hi default price-in karte hain jab tak kuch aur confirmed na ho — ek 25% tariff ka risk jab tak zinda hai, stocks us poore downside ko discount karte hain. Jaise hi ek credible signal aata hai ki actual number kam ho sakta hai (chahe woh abhi bhi unconfirmed ho), market turant us reduced-risk ko re-price karta hai — chahe koi official announcement na hua ho. Isi wajah se traders kehte hain "markets move on expectations, not outcomes." Risk yahan yeh hai: agar final number phir se 25% nikla (ya India us "capped" list mein na aaya), to aaj ka relief-rally bhi utni hi tezi se revert ho sakta hai.

The Iron Butterfly

Iron condor ka ek tighter cousin: at-the-money call aur put bechiye, aur further out-of-the-money call/put protection ke liye khareediye. Ismein payout sabse zyada hota hai jab underlying bilkul middle strike par close ho — ek condor se zyada potential reward deta hai ek aise market ke liye jo bahut still rehne ki ummeed ho, lekin cost par ek narrower profitable range. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "range-bound" tag ke saath — jo aaj ke actual conditions se ek reasonable fit hai: pichle teen din ki aggressive rally ke baad, kal ka session (+0.25-0.46%, late-selling ne gains trim kiye) aur aaj ka sirf-halka GIFT Nifty signal dono is baat ka sanket dete hain ki market abhi ek calmer, narrower-range phase mein move kar raha hai — bilkul wahi environment jahan ek Iron Butterfly jaisi strategy traditionally consider ki jaati hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
