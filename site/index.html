Explainer: Profit Girne Par Bhi Stock Kyun Chadh Sakta Hai

Aaj ka sabse counter-intuitive move: Dr. Reddy's Laboratories ka profit 36% YoY gira, phir bhi stock ~6.62% chadh gaya. Yeh kaise possible hai? Markets stocks ko absolute number par price nahi karte — woh usse expectations ke against price karte hain. Dr. Reddy's ka pichla quarter (Q1 FY27) ek semaglutide-related one-time provision ki wajah se profit mein 69% YoY ka crash dekh chuka tha, aur management ne tab H2 FY27 mein improvement ka guidance diya tha. Is quarter ka 36% decline — bura hone ke bawajood — us trend ko sahi saabit karta hai: giravat ka pace slow ho raha hai, jaisa guide kiya gaya tha. Jab actual result "expected-se-kam-bura" nikalta hai, ya forward commentary positive hoti hai, stock chadh sakta hai chahe headline YoY number negative hi kyun na ho. Yeh ek common pattern hai results season mein — hamesha dekho ki number pichle trend/guidance ke against kaisa hai, sirf YoY headline nahi.

Max Pain Theory

Yeh idea hai ki ek underlying expiry tak us strike price ki taraf gravitate karta hai jahan option buyers collectively sabse zyada paisa lose karte hain — kyunki option sellers (aksar bade, better-capitalized players) us outcome se benefit karte hain. Yeh ek widely-watched lekin imperfect signal hai, ek input ke roop mein useful hai na ki ek guaranteed prediction ke roop mein. Kal (27 Oct) October F&O expiry hone wali hai, isliye yeh concept khaas taur par timely hai — max pain level ke around price action often expiry ke din zyada pronounced hota hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
