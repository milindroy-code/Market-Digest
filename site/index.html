Explainer: Ek Hi Company Ke Results "Q2" Aur "Q3" Dono Kyun Kehlate Hain

Aaj ABB India ke Jul-Sep quarter ke results ko kahin "Q2 FY27" aur kahin "Q3 FY27" bataya gaya — dono hi sahi hain, bas alag reference frame se. India mein zyada companies ka financial year 1 April se shuru hota hai, isliye unka Q1 = Apr-Jun, Q2 = Jul-Sep, Q3 = Oct-Dec — is hisaab se Jul-Sep results "Q2 FY27" kehlate hain. Lekin kuch analysts/platforms calendar-quarter convention follow karte hain (Jan-Mar = Q1, Apr-Jun = Q2, Jul-Sep = Q3), jisse wahi results "Q3 FY27" ban jaate hain. Yeh sirf ek labeling quirk nahi hai — agar aap kisi stock ka YoY ya QoQ comparison kar rahe hain aur do sources ka quarter-label match nahi karta, to aap galti se do alag quarters ko compare kar sakte hain (jaisa aaj ABB India ke ₹362cr aur ₹442.52cr figures ke saath hua, jo asal mein do alag quarters the, ek hi quarter ke do readings nahi). Isliye best practice hamesha yeh hai ki exact date-range ("July-September 2026") dekhen, sirf quarter-label par bharosa na karen.

Bear Put Spread

Bull call spread ka mirror-image: ek higher-strike put buy karna, aur ek lower-strike put sell karna. Yeh ek bearish bet ka cost reduce karta hai (ek put outright buy karne ke against), lekin agar stock sharply girta hai toh aapka max-profit bhi cap ho jaata hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "downtrend" tag ke saath — lekin aaj ka session close tak green hi raha (Sensex +0.52%, Nifty +0.38%), sirf dopahar ke gains ka ek fade hua, pura downtrend nahi — ek acknowledged mismatch. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
