Explainer

Long Straddle

Ek Long Straddle mein same strike aur expiry par ek call aur ek put dono buy kiye jaate hain. Profit tab hota hai jab underlying kisi bhi direction mein ek bada move karta hai — yahan bet direction par nahi, volatility par hi hoti hai. Cost dono options ke combined premium jitna hota hai, jo maximum loss bhi hai agar stock flat reh jaaye. Aaj ka setup exactly woh scenario hai jahan traders is tarah ki strategy reach karte hain: ek taraf GIFT Nifty halka positive hai, doosri taraf Asia mein AI-stocks ka ek sharp crash ho chuka hai aur raat ko Nasdaq bhi affected hua — direction clear nahi hai, lekin volatility elevated hai.
