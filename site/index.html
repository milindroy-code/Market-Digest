Explainer: Rate Hold Ke Bawajood Nikkei Kyun Gira — Aur Nifty IT Kyun Nahi?

Koi bhi rate decision market ko utna apne decision se move nahi karta jitna apne signal se karta hai agle decision ke baare mein. Aaj Bank of Japan ne apna policy rate 1.25% par hold kiya — bilkul expected ke mutabiq — lekin ek board member ne turant 1.5% tak hike ka prastav rakha, central bank ne apna inflation forecast raise kiya, aur aage tightening ke liye readiness signal ki, ab economists December ya January mein hike price kar rahe hain. Yahi "hawkish hold" framing hai — rate khud unchanged rehne ke bajaye — jiski wajah se yen firm hua. Isse alag, Japan ka Nikkei phir bhi ~3% gira — BOJ ki wajah se nahi, balki AI-linked chip aur hardware stocks mein ek fresh selloff ki wajah se (wahi worry jo is hafte Alphabet aur Amazon ke results ko hit kar gayi thi), jo Asian tech mein phail gaya. India ke apne IT majors — Wipro aur Infosys inmein shamil — ne ise ignore kiya aur aaj ki rally lead ki — kyunki Indian IT services companies chips nahi banati ya AI data centres nahi chalati, toh ek global AI-infrastructure scare, ajeeb tarike se, unhe ek safer, cheaper trade bana sakta hai.

Delta Hedging

Delta measure karta hai ki ek option ka price underlying mein ₹1 ke move par kitna move karta hai. Large options traders aur market makers apni book ke delta ko offset karne ke liye underlying stock buy ya sell karte hain, taaki woh chhote price moves ke against roughly neutral reh sakein — yahi tarika hai jisse institutional desks bina simply direction par bet kiye options ko size mein sell kar sakte hain. Yeh sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, ek "high-volatility" tag ke saath — BOJ ke dissenting vote aur global tech volatility dono hi is tag ko fit karte hain. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
