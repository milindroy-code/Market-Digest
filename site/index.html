Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Covered Call

Agar tumhare paas pehle se ek stock hai, to usi ke against ek call option sell karo. Tumhe premium income ke roop mein milta hai, aur agar stock strike ke neeche rehta hai to tum stock aur premium dono rakhte ho. Trade-off: agar stock strike ke upar sharply rally karta hai, to tum us upar ke gains ko miss karte ho, kyunki tum us price par sell karne ke liye obligated ho. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "any" tag ke saath — aaj jaisa ek do-tarfa, sharply-reversing session (jahan APL Apollo/Tata Steel jaisi single-stock moves ne poore index ko hila diya) un investors ke liye ek relevant concept hai jo already apni positions hold kar rahe hain aur extra income generate karna chahte hain. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
