Explainer: Earnings Beat Karne Ke Baad Bhi Stock Zyada Upar Kyun Nahi Jaata

Aaj raat Nvidia ne apne Q3 FY27 results mein har number beat kiya — revenue $115.84 billion (+103% YoY, estimate $109.1 billion se upar), EPS $2.68 (estimate $2.47 se upar), aur agle quarter ka guidance $130 billion, jo $125.25 billion ke street estimate se bhi zyada hai. Phir bhi stock after-hours mein sirf ~4.6% hi upar gaya. Wajah hai ek simple mechanism: options market pehle se hi ek bada move price kar chuka tha — is case mein ~8% ka swing, dono directions mein. Jab actual result us implied move se kam "surprise" deta hai — chahe woh beat hi kyun na ho — to stock price us already-priced-in expectation ke against hi react karta hai, raw numbers ke against nahi. Isi wajah se traders kehte hain "buy the rumor, sell the news": jab tak koi result genuinely expectations se bahut zyada door nahi jaata, price us "priced-in" level ke aas-paas hi settle ho jaata hai, chahe underlying business performance kitna bhi strong kyun na ho.

Calendar Spread

Ek near-term option sell karna aur ek longer-dated option same strike par buy karna. Near-term option fast decay karta hai (time decay expiry ke kareeb aate-aate accelerate hota hai), isliye agar stock roughly flat rehta hai, to position in decay-rates ke difference se profit kar sakta hai — ek strategy jo time par based hai, direction par nahi. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "range-bound" tag ke saath — aaj ke conflicting GIFT Nifty signals (ek weak, ek flat) aur Nvidia ke khud muted stock-reaction ke baad ek indecisive, do-tarah-ka-din ban sakta hai, isliye yeh tag reasonably fit karta hai.
