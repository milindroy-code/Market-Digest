Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Bear Put Spread

Bull call spread ka mirror-image: ek higher strike par put khareedo, aur ek lower strike par put becho. Isse ek bearish bet ka cost kam ho jaata hai ek put outright khareedne ke muqable, lekin iske badle agar stock sharply girta hai to aapka maximum profit bhi cap ho jaata hai. Yeh sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, "downtrend" tag ke saath — lekin aaj ka fit sirf partial hai: yeh ek mild midday reversal hai, ek clean downtrend nahi, isliye is concept ko aaj ke jaisa ek cautious, wait-and-watch session ke context mein dekhna zyada sahi hoga, na ki ek strong directional call ke roop mein. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
