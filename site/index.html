Explainer: "Retest" Kya Hota Hai?

Aaj ke analyst commentary mein baar-baar ek term suna gaya — Nifty ab apne August high ~24,800 ko "retest" karne ki raah par hai. Jab koi index pehli baar kisi level tak pahunchta hai (jaise August mein 24,800) aur phir gir jaata hai, toh woh level ek "resistance" ban jaata hai — ek psychological/technical ceiling jahan sellers aa sakte hain. Jab market baad mein usi level ke paas wapas aata hai, usse "retest" kehte hain, aur yeh ek crucial moment hota hai: agar index us resistance ko decisively cross kar le aur hold bhi kare, toh woh purana resistance ab naya support ban jaata hai (technical analysts isse "role reversal" bhi kehte hain), aur yeh ek strong bullish confirmation signal maana jaata hai. Lekin agar index wahi se phir se reject ho jaaye, toh woh resistance aur bhi strong ho jaata hai. Yahi wajah hai ki aaj ke 24,500 reclaim ko analysts itni close se watch kar rahe hain — agle kuch sessions yeh decide karenge ki kya yeh genuine lagataar chhe-session rally ab 24,800 tak jaane ka momentum rakhti hai, ya profit-booking usse pehle rok degi.

Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur paisa daalna, apna average cost kam karne ke liye — yeh risky hai, kyunki aap ek aise trade mein aur add kar rahe hain jo already aapke against ja raha hai. Pyramiding iska opposite hai: ek winning position mein add karna, jab woh aapke favor mein move kar rahi ho. Zyadatar experienced traders averaging into weakness ke bajaye pyramiding into strength ko prefer karte hain, kyunki isme market khud aapke thesis ko confirm kar raha hota hai, aap market se fight nahi kar rahe hote. "Concept" tag ke saath yeh aaj ka pick hai — aur aaj jaisa lagataar chhata green session, jahan breadth bhi bahut strong tha (2,547 advances vs 1,084 declines), ek textbook example hai ki pyramiding ka logic kab sabse zyada appeal karta hai: jab market khud confirm kar raha ho ki trend genuine hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
