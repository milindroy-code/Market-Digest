Explainer: RIL Khud Kyun Gira Jab Uski Apni Jio Ne Blockbuster Debut Kiya?

Aaj ka sabse counter-intuitive fact: Jio Platforms ne apne listing din ₹1,419 tak (+26.6%) ka intraday high chuaa, lekin iski parent company Reliance Industries khud -3.19% gir kar Nifty ka sabse bada drag ban gayi — same din, same group. Yeh kaise possible hai? Jab ek company (RIL) apne kisi business (Jio) ko alag se list karti hai, toh market pehle se hi us listing ki value ko parent stock ki price mein "price in" kar chuka hota hai — pichle kai mahinon mein RIL ka stock Jio ke IPO buzz par hi chadh-utar raha tha. Listing din khud, ek classic "sell the news" moment hota hai: jis event ka intezaar tha woh ho chuka, toh jo investors sirf is event ke liye RIL mein the, woh profit book kar lete hain — chahe Jio ka debut kitna hi strong kyun na ho. Iske upar, sum-of-the-parts logic mein, RIL ka stock ab apne baaki businesses (energy, retail, telecom ka bacha hua hissa) ke hisaab se hi value hoga, Jio ke standalone upside ke bina — jo short-term mein parent ke liye ek technical headwind ban sakta hai. Yeh ek standard pattern hai jab bhi koi company apni subsidiary ko list karti hai.

Bear Put Spread

Bull call spread ka mirror image: ek higher strike par put kharido, ek lower strike par put becho. Yeh ek bearish bet ka cost kam karta hai put outright kharidne ke muqable, is cost par ki agar stock sharply girta hai toh aapka maximum profit bhi cap ho jaata hai.
