Explainer

AT1 bonds kya hote hain, aur Yes Bank ka Supreme Court case itna bada kyun hai? AT1 (Additional Tier-1) bonds ek special type ka bank debt hain jo bank ko RBI ke capital-buffer rules fulfil karne mein madad karte hain. Inki sabse khaas — aur risky — feature yeh hai: agar bank serious financial stress mein jaaye, to regulator inhe equity mein convert ya poora write-off (value zero) kar sakta hai, kabhi-kabhi shareholders se pehle bhi. 2020 mein, RBI ke Yes Bank reconstruction ke dauraan, ₹8,415 crore ke AT1 bonds poore write-off kar diye gaye the — bondholders ko kuch nahi mila. Unhone isse court mein challenge kiya, yeh keh kar ki yeh RBI ke apne master-circular aur bonds ke information memoranda ke against tha. 2023 mein Bombay High Court ne unke favour mein ruling di; aaj Supreme Court ne usi ruling ko barkarar rakha, Yes Bank ki appeal khaarij karte hue. Matlab: Yes Bank ko ab bondholders ko compensate karna pad sakta hai — exact amount abhi clear nahi, par market ne isko turant ek bada financial-liability risk ki tarah price kiya, stock double-digit % tak gira kar.

Long Strangle

Straddle jaisa hi, lekin ek call aur ek put ek hi strike par nahi, balki alag-alag (out-of-the-money) strikes par buy kiye jaate hain. Straddle se sasta hai kyunki dono legs money se aur door hain, lekin profit ke liye underlying ko aur zyada move karna padta hai. Direction pick kiye bina, ek bade move par kam-cost mein bet lagane ka tarika. Aaj ka index-level move clear tha (upar), lekin single-stock level par volatility kaafi zyada thi — Yes Bank Supreme Court verdict ke baad ek hi din mein double-digit % gira, Nestle India apne strong results ke bawajood gira. Exactly aise hi binary/surprise events (court verdicts, quarterly results) ke around, jahan bada move expected hai par direction guess karna mushkil hai, Long Strangle jaisi strategies useful ban jaati hain.
