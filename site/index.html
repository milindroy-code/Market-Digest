Explainer: GIFT Nifty Flat Kyun Hai Jab Dow 739 Points Gir Gaya

Kal raat Dow 739 points (-1.41%) gira, aur Nasdaq -2.49% gir kar correction territory mein chala gaya — phir bhi aaj subah GIFT Nifty sirf -0.04% par bilkul flat hai. Yeh disconnect genuine hai, aur iski do wajah hain. Pehli, yeh ek narrow, chip-sector-concentrated selloff tha — Nvidia, Micron, Intel jaise names 4-8% tak gire, jabki Nasdaq/S&P ka weight hi inhi AI/semiconductor stocks mein sabse zyada hai. Nifty ka index composition bilkul alag hai — financials aur consumption sectors ka weight sabse zyada hai, aur Indian IT ka revenue chip-capex se nahi, services/outsourcing contracts se aata hai, jo is specific selloff se directly expose nahi hai. Doosri wajah hai November ka record FII inflow — pehle 16 trading days mein hi ~₹89,856 crore ka net buy — jo ek domestic liquidity cushion bana raha hai, jo kisi bhi imported risk-off mood ko absorb kar leta hai. Isiliye kal ka Wall Street crash aaj subah tak pahunch kar sirf ek "flat, cautious" GIFT Nifty signal reh gaya hai, ek matching crash nahi.

Protective Put

Apne paas already jo stock hai, uske upar ek put option buy karna — bilkul insurance ki tarah. Agar stock girta hai, put ki value badhti hai aur loss offset ho jaata hai; agar nahi girta, toh aapne sirf thoda sa premium diya hai peace-of-mind ke liye — bilkul waisa hi jaisa aap car insurance ka premium dete hain, use karne ki ummeed kiye bina. Yeh ek common tareeka hai kisi uncertain event ke through invested rehte hue bhi position ko bina fully exit kiye protect karne ka — aaj ke jaisa hi setup: Nifty aur Bank Nifty dono ka RSI 90 ke paar stretched hai, aaj khud Nifty ka weekly expiry bhi hai, aur global mood Nvidia ke apne earnings (aaj US close ke baad) se pehle hi kal ke 739-point Dow selloff ke baad risk-off ho chuka hai.
