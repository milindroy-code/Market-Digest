Explainer: Profit Beat Ke Baad Bhi Stock Kyun Gir Sakta Hai?

Aaj ka SBI ek achha example hai: profit 14.28% YoY badha, phir bhi stock gira. Market ek forward-looking machine hai — stock price aaj ke number ko nahi, balki us number ke peeche ke story ko price karta hai: asset quality, provisioning trend, margin outlook, aur management ka guidance aage ke liye. Agar headline profit beat ke saath provisioning upar gayi ho, ya net interest margin thoda compress hua ho, ya koi cautious commentary aayi ho, toh market in "quality" signals ko headline number se zyada weight de sakta hai. Ek aur factor hai expectations — agar analysts aur institutional investors already ek strong quarter price-in kar chuke the, toh sirf "beat" hona kaafi nahi, surprise factor zaroori hai. Isliye hamesha sirf headline profit number nahi, balki result ke peeche ka detail (NIM, provisions, guidance) dekhna zaroori hai — yehi samajhne mein madad karta hai ki ek "achha" result bhi stock ko kyun neeche le ja sakta hai.

Bull Call Spread

Ek lower strike par call khareedo aur ek higher strike par call becho, same expiry ke saath. Isse aapka cost aur maximum profit dono cap ho jaate hain, ek call outright khareedne ke muqable — aap unlimited upside give up karte hain ek lower breakeven ke badle mein.
