Explainer: Sector Rotation — Ek Hi Din Mein Dow Upar, Nasdaq Neeche Kaise Ho Sakta Hai

Mangalwar ka Wall Street close confusing lag sakta hai — Dow +0.85% (wapas 50,000 ke upar), lekin Nasdaq -1.48% usi din. Yeh ek classic "sector rotation" hai: jab investors ek hi overall market mood (yahan, risk-off nahi, bas profit-booking) ke andar paisa ek sector se nikal kar doosre mein daalte hain, broad indices alag-alag direction mein move kar sakte hain, kyunki har index ka composition alag hota hai. Nasdaq heavily AI/chip-stocks-weighted hai (CrowdStrike, TSMC, Oracle), jinmein Somwar ki badi rally ke baad traders profit book kar rahe the — wahi paisa industrials aur healthcare (GE Vernova, Eaton, UnitedHealth) mein gaya, jo Dow mein zyada weight rakhte hain. Yeh koi market-wide panic nahi hai, bas ek repositioning hai: "AI trade thoda overbought ho gaya tha, ab doosri jagah value dikh rahi hai." India ke liye iska matlab yeh hai ki agar yeh rotation continue karta hai, to Nifty IT (jo khud heavily global-tech-linked hai) par pressure aa sakta hai, jabki domestic-facing sectors (banking, FMCG, industrials) us pressure se kaafi hadd tak insulated rehte hain — exactly waisa hi divergence jo humne Nasdaq/Dow ke beech dekha.

Position Sizing

Har trade ko same amount of capital dena zaroori nahi hai. Professional traders position ko is hisaab se size karte hain ki unka stop-loss kitni door hai aur woh apne total capital ka kitna hissa ek idea par risk karne ko taiyaar hain — often sirf 1-2% per trade — fixed number of shares ya lots ke bajaye. Strategy khud utni important nahi jitni size — ek great setup bhi, agar position oversized ho, to poora account wipe out kar sakta hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "concept" tag ke saath — ek safe default jab market ka aaj ka direction khud hi uncertain ho (gap-up signal vs mixed global cues). Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
