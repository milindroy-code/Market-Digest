Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Dollar-cost averaging — India mein SIP ke naam se jaana jaata hai — matlab ek fixed amount regular intervals par invest karna, price kuch bhi ho, ek single lump-sum entry ka "sahi time" dhoondhne ki jagah. Yeh "kya abhi sahi time hai buy karne ka" wali emotional guesswork ko hata deta hai, aur historically long-term investors ke returns ko smooth kiya hai aise hi volatile stretches mein — bilkul aaj jaisa din, jahan market subah green open hua aur dopahar tak red ho gaya.
