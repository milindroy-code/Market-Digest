Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur paisa daalna, apni average cost kam karne ke liye — risky hai, kyunki aap ek aise trade mein aur add kar rahe hain jo already aapke against ja raha hai. Pyramiding iska ulta hai: ek winning position mein add karna jab woh aapke favor mein move kar rahi ho. Zyadatar experienced traders averaging into weakness ke bajaye pyramiding into strength ko prefer karte hain, kyunki isme market thesis ko confirm kar raha hota hai, na ki aap market se fight kar rahe hote hain. Yeh sequential rotation se aaj ka concept pick hai, ek safe "concept" tag ke saath. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.

Yeh digest sirf news summary hai, investment advice nahi. Strategy Corner ek concept explain karta hai, use karne ki recommendation nahi. Numbers multiple sources se liye gaye hain aur revise ho sakte hain — trading se pehle apne broker ya exchange par live prices confirm karein.
