Explainer: Sector Rotation Kya Hai?

Aaj ka din sector rotation ka ek textbook example hai: same broad market rally ke andar, Nifty Media 5%+ upar gaya jabki Nifty IT akela sector tha jo red mein band hua. Sector rotation tab hota hai jab institutional money ek sector se nikal kar doosre mein shift hoti hai — poore market ke direction ke bina badle. Aaj IT se paisa nikalne ki ek wajah global AI-valuation aur AI-capex ko lekar fresh chinta thi (jo Asia ke tech-heavy indices, jaise Kospi aur Taiex, mein bhi dikhi), jisne domestic IT majors (TCS, HCL Tech, Infosys) ko bhi drag kiya chahe unka apna India-specific business us cycle se seedha connected na ho. Wahi paisa phir Media, Realty aur Metal jaise sectors mein gaya, jo abhi tak is rally mein peeche the aur jahan valuations relatively cheap lag rahe the. Isliye ek single din ka index number ("Nifty +0.63%") poori kahani nahi batata — kaunsa sector upar gaya aur kaunsa neeche, yeh batata hai ki market ke andar paisa actually kahan move kar raha hai.

Calendar Spread

Ek near-term option becho aur ek same strike par longer-dated option khareedo. Near-term option time decay se jaldi girta hai (expiry ke kareeb aate hi decay tez ho jaata hai), toh agar stock roughly flat rahe, position in decay rates ke difference se profit kar sakta hai — ek strategy jo time ke upar built hai, direction ke upar nahi. "Range-bound" tag ke saath yeh aaj ka sequential-rotation pick hai — ek mixed fit hai, kyunki aaj ka closing move toh ek clear uptrend continuation tha (lagataar paanchva green session), lekin din ke andar ka intraday whipsaw (subah neeche, midday reversal, phir close tak rally) khud ek genuine two-way, range-bound chop tha is session ke andar.
