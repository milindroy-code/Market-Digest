Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
The Iron Butterfly

Iron condor ka ek tighter cousin: at-the-money call aur put dono sell karo, aur further out-of-the-money call aur put protection ke liye buy karo. Yeh sabse zyada payout deta hai jab underlying exactly middle strike par finish ho — condor se zyada potential reward deta hai ek aisi market ke liye jo bahut still rehne ki expect ki ja rahi ho, par iske liye profitable range bhi narrower hota hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, "range-bound" tag ke saath — lekin aaj jaisa sharp dip-then-rally wala din iska ek mixed fit hai: aaj ka market kuch bhi "still" nahi tha, balki ek sharp V-shaped reversal dikhaya. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
