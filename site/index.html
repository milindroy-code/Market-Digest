Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Fixed amount ko regular intervals par invest karna, price dekhe bina, ek single lump-sum entry ko time karne ki koshish karne ke bajaye. India mein isey SIP kehte hain — yeh "kya abhi sahi time hai buy karne ka" wala emotional guesswork hata deta hai. Aaj jaisa din — jahan GIFT Nifty mixed signal de raha hai aur overnight global selloff ka asar abhi clear nahi — exactly wahi stretch hai jahan timing-anxiety sabse zyada hota hai, aur SIP jaisa disciplined approach historically aise volatile periods mein long-term investors ke liye returns smooth karta aaya hai.
