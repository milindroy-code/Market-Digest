Explainer: "AI Trade" Jitters — Jab Chip-Stock Doubts Poori Duniya Mein Failte Hain

Pichle kai hafton se "AI trade" naam ka ek naya tareeka market mein circulate ho raha hai — yeh us theme ko refer karta hai jahan bade tech hyperscalers (jaise Microsoft, Meta, Amazon) data-centers aur AI-chips mein hundreds of billions of dollars invest kar rahe hain, zyadatar debt-funded. Jab bhi investors ko shaq hota hai ki yeh massive spending utna hi revenue/profit generate kar payega ya nahi, ek sell-off trigger hoti hai — pehle un companies mein jo seedhe is spending cycle se judi hain: chipmakers (SK Hynix, Samsung, TSMC-linked names), memory-makers aur data-center hardware suppliers, jo mostly Korea, Taiwan aur Japan mein listed hain. Yahi wajah hai ki aaj jaisa din aata hai jab Nikkei, Kospi aur Hang Seng weak hote hain bina kisi India-specific news ke. India ka seedha linkage kam hai — yahan koi major chip-manufacturer listed nahi hai — lekin asar fir bhi do channels se pahunchta hai: pehla, global risk-off mood FIIs ko emerging markets (India included) se paisa nikalne ke liye prompt kar sakta hai; doosra, GIFT Nifty jaisa pre-market indicator seedha global sentiment ko mirror karta hai, isliye Asia ki weakness aaj GIFT Nifty ke flat-to-negative signal mein seedha dikh rahi hai, bina kisi Indian company-specific trigger ke.

Bear Put Spread

Bull call spread ka mirror-image: ek higher strike par put option khareedo, aur ek lower strike par put option becho. Yeh outright put khareedne ke comparison mein aapki cost kam karta hai ek bearish view express karne ke liye, lekin iske against aapka maximum profit bhi cap ho jaata hai agar stock sharply gire. Lagatar do red sessions ke baad, crude ke elevated level aur GIFT Nifty ke flat-to-negative signal ke saath, yeh concept aaj ke cautious mood se reasonably fit baithta hai.
