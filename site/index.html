Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Bear Put Spread

Bull call spread ka mirror-image: ek higher-strike put buy karna, aur ek lower-strike put sell karna. Yeh ek bearish bet ka cost reduce karta hai (ek put outright buy karne ke against), lekin agar stock sharply girta hai toh aapka max-profit bhi cap ho jaata hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "downtrend" tag ke saath — lekin aaj ka actual session ek genuinely strong rally raha hai (Bank Nifty ka fresh record high bhi isi mein shaamil hai), downtrend nahi, toh yeh ek acknowledged mismatch hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
