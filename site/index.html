Explainer: "Circuit Breaker" Kya Hota Hai, Aur Korea Mein Aaj Yeh Kyun Trigger Hua?

Aaj subah South Korea ka Kospi itni tezi se gira ki uske exchange ne ek circuit breaker trigger kar diya — 20 minutes ke liye poora trading halt ho gaya. Circuit breaker ek automatic safety mechanism hai: jab koi index ek din mein ek pre-defined threshold (jaise -8% ya -10%) se zyada gir jaata hai, exchange trading ko temporarily rok deta hai, taaki panic-selling ko thoda thamne ka mauka mile aur investors ko naye information ko absorb karne ka time mil sake, bina bina-socha-samjha order flow ke. India mein bhi yeh system exist karta hai — NSE/BSE par Nifty ya Sensex ke 10%, 15% ya 20% move karne par alag-alag duration ke market-wide halts lagte hain — lekin India ka market structure typically itna synchronized-aur-leveraged nahi hota jitna chip-heavy Korea ka, jahan bade institutional aur retail-margin positions concentrated hote hain chand hi stocks mein (Samsung, SK Hynix jaise). Isi wajah se aaj jaisa ek sharp, sector-specific global shock Korea mein circuit-breaker tak pahunch gaya, jabki India mein abhi tak aisi koi halt trigger nahi hui — ek reminder ki ek hi global news event alag-alag markets ko unki apni structure ke hisaab se alag-alag severity se hit kar sakta hai.

Delta Hedging

Delta yeh measure karta hai ki ek option ka price, underlying stock ya index ke ₹1 move par kitna move karta hai. Bade options traders aur market-makers apni book ka delta offset karne ke liye underlying stock ko khud buy ya sell karte hain, taaki woh chhote price-moves ke against roughly neutral rahein — isi tarah institutional desks bade size mein options sell kar paate hain, bina sirf direction par bet kiye. "High-volatility" tag ke saath yeh aaj ka pick hai, aur aaj se iska fit bahut direct hai: VIX raat ko ~70% chadh gaya aur India VIX bhi kal hi upward move mein tha — aisi conditions mein option premiums sharply badh jaate hain, aur market-makers ka delta-hedging activity khud bhi underlying market mein extra volatility add kar sakta hai (jaise unhe apni position neutral rakhne ke liye bade stock-moves ke against buy/sell karna padta hai). Yeh samajhna retail investors ke liye bhi useful hai — aaj jaisa ek sharp, global-event-driven gap-down din, options premiums ko artificially inflate kar sakta hai, jo directional option-buying ko mehenga aur risky bana deta hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
