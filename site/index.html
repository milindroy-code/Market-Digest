Explainer: "Correction Territory" Ka Matlab Kya Hota Hai?

Aaj ka Nasdaq 100 ek achha example hai: jab koi index apne recent peak se 10% ya usse zyada gir jaata hai, toh market-speak mein use "correction" kaha jaata hai — yeh "bear market" (20%+ ki girawat) se chhota hota hai, aur typically kam time tak rehta hai. Yeh threshold koi magic number nahi hai, balki ek widely-used convention hai jo overvaluation ko unwind karne ka ek normal process signal karta hai. Important baat yeh hai ki correction na toh recession ka cause hota hai, na hi uska reliable predictor — 1980 se ab tak US markets ne 30+ corrections dekhe hain, jinme se zyadatar bina kisi badi economic slowdown ke resolve ho gaye. Aaj ke case mein, Nasdaq 100 ka correction AI/semiconductor-stock-specific hai (capex aur valuation concerns), jabki broader US economy (JOLTS data) abhi bhi resilient dikh rahi hai. Isliye ek index ka "correction mein jaana" sunke panic karne ki zaroorat nahi — yeh samajhna zyada zaroori hai ki girawat kis specific wajah se aa rahi hai.

Bull Call Spread

Ek lower strike par call khareedo aur ek higher strike par call becho, same expiry ke saath. Isse aapka cost aur maximum profit dono cap ho jaate hain, ek call outright khareedne ke muqable — aap unlimited upside give up karte hain ek lower breakeven ke badle mein. Yeh sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, "uptrend" tag ke saath — lekin aaj ka fit kal jitna clean nahi hai: chaar-session ki rally ke baad GIFT Nifty ka signal ab flat-to-negative ho gaya hai, aur US midterm elections ka final outcome bhi abhi pending hai. Agar underlying uptrend (jaisa pichle chaar sessions mein dikha) hold karta hai aur yeh sirf ek short pause hai, toh Bull Call Spread moderately bullish view ko cheaply express karne ka ek tareeka rehta hai — lekin aaj ke subdued opening signal ko dekhte hue, entry se pehle trend-confirmation ka wait karna samajhdari hogi.
