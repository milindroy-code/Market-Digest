Explainer: "Hawkish Hold" Kya Hota Hai — Aur Fed Mein Do Log Hike Kyun Maang Rahe The?

Raat ko US Fed ne apni rate 3.75-4.00% par hold ki — ek "hold" sunne mein neutral lagta hai, lekin is baar vote 10-2 se split hua. Do members, Lisa Cook aur Beth Hammack, ne rate ghatane ki jagah badhane ki maang ki. Aisa kyun? Kyunki September mein US inflation 3.4% se badhkar 3.7% ho gayi — Fed ke 2% target se kaafi upar — aur jab inflation accelerate kar rahi ho, kuch policymakers mante hain ki rate ghatana (ya yahan tak ki hold karna) risky ho sakta hai, isse inflation aur bhi badh sakti hai. Is tarah ke dissent ko "hawkish hold" kaha jaata hai — rate toh same rahi, lekin committee ka tone aage ke liye zyada sakht ho gaya. Market isko kaise padhta hai? Dot-plot aur fed funds futures dono ab December meeting mein ek hike ko pehle se zyada likely maan rahe hain. Indian investors ke liye yeh matter karta hai kyunki higher US rates ka matlab hota hai ki FIIs ke liye US assets zyada attractive ho jaate hain (higher yield), jo potentially India se outflows ko badha sakta hai aur rupee par bhi pressure daal sakta hai — ek dynamic jo aane wale hafton mein USD/INR aur FII flows dono mein dikh sakta hai.
