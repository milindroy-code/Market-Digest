Explainer

"Sell-on-rise" session kya hota hai, aur yeh ek seedhe selloff se kaise alag hai? Ek seedha selloff tab hota hai jab market pehle se hi negative sentiment ke saath open hota hai aur poore din neeche hi rehta hai. "Sell-on-rise" alag hai: market positive cues (jaisa aaj GIFT Nifty aur crude ki softness tha) ke saath green open hota hai, ek higher level tak chadhta bhi hai, lekin har rally par bade players apni holdings bech dete hain — unhe lagta hai ki upside limited hai ya underlying concerns (jaise aaj ki Middle East tension aur FII outflows) abhi reflect nahi hui hain. Result: ek intraday high banta hai, phir selling pressure gradually poore din dominate karti hai, jab tak index apne open se bhi neeche band na ho jaaye — exactly jo aaj Nifty ke saath hua, 22,651.10 ke high se 22,304.45 ke low tak. Yeh pattern aksar signal karta hai ki market mein confidence fragile hai — har bounce ko exit opportunity treat kiya ja raha hai, naya buying conviction nahi.

Max Pain Theory

Max Pain Theory kehta hai ki ek underlying (jaise Nifty) apne expiry tak us strike price ki taraf gravitate karta hai jahan option buyers, collectively, sabse zyada paisa gawaate hain — kyunki option sellers (aksar bade, better-capitalized players) usi outcome se benefit karte hain. Yeh ek widely-watched lekin imperfect signal hai, ek guaranteed prediction nahi balki ek input hai doosre signals ke saath. Aaj jaisa sell-on-rise session — jahan Nifty ek higher open se fade hoke close hua — exactly woh scenario hai jahan traders max-pain level ko ek reference point ki tarah dekhte hain, yeh samajhne ke liye ki kya price action genuine hai ya options positioning se bhi influenced ho raha hai.
