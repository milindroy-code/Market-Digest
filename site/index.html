Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Position Sizing

Har trade same amount of capital deserve nahi karta. Professional traders position ka size is baat se decide karte hain ki unka stop-loss kitna door hai aur total capital ka kitna hissa woh ek hi idea par risk karna chahte hain — aksar sirf 1-2% per trade — na ki ek fixed number of shares ya lots se. Strategy khud utni important nahi hoti jitna size: ek great setup bhi, agar oversized position ke saath liya jaaye, poora account wipe out kar sakta hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "concept" tag ke saath — ek safe default, kisi specific market-condition se tied nahi. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
