Explainer: FOMC Minutes Market Ko Kyun Hila Dete Hain, Jabki Fed Ka Decision Pehle Hi Pata Tha

Jab Fed apni meeting mein rate decide karta hai, woh turant news ban jaata hai. Lekin teen hafte baad Fed ek detailed "minutes" release karta hai — ek summary ki room mein individual policymakers ne actually kya kaha. Isme aksar wo nuance hota hai jo initial statement nahi batata: vote kitna close tha, kaun se officials zyada hikes push kar rahe the, kaunsa data woh watch kar rahe hain. Jab minutes market ke priced-in expectation se zyada hawkish nikalte hain — jaisa October ke minutes mein hua, jahan "almost all officials" ne aage hikes ko likely appropriate bataya — to yeh future moves ki probability badal deta hai (tools jaise CME ke FedWatch ke through). Emerging markets jaise India ke liye yeh doubly matter karta hai: higher US rates capital ko wapas dollar-assets ki taraf khींchte hain (FII outflow ka risk), aur dollar ko rupee ke against strong rakhte hain. Isliye bina koi actual rate-move ke bhi, sirf odds ka shift hona hi GIFT Nifty aur global risk-sentiment tak ripple karne ke liye kaafi hai.

Trailing Stop-Loss

Ek fixed stop-loss ki jagah, ek trailing stop price ke upar jaane ke saath upar move karta hai — gains lock karte hue, trade ko chalte rehne ki room deta hai. Yeh "let your winners run" wali trading wisdom ko implement karne ka ek tarika hai, bina yeh predict kiye ki trend kab khatam hoga. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "uptrend" tag ke saath — November ki overall >10% rally ke context mein fit baithta hai: jo investors us rally mein gains bana chuke hain, unke liye ab, jab kal/aaj jaisa pullback aa raha hai, apne gains ko protect karne ka yeh bilkul sahi concept hai, bina poori position exit kiye. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
