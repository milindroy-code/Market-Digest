Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Ek fixed amount ko regular intervals par invest karna, price ki parwah kiye bina, is bajaye ki ek single lump-sum entry ko time karne ki koshish ki jaaye. India mein ise SIP kaha jaata hai — yeh "abhi sahi time hai kya" wala emotional guesswork hata deta hai, aur long-term investors ke liye volatile stretches mein historically returns ko smooth kiya hai, bilkul aaj jaisi intraday swing wale din ke liye relevant concept. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
