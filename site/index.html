Trailing Stop-Loss

Fixed stop-loss ke bajaye, trailing stop price ke upar jaane ke saath khud bhi upar move karta hai — gains ko lock karte hue trade ko chalne ke liye room bhi deta hai. Aaj exactly woh din hai jahan yeh concept kaam aata hai: kal ka 22,747/22,947 breakout aaj apna pehla test hold kar raha hai, aur jo log kal entry le chuke the, unke liye ab asli sawaal yeh hai ki agar yeh steady continuation slow ho jaaye toh gains ko kaise protect karein — ek fixed exit predict karne ki zaroorat nahi, bas price ke saath upar move karne do.
