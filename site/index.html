Long Strangle

Straddle jaisa hi, lekin isme ek call aur ek put alag-alag (out-of-the-money) strikes par kharide jaate hain, same strike par nahi. Yeh straddle se sasta padta hai kyunki dono legs money se aur door hoti hain, lekin profit banane ke liye underlying ko aur zyada move karna padta hai — bina direction pick kiye ek bade move par cheaper bet, bilkul aaj jaise F&O expiry din ke liye relevant concept jab expiry-se-pehle volatility ki guunjaish rehti hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
