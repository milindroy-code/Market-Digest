Explainer: GIFT Nifty Kya Hai Aur Yeh Opening Ka Sanket Kaise Deta Hai

GIFT Nifty (pehle SGX Nifty) ek Nifty 50 futures contract hai jo GIFT City (Gandhinagar) ke NSE IX exchange par trade hota hai, aur Indian markets khulne se kaafi pehle — raat bhar — live rehta hai. Chunki yeh global trading hours ke saath overlap karta hai, isiliye yeh overnight US market moves, Asian cues aur breaking news ko absorb kar leta hai, aur NSE/BSE ke 9:15 AM khulne se pehle hi ek directional hint de deta hai. Traders isse Nifty ke previous close ke against compare karte hain: agar GIFT Nifty us close se upar trade kar raha hai, toh ek gap-up opening expected hoti hai, aur neeche hone par gap-down. Lekin yeh sirf ek indicator hai, guarantee nahi — actual opening price NSE ke apne pre-open auction session se decide hoti hai, aur agar 9:00-9:15 AM ke beech koi bada news aata hai, toh actual open GIFT Nifty ke signal se kaafi alag bhi ho sakta hai. Isi wajah se yeh digest GIFT Nifty ko ek "directional cue" ki tarah treat karta hai, ek confirmed number ki tarah nahi.

Long Straddle

Same strike aur expiry par ek call aur ek put dono buy karo. Isme aapko profit tab hota hai jab underlying kisi bhi direction mein bada move kare — aap direction par nahi, volatility par hi bet laga rahe ho. Cost dono premiums ka combined total hota hai, jo aapka maximum possible loss bhi hai agar stock flat reh jaaye. Traders is strategy ko binary events se pehle use karte hain — jaise ek Fed decision ya earnings call — bilkul wahi setup jo aaj ke edition mein hai: kal Fed ka rate decision aana hai, aur aaj hi October ka monthly F&O expiry bhi hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
