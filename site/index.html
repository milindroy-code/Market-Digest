Explainer: Ek Stock Poore Index Ko Itna Kaise Move Kar Sakta Hai?

Nifty 50 ek free-float-adjusted market-cap-weighted index hai — matlab har stock ka weight uske total market value (sirf publicly-tradable shares ke hisaab se) ke proportion mein hota hai, har stock ko barabar weight dene ke bajaye. Reliance Industries, apne ₹17 lakh crore market cap ke saath, Nifty ke sabse heavy-weighted constituents mein se ek hai. Long Strangle

Long Strangle ek Long Straddle jaisa hi hai, lekin aap same strike ke bajaye call aur put alag (out-of-the-money) strikes par khareedte hain. Yeh Straddle se cheaper hota hai kyunki dono legs money se aur door hote hain, lekin profit ke liye underlying ko aur zyada move karna padta hai. Ek lower-cost tarika hai bade move par bet lagane ka, bina direction pick kiye. Yeh sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, ek "high-volatility" tag ke saath — kal ke binary US midterm elections event ke liye ek fitting setup, agar koi is tarah ka hedge consider kar raha ho. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
