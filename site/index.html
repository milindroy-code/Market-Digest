Bear Put Spread

Bear Put Spread mein tum ek higher-strike put khareedte ho aur simultaneously ek lower-strike put bechte ho, same expiry ke saath — yeh ek Bull Call Spread ka mirror-image hai, jo ek outright put khareedne ke comparison mein cost kam karta hai, lekin agar stock sharply gire toh maximum profit bhi cap ho jaata hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "downtrend" tag ke saath — aaj khud koi trading session nahi hai, lekin yeh concept Somwar ke down-close ke reference frame se fit baithta hai.
