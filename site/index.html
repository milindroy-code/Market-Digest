Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur buy karna taaki average cost kam ho jaaye — risky, kyunki aap ek trade mein aur add kar rahe hain jo already aapke against ja raha hai. Pyramiding iska opposite hai: ek winning position mein add karna jab woh aapke favor mein move kar rahi ho. Zyada experienced traders pyramiding ko prefer karte hain, kyunki isme market khud thesis ko confirm karta hai, na ki aap market ke against fight kar rahe hote hain. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, concept tag ke saath — aur aaj jaisa ek sharp-reversal session isko ek genuinely relevant frame deta hai: jo traders subah ke ~81,699 ke low par panic mein "average down" karne chale gaye hote, unhe dopahar ka poora recovery miss ho jaata; pyramiding is baat par depend karta hai ki move pehle confirm ho, hope par nahi. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
