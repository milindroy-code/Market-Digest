Bull Call Spread

Ek lower strike par call kharido aur ek higher strike par call becho, same expiry ke saath. Isse aapka cost aur maximum profit dono cap ho jaate hain, call outright kharidne ke muqable — ek moderately bullish view ko sasta mein express karne ka tarika, unlimited upside ke badle lower breakeven ke saath. Yeh concept sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, lekin iska "uptrend" tag aaj ke red, weak session se match nahi karta — flag kar rahe hain. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
