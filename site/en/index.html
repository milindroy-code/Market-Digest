Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
The Iron Butterfly

A tighter cousin of the iron condor: sell an at-the-money call and put, and buy further out-of-the-money call and put as protection. It pays out most if the underlying finishes exactly at the middle strike, offering a higher potential reward than a condor for a market expected to stay very still — at the cost of a narrower profitable range. Fitting for a day like today, where both the Sensex and Nifty are flat in a tight range, with neither a gap-up nor a crash showing up. This is an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
