Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Trailing Stop-Loss

Instead of a fixed stop-loss, a trailing stop moves up as the price rises, locking in gains while still giving the trade room to run. It's a way to let winners run — a piece of trading wisdom you'll see quoted often — without needing to predict exactly when a trend will end. A day like today — where this morning's gap-up signal hasn't been confirmed yet and direction may take time to clarify — is exactly the kind of setup where discussing a disciplined exit rule is useful, whichever direction the trend eventually confirms. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
