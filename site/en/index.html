Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Long Straddle

A long straddle means buying a call and a put at the same strike and expiry. You profit if the underlying makes a big move in either direction — you're betting on volatility itself, not a direction. The cost is the combined premium of both options, which is also your maximum loss if the stock sits still. Traders reach for this ahead of binary events like a Fed decision or an earnings call — exactly the kind of setup tomorrow's (3 November) US midterm elections create. This is today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged high-volatility. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
