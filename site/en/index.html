Markets reopened on Wednesday, 25 November, after the Guru Nanak Jayanti holiday, and this morning's Morning Briefing's gap-up signal didn't just hold — it extended through the morning. Per Univest, three factors are driving this rally: (1) a "catch-up" effect — India was shut Tuesday, so today is pricing in an overnight Nasdaq rebound (GIFT Nifty was signalling +237 pts at 8:34 AM); (2) continued FII buying; (3) a firmer rupee and softer crude. One notable oddity: APL Apollo hit a 52-week low today despite the broader rally — the Tata Steel deal/open-offer overhang is still weighing on the stock.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Risk-Reward Ratio

Before entering a trade, compare what you stand to lose (distance to your stop-loss) against what you stand to gain (distance to your target). A trade with a 1:3 risk-reward ratio can still be profitable overall even if you're wrong more often than you're right — this is why professional traders talk about expectancy, not just win rate. Today's pick comes from the sequential rotation, tagged "concept" — especially relevant on a day like today, with a sharp rally underway, when defining your risk-reward before chasing the move out of FOMO is the most important discipline. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
