Today (Monday, 9 November), Samvat 2083's first full regular-hours session opened with strength — the Nifty opened at 25,076.40 and touched an intraday high of 25,084.05 (TradeBrains), a clean move above Sunday's confirmed Muhurat-session close of 24,986.40. That strength didn't last — selling pressure emerged through the session and the Nifty slipped to an intraday low of 24,838.75. Business Standard's live blog (headline: "Sensex falls ~300 pts, Nifty below 24,900; Nifty Pharma hits new high") and TradeBrains' 12:53 PM IST update cross-confirm that, right now (~12:50-12:55 PM IST), the Nifty is at 24,897.40 (-89.00 points/-0.36%) and the Sensex at 80,691.06 (roughly -308 points/-0.38%) — negative against Sunday's Muhurat close too, meaning Samvat 2083's first full session is still in the red so far. No circuit filter or surprise policy move has occurred — this is simply a standard give-back-the-early-gains, sector-rotation day. The most notable divergence: Nifty Pharma has hit a fresh high today (+1.26%), on a clutch of Q2 earnings beats — Divi's Laboratories (profit +63.6% YoY to ₹1,127 crore, stock +4.47%, cross-confirming this morning's edition's "~64% YoY" figure from an independent source), Gland Pharma (profit +58% to ₹290.80 crore, stock +6.43%) and GSK Pharma (+6.33%) led. On the other side, Nifty IT is today's weakest sector (-1.93%), the single biggest drag on the overall index weakness. The rest of the sectors are mixed — Media (+0.79%), Realty (+0.53%), Financial Services (+0.22%) green, while Metal (-0.71%), Infrastructure (-0.59%), FMCG (-0.56%), Auto (-0.23%) and Energy (-0.16%) are red. At the stock level, some genuinely odd divergences showed up: Data Patterns is today's top intraday gainer (+7.62%) despite its Q2 profit falling 55.3% YoY (revenue -56.7%) — a contradiction worth flagging outright; IREDA (+6.52%) and Sai Life Sciences (+6.08%) are also gainers. A fresh "beat-but-falls" case: Finolex Cables' profit rose 67.8% YoY, yet the stock fell 5.34%; TD Power Systems (-6.73%), RR Kabel (-5.69%) and MTAR Technologies/Sterlite Technologies (both -5%) were the top losers. On corporate news, Tata Steel's Q2 profit rose 16.2% YoY to ₹3,700 crore (EBITDA +17.1%) — broadly consistent with this morning's edition's "operating profit +18%" figure, a slightly different metric; L&T won a ₹1,000-2,500 crore Noida Metro Aqua Line Extension order; Hindalco said Novelis's EBITDA is tracking toward $500/tonne; and BlueStone (+3.63%) is up on festive demand. Asia has improved sharply from this morning's mixed picture — Taiwan (+5.46%), Nikkei (+3.15%) are sharply higher, and Kospi has also turned positive (+1.04%), a clean reversal from its -1.33% open this morning; Hang Seng (+0.20%) is mildly green. Crude is at Brent ~$98 and WTI ~$89.70 (Trading Economics benchmark), with USD/INR ~93.85. India's 10-year bond yield is at 7.178%, easing from its ~7.5% October peak. Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Long Strangle

Like a straddle, but you buy a call and a put at different (out-of-the-money) strikes instead of the same one. It's cheaper to put on than a straddle because both legs are further from the money, but the underlying needs to move even further before you profit. A lower-cost way to bet on a big move without picking a direction. This is today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged "high-volatility" — and today's session continues that theme: a sharp give-back from a fresh high, plus odd earnings-vs-stock-reaction divergences like Data Patterns and Finolex Cables, show that direction remains genuinely two-way. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
