Today's Concept
Position Sizing

Not every trade deserves the same amount of capital. Professional traders size a position based on how far their stop-loss is and how much of their total capital they're willing to risk on one idea — often just 1-2% per trade — rather than a fixed number of shares or lots. The strategy matters less than the size; a great setup with an oversized position can still wipe out an account. Today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged "concept" — a safe default not tied to any specific market condition. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
