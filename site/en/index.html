Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Calendar Spread

Sell a near-term option and buy a longer-dated option at the same strike. The near-term option decays faster — time decay accelerates as expiry approaches — so if the stock stays roughly flat, the position can profit from that difference in decay rates: a strategy built around time itself, not direction. Today's pick in sequential rotation, tagged "range-bound" — and there's no more range-bound a day than one with zero trading at all, Nifty and Sensex both literally unmoved. This is an educational concept only, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
