Thursday, 12 November's session has been a genuinely choppy, range-bound day so far — no breaking news, just normal intraday give-and-take. The market opened below yesterday's (11 Nov) confirmed close — Nifty 25,255.05, Sensex 81,879.40 — with a mild gap-down (Kotak Neo had the Sensex in early trade down 237.11 points/-0.29% at 81,642.29, the Nifty below 25,200), but recovered quickly: by ~10:09 AM IST the Sensex was up 166.44 points at 82,013.58 and the Nifty up 55.65 points at 25,316.55 (ETV Bharat). By midday some of that was given back — Business Standard's live blog's ~12:00 PM IST reading had the Sensex up 89.74 points (+0.11%) at 81,969.14, the Nifty up 14.55 points (+0.06%) at 25,269.60, and a slightly later update (~12:21 PM, Tradebrains) had the Nifty essentially flat, -0.01% at 25,253 — right back near yesterday's close. Business Standard's own latest headline reads "Sensex pares ~400 pts from day's high, Nifty below 25,300" — the exact day's high wasn't confirmed this run, but the shape is clear: an early-morning rally, then a steady midday give-back. No circuit filter triggered, and no surprise policy move shook the market — there is one genuine regulatory item today (the RBI's new Domestic Money Transfer Directions, 2026, capping cash payouts at ₹10,000 per transaction and ₹25,000 per beneficiary per month, with mandatory KYC/authentication, effective immediately), but it reads as routine compliance tightening rather than an index-moving event. By sector, Realty was the strongest performer (+3.23%), Bank Nifty also +0.64% in the green, while Auto, IT and Energy dragged the index lower — at the stock level, Bajaj Finance, Bajaj Finserv, ITC (+1.5%, after a Gold Flake price hike), Eternal, SBI and ICICI Bank were gainers, while M&M, Sun Pharma, UltraTech Cement, HUL and TCS were the laggards. Today's 12 pending Q2 FY27 results — Hindustan Aeronautics, Oil India, NMDC, Vodafone Idea, Ola Electric, Alkem, Amber Enterprises, Manappuram Finance, Bharat Dynamics, Page Industries, RVNL and IRCTC — remain unconfirmed as of midday.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Trailing Stop-Loss

Instead of a fixed stop-loss, a trailing stop moves up as the price rises, locking in gains while still giving the trade room to run. Today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged "uptrend" — and a genuinely relevant fit for today's session: the market touched a sharp morning high, then gave back some of those gains by midday, exactly the scenario where a trailing stop beats a fixed target — neither exiting too early nor handing back all the gains, unless the trend genuinely reverses. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
