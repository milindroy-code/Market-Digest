Monday, 16 November's session opened by making good on the genuine gap-up GIFT Nifty signal flagged this morning (~25,200-25,260, against Friday 13 November's confirmed close), and the gains widened through the morning. Friday's confirmed close was Nifty 25,144.30, Sensex 81,592.45. In early trade (~9:25-9:26 AM IST) Upstox and HDFC Sky both cross-confirm Sensex ~81,984-82,011 (+392 to +418 points/+0.48% to +0.51%) and Nifty 25,227.40-25,227.60 (+83 points/+0.33%), which briefly crossed 25,250. Gains widened further after that — Business Standard's ~10:00 AM IST read has the Sensex at 82,292.86 (+700.41 points/+0.86%), and Zee Business's early-trade read confirms a similar range (~82,195). No later cross-confirmed Nifty read was found this run — flagged rather than guessed at. The drivers are mixed: on one side there's capital rotation — FIIs moving out of weak Asian markets (Taiwan -3.33%, Kospi -2.29%) and into Indian stocks, with FII inflows for November so far crossing $8 billion (the highest monthly total since December 2020, per HDFC Sky), and crude easing a bit further too (Brent ~$95.12/-0.75%, WTI ~$88.11/-0.51%), which helps the import bill, inflation and the rupee. On the other, strong Q2 earnings — Eicher Motors (consolidated PAT +14.9% YoY to ₹1,573.37 crore) and Godrej Industries (consolidated profit +130.8%). IT (+1.04%) and PSU Bank (+1.15%) were the leading sectors. But there are two genuinely sharp moves too, that don't fit a simple "no circuit filter, just a routine gap-up day" story: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles was a major single-stock drag — after a Q2 consolidated net loss of ₹5,531 crore (on exceptional items), the stock fell as much as 8.73% in early trade (per a 10:39 AM update), with headline-level reads rounding this to ~9%. And Eicher Motors itself showed a sharp intraday reversal — the stock rose as much as 4% (to an intraday high of ₹7,947) after its results, then pared those gains to trade down 0.28% at ₹7,625.50 by 11:15 AM, as analysts (Citi, CLSA, Nomura, Jefferies) flagged margin pressure from higher other expenses and staff costs. The biggest "breaking" item of the day is a macro surprise — MCX December gold futures fell as much as 1.7% to an intraday low of ₹1,34,385 (down 1.39% at ₹1,34,802 by ~11:43 AM), and silver fell to a record intraday low of ₹1,69,194, down 4.04% (down 3.75% at ₹1,69,700 at writing) — on a stronger US dollar, elevated US Treasury yields, and an October US CPI print of +0.4% month-on-month, which pushed the odds of a December Fed rate hike (not just a hold) to 92% per CME FedWatch. That's a genuine step up from this morning's "hot PPI, rate uncertainty" framing — markets are now pricing an actual 25-basis-point hike as the base case.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Iron Condor

Sell an out-of-the-money call spread and an out-of-the-money put spread at the same time. You collect premium upfront and keep it if the underlying stays within a range through expiry — the trade that profits from a market going nowhere. Risk is capped on both sides, which is why it's a popular income strategy in sideways markets. Today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged "range-bound" — but today's session has genuinely been a trending gap-up-and-extend session, not range-bound, so this is an acknowledged mismatch (as has happened on a few prior editions too). This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
