Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Bear Put Spread

The mirror image of a bull call spread: buy a put at a higher strike, sell a put at a lower strike. It reduces the cost of a bearish bet versus buying a put outright, at the cost of capping how much you can make if the stock falls sharply. Today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged "downtrend" — but today's actual session has been a genuinely strong rally (the Bank Nifty's fresh record high included), not a downtrend, so this is an acknowledged mismatch. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
