Explainer: How Can Realty And FMCG — Two Very Different Sectors — Both Top The Same Day?

Today Nifty Realty (+2.75%) and Nifty FMCG (+2.34%) were both top performers — two sectors with very different business models. Their reasons were different too, not one single macro trigger. Realty is typically a "rate-sensitive" sector: developers are heavily debt-financed, and homebuyers depend on loans too. When the US Fed holds rates (rather than hiking), it raises the odds that global bond yields stay range-bound, which helps capital-intensive, debt-heavy businesses. FMCG's story is different: it's a "defensive" sector whose margins are tied to crude-linked input costs (packaging, transport, raw materials) — Thursday's softer Brent crude ($114, -1.4%) offered direct cost relief, and after Wednesday's IT-led selloff, investors also rotated into consumption names with clearer earnings visibility. The lesson: when an index-level rally shows up, each sector can have its own distinct "why" — looking for one single headline cause isn't always accurate.

Risk-Reward Ratio

Before entering a trade, compare what you stand to lose (distance to your stop-loss) against what you stand to gain (distance to your target). A trade with a 1:3 risk-reward ratio can still be profitable overall even if you're wrong more often than you're right — this is why professional traders talk about expectancy, not just win rate.
