Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Trailing Stop-Loss

Instead of a fixed stop-loss, a trailing stop moves up as the price rises, locking in gains while still giving the trade room to run. Today is exactly the kind of day this concept is built for: yesterday's 22,747/22,947 breakout is holding through its first test, and for anyone who entered yesterday, the real question now is how to protect those gains if this steady continuation slows down — without needing to predict a fixed exit, just letting the stop move up with the price. This is an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
