Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Covered Call

If you already hold a stock, sell a call option against it. You collect the premium as income, and if the stock stays below the strike, you keep both the stock and the premium. The tradeoff: if the stock rallies hard past the strike, you miss out on gains beyond it, since you're obligated to sell at that price. Today's pick comes from the sequential rotation, tagged "any" — a two-way, sharply-reversing session like today's, where single-stock moves (APL Apollo Tubes/Tata Steel) shook the whole index, is a relevant concept for investors already holding positions who want to generate some extra income. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
