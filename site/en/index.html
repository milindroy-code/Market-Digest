Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Protective Put

If you already hold a stock, you can buy a put option on it, as insurance. If the stock falls, the put gains value and offsets the loss; if it doesn't, you've paid a small premium for peace of mind — the same logic as paying for car insurance you hope never to use. A day like today — when a strong gap-up morning turns into a clean reversal by midday — is exactly the kind of scenario where investors discuss a strategy like the protective put, to hedge existing holdings against uncertainty without fully exiting a position. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
