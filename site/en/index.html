Markets reopened Wednesday, 11 November, after the Balipratipada holiday, and this morning's GIFT Nifty signal (a ~+1.1-1.2% implied gap-up against Monday's 25,044.65 close) fully materialised. By Equitymaster's 10:30 AM IST update, the Sensex was trading ~1,179 points higher and the Nifty above 25,350 — Bajaj Finance, Asian Paints and ICICI Bank were the top gainers. That momentum extended through the late morning: Business Standard's live blog, in its ~12:00 PM IST update, had the Sensex up 1,246.43 points (+1.54%) at 82,353.08, and the Nifty up 332.95 points (~25,377.60) — a move even bigger than this morning's GIFT Nifty signal implied. That high didn't hold for long: the same live blog's latest headline reads "Sensex off 500 pts from day's high, Nifty below 25,300; IT, pharma shares weigh," meaning the Sensex is now about 500 points off its day's high and the Nifty has slipped below 25,300, with IT and pharma stocks dragging on the pullback. No circuit filter or surprise policy move — this is a normal profit-booking pullback after a sharp move, and both indices remain solidly higher than Monday's close. Broader-market strength is holding up: the Nifty Midcap (+0.68%) and Nifty Smallcap (+1.32%) are both in the green, suggesting this pullback is benchmark-level profit-booking rather than a broader reversal. This morning's pending Q2 FY27 results — Tata Power, Hindustan Copper, Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers, Mishra Dhatu Nigam, Power Mech Projects and Triveni Turbine — remain unconfirmed this run; all are scheduled to report today, and actual figures are still awaited.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Calendar Spread

Sell a near-term option and buy a longer-dated option at the same strike — it profits when the underlying stays roughly flat, since the near-term option decays faster. Today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged "range-bound" — and honestly, today's session isn't a perfect match: the Nifty showed a sharp gap-up followed by a sharp give-back. But if these swings settle down into the close and the Nifty bands into a narrower range (as often happens in the aftermath of a big move), the concept becomes relevant. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
