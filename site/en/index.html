Friday, 13 November's session — continuing from the flat-to-mildly-negative GIFT Nifty signal flagged in this morning's Morning Briefing — has turned into a broader, deepening selloff by midday. The market opened below yesterday's (12 Nov) confirmed close — Nifty 25,299.35, Sensex 81,911.01 — the Nifty down 89.50 points/-0.35% at 25,209.85 at the 9:15 AM IST open, the Sensex down 77.23 points/-0.09% at 81,833.78 (Upstox). The selloff deepened quickly after the open — the Nifty touched an early low of ~25,077.55 (-222 points/-0.87%) and the Sensex fell to ~81,300 (down more than 600 points), a magnitude cross-confirmed by both Upstox and Zee Business. By midday the decline had stabilised somewhat but remained deep — HDFC Sky's ~12:29-12:37 PM IST live-blog reading has the Nifty at 25,113.35 (-186.00 points/-0.74%) and the Sensex at 81,441.32 (-469.69 points/-0.57%), with the Bank Nifty also down 0.97% (exact level not confirmed this run). No circuit filter triggered, and no fresh surprise policy move landed — this entire move is a continuation of this morning's story: the US 10-year Treasury yield remains elevated at ~5.12%, crude was at Brent ~$95.45-95.70 this morning and WTI around $88.95 (+0.23%) by midday, following Trump's remark that a deal with Iran is "no longer" of interest, and October CPI at 6.09% — above the RBI's 6% upper tolerance band for the first time in two years — all three factors were already flagged in this morning's edition and are simply weighing on the market all day. There's added context here too: both the Nifty and Bank Nifty were technically overbought yesterday (RSI 79.82/80.50) — today's pullback looks, to a significant degree, like an unwind of that overbought condition. Breadth was weak this morning — only 12 of 50 Nifty stocks were green, 38 red (Upstox, ~9:52 AM IST reading, not reconfirmed by midday). By sector, Financial Services (-1.15%), Auto (-1.01%) and IT (-0.72%) were the biggest laggards, while Energy (+0.34%) was the day's only green sector. One interesting divergence: India VIX actually slipped to 16.33 (-1.74% from yesterday's 16.62) this morning despite the selloff — suggesting this reads less like a panic-driven crash and more like a steady, macro-driven unwind.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
The Iron Butterfly

A tighter cousin of the iron condor: sell an at-the-money call and put, and buy further out-of-the-money call and put as protection. It pays out most if the underlying finishes exactly at the middle strike, offering a higher potential reward than a condor for a market expected to stay very still — at the cost of a narrower profitable range. Today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged "range-bound" — but today's session has genuinely been trending/declining, not range-bound, so this is an acknowledged mismatch (as has happened on a few prior editions too). This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
