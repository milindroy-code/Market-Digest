Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Bull Call Spread

Buy a call at a lower strike and sell a call at a higher strike, same expiry. This caps both your cost and your maximum profit compared to buying a call outright, making it a way to express a moderately bullish view more cheaply — you give up unlimited upside in exchange for a lower breakeven. This is today's sequential pick from the rotation, but its "uptrend" tag doesn't fit today's red, weak session — flagging the mismatch. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
