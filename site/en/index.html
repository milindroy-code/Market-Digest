Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Delta Hedging

Delta measures how much an option's price moves for a ₹1 move in the underlying. Large options traders and market makers buy or sell the underlying stock to offset their book's delta, staying roughly neutral to small price moves — it's how institutional desks can sell options in size without simply betting on direction. Today's pick comes from the sequential rotation, tagged "high-volatility" — a loose fit for today's actual conditions (VIX stable, no panic, modest gains), flagged rather than force-fit. It's still relevant though: even on a calm-looking day, institutional desks keep delta-hedging in the background, whether or not headline-level volatility shows up. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
