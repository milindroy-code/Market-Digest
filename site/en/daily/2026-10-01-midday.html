Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Covered Call

If you already hold a stock, sell a call option against it. You collect the premium as income, and if the stock stays below the strike, you keep both the stock and the premium. The tradeoff: if the stock rallies hard past the strike, you miss out on gains beyond it, since you're obligated to sell at that price. A sector-rotation session like today's — where names like Kotak Mahindra Bank are firm but the broader index is flat-to-down — is exactly the kind of market where traders explore covered calls on existing holdings for extra income, a concept to understand, not a recommendation to place the trade.
