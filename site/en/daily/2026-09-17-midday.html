Today's Biggest Story: NSE's Own IPO

India's own stock exchange — NSE — came to market today. A ₹22,569-crore issue, price band ₹1,700–1,785, lot size 8 shares — the second-largest IPO ever after Hyundai Motor India's ₹27,870-crore issue in 2024. Subscription stays open until 21 September. Overall subscription was only ~15% in the first hour (retail 19%, NII 23%) — typical, since QIB demand usually shows up closer to the closing days — and GMP trackers vary between roughly 8% and 11–12% on implied listing gains, so treat that as a range rather than a precise figure.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Iron Condor

Sell an out-of-the-money call spread and an out-of-the-money put spread at the same time. You collect premium upfront and keep it if the underlying stays within a range through expiry — the trade that profits from a market going nowhere. Risk is capped on both sides, which is why it's a popular income strategy for a sideways, fade-then-hold session like today's.
