Today's Concept
Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down means buying more of a losing position to lower your average cost — risky, since you're adding to a trade that's already going against you. Pyramiding is the opposite: adding to a winning position as it moves in your favor. Most experienced traders favor pyramiding into strength over averaging into weakness, since it means the market is confirming the thesis rather than you fighting it. This is today's sequential pick from the rotation, carrying a safe "concept" tag. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
