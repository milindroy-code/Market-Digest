Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Bear Put Spread

A Bear Put Spread is the mirror image of a Bull Call Spread: buy a put at a higher strike and sell a put at a lower strike, same expiry. This reduces the cost of a bearish bet versus buying a put outright, at the cost of capping how much you can make if the stock falls sharply. Today's pick in sequential rotation, tagged "downtrend" — there's no trading session today, but the concept fits the frame of Monday's down-close reference point. This is an educational concept only, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
