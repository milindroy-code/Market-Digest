The morning's weak open, extending Monday's broad-based rout, is now partly reversing as the session goes on — on the back of a surprise pharma-sector tailwind. Nifty slipped below 22,700 after the open to touch its intraday low, but has clawed back to 22,716.40 (↓63.85, 0.28%) as of ~12:00 PM IST, while the Sensex is at 72,555.37 (↓216.35, 0.30%) — roughly 500 points off its day's low. The trigger is a genuine policy surprise: the US Commerce Department has included India among 20 countries and jurisdictions eligible for zero-tariff treatment on qualifying speciality pharmaceutical products — exempting them from the previously threatened 100% tariff. That's lifted Nifty Pharma about 1% (into the 26,969-27,032 zone) and Nifty Healthcare +0.61% (16,638.05), with Dr Reddy's Laboratories (+1.66%) leading Nifty50's gainers and Mankind Pharma, Gland Pharma, Lupin, Sun Pharma and Aurobindo Pharma all trading higher. The rest of the market is still soft — Nifty FMCG, Nifty Auto and Nifty Chemical are the worst-hit sectors, and Bajaj Finance, Infosys and HCLTech are the top losers on the Sensex. Bank Nifty and the broader financials space also remain weak, with PSU banks still the laggard after Monday's ~3% slide — though a precise, independently-confirmed intraday Bank Nifty print wasn't available across sources at file time, so no exact figure is given here. India VIX has climbed a further 6.6% to around 14.5 this morning, extending Monday's spike. Crude remains elevated (Brent ~$107), and the rupee has weakened further to ₹96.13/dollar (down 16 paise), firmly through the 96 mark. No index-level circuit filter or trading halt has kicked in — this is an orderly, sector-divergent session.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
The Iron Butterfly

A tighter cousin of the iron condor: sell an at-the-money call and put, and buy further out-of-the-money call and put as protection. It pays out most if the underlying finishes exactly at the middle strike, offering a higher potential reward than a condor for a market expected to stay very still — at the cost of a narrower profitable range. A day like today — where the index opened sharply lower before pulling back into a tight range (~22,700-22,900) on pharma's support — is exactly the kind of setup where this concept becomes relevant, though Bank Nifty's expiry-driven flows could disrupt that range at any point.
