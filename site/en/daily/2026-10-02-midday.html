Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Bear Put Spread

The mirror image of a bull call spread: buy a put at a higher strike, sell a put at a lower strike, same expiry. It reduces the cost of a bearish bet compared to buying a put outright, at the cost of capping how much you can make if the stock falls sharply. A clean four-session downtrend like this week's is exactly the kind of setup where traders look at defined-risk, bearish strategies like this one — in place of unlimited-downside put-buying, for a lower cost.
