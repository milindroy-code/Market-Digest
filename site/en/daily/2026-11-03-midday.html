Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Covered Call

If you already hold a stock, sell a call option against it. You collect the premium as income, and if the stock stays below the strike, you keep both the stock and the premium. The tradeoff: if the stock rallies hard past the strike, you miss out on gains beyond it, since you're obligated to sell at that price. For a thin-data, wait-and-watch session like today, this is an "any" condition fit — today's pick from the sequential rotation. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
