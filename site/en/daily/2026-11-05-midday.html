Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Trailing Stop-Loss

Instead of a fixed stop-loss, a trailing stop moves up as the price rises, locking in gains while still giving the trade room to run. It's a practical way to "let winners run" without needing to predict exactly when a trend will end. This is today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged "uptrend" — and it's a strong fit for a day like today's sharp, broad-based rally: as the Nifty makes fresh highs, moving the stop up with it is a disciplined way to protect gains without exiting too early. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
