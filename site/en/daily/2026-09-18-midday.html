Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Bull Call Spread

Buy a call at a lower strike and sell a call at a higher strike, same expiry. This caps both your cost and your maximum profit compared to buying a call outright, making it a way to express a moderately bullish view more cheaply — you give up unlimited upside in exchange for a lower breakeven. A broad-based move like today's — where the gap-up is holding and banks have joined in too — is a classic use case for a strategy like this.
