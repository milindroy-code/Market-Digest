Today's (Thursday, 29 October) session has turned into a clean rally after a morning of mixed, inconsistent GIFT Nifty signals. The open was muted-to-mildly-positive — around 9:19 AM IST the Sensex was up 92 points (+0.13%) at 73,337 and the Nifty up 25 points (+0.11%) at 22,723 — but buying built through the morning, and by a midday snapshot (~12:10-12:25 PM IST, cross-confirmed by Business Standard's live blog and dsij.in) the Sensex was up 608 points (+0.83%) at 73,854 and the Nifty up 163 points (+0.72%) at 22,862, against Wednesday's (28 Oct) confirmed close of 73,246.03/22,698.85. Sector leadership is clear: Nifty FMCG (+1.6%) and Nifty PSU Bank (+1.3%) were the top performers — United Spirits, ITC and Dabur all up more than 2% — while Nifty Auto (-0.40%) was the only sector in the red; both Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 were up more than 0.4%. Overnight, Alphabet's weaker-than-expected Q3 results had reignited global AI-spending worries, and one pre-market reading had dragged Nifty IT down nearly 2% — but the sector recovered through the session to trade +0.9% in early trade (9:27 AM); the Fed's hawkish hold two nights ago hasn't visibly hurt Indian IT stocks so far. Among Sensex constituents, Adani Ports, TCS, Bharti Airtel, Power Grid and HCL Tech led the gainers, while Trent, Maruti Suzuki, HDFC Bank and Bajaj Finserv were the laggards. The day's other big trigger is Jio Platforms — on its second trading day, the stock added another ~5% intraday, touching ₹1,489.70 on the NSE (above yesterday's ₹1,417.50 close), now trading ~33% above its ₹1,119 IPO price; its market cap has climbed from ~₹12.5 lakh crore at listing to ~₹13.5 lakh crore today. No circuit filter, surprise RBI/SEBI move, or sharp reversal has been reported this run. Hindustan Unilever's Q2 FY27 results are still due, not out as of this run — the wait carries over from yesterday. Today's fresh FII/DII cash-segment figures will only be confirmed by evening. On the global backdrop, Brent crude is at $114.40 (-0.86%), gold at $4,061.58 (-0.14%), and USD/INR steady around ₹95.21 — nothing dramatic there. Today's Concept
Position Sizing

Not every trade deserves the same amount of capital. Professional traders size a position based on how far their stop-loss is and how much of their total capital they're willing to risk on one idea — often just 1-2% per trade — rather than a fixed number of shares or lots. The strategy matters less than the size; a great setup with an oversized position can still wipe out an account. This is today's sequential pick from the rotation, carrying a safe "concept" tag. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
