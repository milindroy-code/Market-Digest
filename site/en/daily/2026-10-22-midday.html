Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Calendar Spread

Sell a near-term option and buy a longer-dated option at the same strike. The near-term option decays faster (time decay accelerates as expiry approaches), so if the stock stays roughly flat, the position can profit from that difference in decay rates — a strategy built around time itself, not direction. A session like today — where this morning's gap-down has settled into a narrow range by midday, without deepening further — is exactly the kind of setup that makes a time-based, range-bound strategy like this worth discussing. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
