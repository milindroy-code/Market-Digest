Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Long Strangle

Like a straddle, but you buy a call and a put at different (out-of-the-money) strikes instead of the same one. It's cheaper to put on than a straddle because both legs are further from the money, but the underlying needs to move even further before you profit. A lower-cost way to bet on a big move without picking a direction — a fitting concept for a monthly F&O expiry day like today, when pre-expiry volatility can flare up. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
