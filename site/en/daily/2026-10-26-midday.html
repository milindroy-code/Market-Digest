Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, regardless of price, rather than trying to time a single lump-sum entry. Known in India as a SIP, it removes the emotional guesswork of "is this the right time to buy" and has historically smoothed returns for long-term investors through volatile stretches — a fitting concept for a day with exactly this kind of intraday swing. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
