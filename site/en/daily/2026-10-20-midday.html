Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Long Strangle

Like a straddle, but you buy a call and a put at different (out-of-the-money) strikes instead of the same one. It's cheaper to put on than a straddle because both legs are further from the money, but the underlying needs to move even further before you profit. A setup like this week's — Jio's mega-IPO subscription opening Wednesday, Infosys' results on Friday, and the question of follow-through in bank stocks — is exactly the kind of binary-event window where traders discuss lower-cost, high-volatility strategies like this one, whichever direction things break. This is an educational concept only, not a recommendation to place the trade.
