The light ~20-40-point GIFT Nifty gap-up signal flagged this morning reversed as the session wore on. The biggest driver behind this reversal is Brent crude crossing $97/barrel (up 4.2% for the week) — reports of tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz and attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure have stoked supply-disruption fears, which directly hit India's oil-import bill. Alongside that, there's some profit-booking after the market's recent run (one report cites roughly a 15% gain over the past four weeks). The Nifty IT index is down about 1-1.5% (a range across two sources), giving back part of its roughly 8% gain over the past four sessions. The rupee has also weakened — after opening at 92.44, it slipped 24 paise to ₹92.62/$, as crude rose and equities weakened. The broader market looks relatively resilient — the Nifty MidCap 100 and SmallCap 100 are both outperforming the headline indices, and metal/realty stocks reportedly helped limit the early-session decline (a single-source detail not fully cross-confirmed this run). Separately, a single-source report says SEBI is examining the Tata Steel-APL Apollo deal, with its chairperson stressing minority-shareholder protection — another chapter in the Tata Steel-APL Apollo saga carried forward from prior editions. The other big story today: Mahanadi Coalfields' ₹8,981-crore IPO — this Coal India subsidiary, the year's biggest coal-PSU listing, lists on the NSE/BSE today (special pre-open 9:00-9:45 AM, normal trading from 10 AM); its pre-listing GMP signaled roughly ₹3/share (~2.21% premium over the ₹136 issue price), but the actual listing price/debut performance couldn't be confirmed this run.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, regardless of price, rather than trying to time a single lump-sum entry. Known in India as a SIP, it removes the emotional guesswork of "is this the right time to buy" and has historically smoothed returns for long-term investors through volatile stretches — exactly like today. Picked up by sequential rotation, tagged "concept" — a safe default that's especially relevant today: when headline indices suddenly reverse (as happened today), a disciplined SIP investor doesn't need to try to time the intraday swings. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place any specific trade.
