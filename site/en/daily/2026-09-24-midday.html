Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Covered Call

If you already hold a stock, sell a call option against it. You collect the premium as income, and if the stock stays below the strike, you keep both the stock and the premium. The tradeoff: if the stock rallies hard past the strike, you miss out on gains beyond it, since you're obligated to sell at that price. In a choppy, sharply-down session like today's — where many stocks are under pressure but there's no single clear directional crash — a covered call is one way to generate some income while holding on to an existing position.
