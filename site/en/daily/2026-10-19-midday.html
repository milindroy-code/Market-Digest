Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, regardless of price, rather than trying to time a single lump-sum entry. Known in India as a SIP, it removes the emotional guesswork of "is this the right time to buy." A day like today — where GIFT Nifty is sending a mixed signal and the fallout from an overnight global selloff isn't yet clear — is exactly the kind of stretch where timing-anxiety runs highest, and a disciplined approach like this has historically smoothed returns for long-term investors through volatile periods. This is an educational concept only, not a recommendation to place a specific trade.
