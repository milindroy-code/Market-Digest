Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down means buying more of a losing position to lower your average cost — risky, since you're adding to a trade that's already going against you. Pyramiding is the opposite: adding to a winning position as it moves in your favor. Most experienced traders favor pyramiding into strength over averaging into weakness, since it means the market is confirming the thesis rather than you fighting it. Today's pick comes from the sequential rotation, tagged "concept" — and a sharp-reversal session like today's gives it a genuinely relevant frame: anyone who panicked into "averaging down" near this morning's ~81,699 low would have missed the midday recovery entirely; pyramiding only ever depends on a confirmed move, not hope. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade.
