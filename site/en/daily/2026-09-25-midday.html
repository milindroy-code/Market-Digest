Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Bear Put Spread

The mirror image of a bull call spread: buy a put at a higher strike and sell a put at a lower strike, same expiry. This reduces the cost of a bearish bet compared to buying a put outright, at the cost of capping how much you can make if the stock falls sharply. Today's modest bounce suggests panic has eased somewhat, but crude is still at ~$107 and the Hormuz-deal talks remain unfinalized — for anyone who thinks downside risk isn't fully behind us, a bear put spread is a cost-efficient way to express that view without taking on the full premium risk of a naked put.
