Today's Big Story: A Morning Gain That Vanished By Midday

Today's pattern was a clean round trip. Nifty opened at 23,330.20 — slightly below Monday's close despite GIFT Nifty's modest gap-up signal — and dipped further to an early intraday low of 23,314.80. It then staged a strong recovery, helped by Wall Street's overnight AI/chip-led rally and falling crude oil, crossing 23,400 and touching a mid-morning intraday high of 23,466.80 (the Sensex rose as much as ~692 points, 0.93%, to a high of 74,987.40). But the gain didn't hold — selling in Nifty IT intensified, with HCLTech, Tech Mahindra and Infosys leading the declines, amid a cautious tech-spending outlook (weak macro conditions, elevated rates and inflation, and geopolitical uncertainty keeping discretionary IT spending muted) and continued FII selling. By ~12:00 PM IST both indices were back below Monday's close. In contrast, Nifty Realty was today's top-performing sector, and the broader market stayed mixed — midcaps slightly up, smallcaps slightly down.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Delta Hedging

Delta measures how much an option's price moves for a ₹1 move in the underlying. Large options traders and market makers buy or sell the underlying stock to offset their book's delta, staying roughly neutral to small price moves — it's how institutional desks can sell options in size without simply betting on direction. On a day like today — an expiry-cum-reversal session where Nifty climbed to 23,466 in the morning and slipped back to around 23,350 by midday — that discipline matters especially.
