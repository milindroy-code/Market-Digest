Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Long Straddle

A long straddle involves buying both a call and a put at the same strike and expiry. It lets a trader profit from a big move — in either direction — as long as the move is large enough to cover the combined cost of both premiums. This works best when direction is unclear but a volatility spike is expected, exactly what today's 14%+ jump in India VIX is signaling — with crude and geopolitical headlines driving the session, today is precisely the kind of uncertain, high-volatility setup where this concept is relevant.
