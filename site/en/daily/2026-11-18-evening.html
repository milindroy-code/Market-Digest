Wednesday, 18 November's session put the brakes on November's rally of more than 10% so far. The Sensex fell 500.38 points (-0.61%) to close at 82,031.81, and the Nifty dropped 159.45 points (-0.63%) to 25,227.95 — Business Standard, Free Press Journal, Daijiworld and Zee Business all cross-confirm these figures — against yesterday's (17 Nov) confirmed close of Nifty 25,387.40/Sensex 82,532.19. Both indices had closed near nine-month highs on Tuesday after a rally of more than 10% in November — today was simply a natural pause, with no specific trigger. The Nifty's intraday low was 25,141.80, from which it recovered roughly 86 points into the close. The broader market was weak too — Nifty Midcap 100 down 1.22%, Smallcap 100 down 0.35%.

There was no circuit filter or policy surprise behind the selloff — this was a classic profit-booking day, even as Nvidia overnight reported a blockbuster Q3 FY27 result that hasn't been priced into Indian markets yet (today's Explainer covers exactly why). Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in revenue (+103% YoY) after Tuesday's US close, above the ~$108-109bn consensus, with Data Center revenue of $107.6bn (+110% YoY) — adjusted EPS of $2.68 also beat, and the company guided Q4 revenue to $130bn (±3%), assuming no China data-center compute revenue. The stock recovered in after-hours trading from a regular-session close of $155.46 to roughly $163.29 (about +4-5%) — the first earnings print since Jensen Huang's resignation. The full Wall Street/Asia reaction is still to come, since the number landed well after Indian markets had already closed for the day.

In commodities, gold and silver's crash continued — MCX December gold opened ₹2,601 lower at ₹1,31,622/10g, silver also fell sharply (to roughly ₹1,63,775, down from yesterday's close of about ₹1,69,597) — on a stronger dollar and rising odds of a December Fed hike. India VIX edged up to 15.56 (+0.26%) from yesterday's 15.52. Brent crude stayed in the ~$91.55-92.92/barrel range (slight variance across sources), the rupee in the ~₹93.3-93.6/USD range — none of these had an exact confirmed end-of-day print in this run, so ranges are given instead.

Explainer: Why Nvidia's Blockbuster Q3 Didn't Show Up In Indian Markets Today

The Nifty and Sensex fell today even though Nvidia reported results well above estimates overnight — that isn't a contradiction, just a timing mismatch. Nvidia's numbers landed after Tuesday's US market close, while Indian markets ran their full session today and closed well before Wall Street itself had a chance to react for a full day — the real test comes tomorrow, when Asia and GIFT Nifty get to price in the print. There's a second factor too: Indian IT majors like TCS, Infosys and HCLTech are staffing-and-services businesses, not GPU or chip makers — they don't get a mechanical lift from Nvidia's data-center capex boom the way US semiconductor-adjacent stocks might. Today's selloff ran on its own domestic logic instead — a profit-booking pause after an overbought rally, plus a gold/silver crash that dragged Consumer Durables down — largely decoupled from Nvidia for the same day.

Today's Concept
Risk-Reward Ratio

Before entering a trade, compare what you stand to lose (distance to your stop-loss) against what you stand to gain (distance to your target). A trade with a 1:3 risk-reward ratio can still be profitable overall even if you're wrong more often than you're right — this is why professional traders talk about expectancy, not just win rate. Today's pick from the sequential rotation, tagged "concept" — especially relevant on a profit-booking day like today, when deciding whether to hold winners or exit. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
