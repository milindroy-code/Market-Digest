Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Long Straddle

Buy a call and a put at the same strike and expiry. You profit if the underlying makes a big move in either direction — you're betting on volatility itself, not a direction. The cost is the combined premium of both options, which is also your maximum loss if the stock sits still. Traders reach for this ahead of binary events — like tomorrow's Trump-Xi summit, whose outcome is a genuine two-sided risk for markets, even though today's move has been mild and positive.
