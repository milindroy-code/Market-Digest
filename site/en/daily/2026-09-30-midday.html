Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Long Straddle

Buy a call and a put at the same strike and expiry. You profit if the underlying makes a big move in either direction — you're betting on volatility itself, not a direction. The cost is the combined premium of both options, which is also your maximum loss if the stock sits still. Traders reach for this ahead of binary events like a Fed decision or an earnings call — and today's session showed exactly that kind of volatility, with a weak morning open flipping into a sharp green reversal, and a single-stock move like PB Fintech's underscoring how much movement is on offer.
