Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Protective Put

Buy a put option on a stock you already own, as insurance. If the stock falls, the put gains value and offsets the loss; if it doesn't, you've paid a small premium for peace of mind — the same logic as paying for car insurance you hope never to use. A way to stay invested through an uncertain event — like this evening's US CPI print — without fully exiting a position. Today's pick by sequential rotation. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation for any specific trade or strike.
