Explainer: The Fed Decides Tonight — So When Does It Actually Hit India?

Tonight the US Federal Reserve will announce its rate decision — at US Eastern Time 2:00 PM, roughly 11:30 PM IST, with a press conference following around midnight. But the Indian stock market will have closed for the day hours earlier, at 3:30 PM IST. So is tonight's decision simply irrelevant to Indian investors until tomorrow? Not quite — it just doesn't travel directly, it travels through a chain. US stocks react first, overnight; that move then carries into Asian markets when they open the next morning; and GIFT Nifty — which trades almost around the clock — absorbs that entire overnight mood and hands Indian markets a directional signal before the opening bell even rings. That's why this digest's own playbook treats a Fed decision's "full market reaction" as something that usually shows up in India's next complete trading session, not the same day — which, for tonight's decision, means tomorrow (29 October).

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Protective Put

Buy a put option on a stock you already own, as insurance. If the stock falls, the put gains value and offsets the loss; if it doesn't, you've paid a small premium for peace of mind — the same logic as paying for car insurance you hope never to use. It's a common way to stay invested through an uncertain event — like tonight's Fed decision — without fully exiting a position. This is an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
