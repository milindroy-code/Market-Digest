Explainer

What is GIFT Nifty, and why can it diverge from how Asia actually trades? GIFT Nifty — a Nifty futures contract traded out of GIFT City, Gujarat — runs across two sessions: 6:30 AM to 3:40 PM IST, then 4:35 PM to 2:45 AM IST the next day, keeping it live through US market hours and letting it absorb overnight global cues almost instantly. That's why traders treat it as the most accurate same-morning proxy for where Nifty will open. But it isn't perfect: it's a thin, derivatives-only market, and if its last trade is driven by one specific, short-lived piece of news, it can look out of step with the broader mood across a full Asia session — exactly what happened today, when a single GIFT Nifty read showed +1.91% even as the KOSPI had already crashed 7.4%. The real test always comes at the actual cash-market open, once full liquidity arrives.

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Delta Hedging

Delta measures how much an option's price moves for a ₹1 move in the underlying. Large options traders and market makers buy or sell the underlying stock to offset their book's delta, staying roughly neutral to small price moves — it's how institutional desks can sell options in size without simply betting on direction. This concept is especially relevant on a day like today — strong domestic uptrend momentum on one side, a sharp overnight global risk-off move on the other — where players like market makers would want to hedge their positions without betting on either direction. This is purely an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
