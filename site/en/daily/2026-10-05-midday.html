"The trend is your friend until the end when it bends."

— Ed Seykota

This morning's optimism has faded by midday. On Day 1 of the RBI's three-day MPC meeting, markets gapped up sharply on upbeat Asian cues and softer crude — Nifty opened 110.45 points (0.49%) higher at 22,532.40, and Sensex opened 431.25 points (0.60%) higher at 72,340.95, with Sensex's gain stretching to 453 points (0.63%) by 9:25 AM. Banks, Metal, and Oil & Gas have been today's sector leaders, while Healthcare, Pharma, and IT are laggards. The RBI's MPC meeting is passing through Day 1 without any fresh signal — the outcome is still due Wednesday (7 October).

Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Trailing Stop-Loss

Instead of a fixed stop-loss, a trailing stop moves up as the price rises, locking in gains while still giving the trade room to run. It's a way to let winners run — a piece of trading wisdom you'll see quoted often — without needing to predict exactly when a trend will end. Today illustrates the concept well — a strong morning open that has largely given back its gains, and a stock like Orient Cables falling well off its intraday high on profit-booking, both show why protecting gains, not just chasing them, matters. This is an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
