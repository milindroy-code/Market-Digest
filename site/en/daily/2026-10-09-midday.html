Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Trailing Stop-Loss

A trailing stop-loss is a stop order that moves up automatically as a stock's price rises, locking in gains while still letting the position run — unlike a fixed stop, it never moves backward. On a day like today, where the market is bouncing sharply off an oversold low but hasn't yet confirmed a genuine trend reversal, this is exactly the kind of tool a trader riding the bounce might use: stay in if the rally extends, but exit automatically if it turns out to be just a short-covering move that fades by the close. This is an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
