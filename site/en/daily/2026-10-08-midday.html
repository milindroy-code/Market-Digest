Strategy Corner
Today's Concept
Protective Put

Buy a put option on a stock you already own (say, an IT holding) — as insurance. If the stock falls, the put gains value and offsets the loss; if it doesn't, you've paid a small premium for peace of mind — the same logic as car insurance you hope never to use. A day like today — with TCS's results due tonight and the whole IT pack waiting on the reaction — is exactly the kind of setup where investors look to protect an existing holding without selling out of it. This is an educational concept, not a recommendation to place the trade.
