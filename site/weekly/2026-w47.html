Explainer

Is hafte Wall Street se chaar alag-alag raat ke shocks aaye — chip-selloff, phir Fed minutes, phir ek IPO crash — phir bhi Nifty/Sensex ne hafta 2.6%/2.85% upar band kiya. Yeh decoupling kaise hua? Pehli wajah composition hai: Nifty ka sabse bada weight financials/consumption/auto mein hai, semiconductor ya AI-capex mein nahi — isliye Nvidia-linked selloffs (Monday, Thursday raat) jo US indices ko directly hit karte hain, unka Indian market par sirf sentiment-level, ek-din ka asar hota hai, structural nahi. Doosri wajah hai continued FII buying — teen confirmed sessions mein hi ~₹14,926cr net inflow, jo har gap-down ke baad ek ready buying-cushion deta hai. Teesri wajah timing hai: Nvidia ka apna strong Q3 beat (Tuesday raat) bhi same-day Indian market mein nahi dikha, kyunki woh US close ke baad aata hai — iska matlab Indian market US news ko agle poore trading din ke through react karta hai, ek raat mein nahi. Isliye jab US market "correction" jaisa dikhta hai, zaroori nahi ki Indian market usi scale mein follow kare — composition, liquidity aur timing, teeno alag-alag decoupling create karte hain.

Long Strangle

Straddle jaisa hi, lekin aap ek hi strike ke bajaye alag (out-of-the-money) strikes par ek call aur ek put khareedte hain. Yeh straddle se sasta hota hai put on karne mein, kyunki dono legs money se aur door hote hain, lekin underlying ko profit mein aane ke liye aur zyada move karna padta hai. Direction pick kiye bina ek bade move par bet lagane ka ek lower-cost tareeka. Sequential rotation se is hafte ka pick, high-volatility tag ke saath — aur ek hafta jisme Nifty/Sensex ne do separate 1,300+/1,500+ point ke intraday round-trips dikhaye (Thursday aur Friday dono), ek strategy jo sirf "bada move hoga" par bet karti hai, direction par nahi, is hafte ke actual price action se kaafi directly relate karti hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
