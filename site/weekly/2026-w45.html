Explainer

Is hafte do alag-alag dino par ek hi mechanism dikha — market-cap weighting. Monday ko Reliance Industries akeli +11.5-11.6% chadhi, aur apne bade market cap (₹17 lakh crore) ki wajah se akele ~198 Nifty points contribute kar gayi — hafte ke ~388-point move ka lagbhag aadha. Friday ko bilkul ulta dikha: index 2%+ upar band hua, lekin NSE par 1,439 stocks chadhe aur 2,141 gire — phir bhi ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank aur Bharti Airtel jaise teen heavyweights ne, apne bade weightage ki wajah se, akele 240+ index points contribute karke poore market ko upar khींच diya, baaki 2,000+ chhote stocks jo bhi kar rahe the usse independent. Nifty aur Sensex "equal-weighted" nahi hain — har stock ka index par asar uske market capitalisation ke proportional hota hai, ek equal share nahi. Yahi wajah hai ki sirf index number dekhna ("Nifty +2% upar") misleading ho sakta hai — asli tasveer ke liye breadth (advances vs declines) aur broader indices (Midcap/Smallcap) dono dekhna zaroori hai, jaisa is poore hafte ne baar-baar dikhaya.

Is Hafte Ka Concept
Max Pain Theory

Yeh idea ki ek underlying expiry tak us strike price ki taraf gravitate karta hai jahan option buyers, collectively, sabse zyada paisa lose karte hain — kyunki option sellers (jo aksar bade, better-capitalized players hote hain) usi outcome se fayda uthate hain. Ek widely-watched lekin imperfect signal, ek guaranteed prediction ki jagah ek input ki tarah useful. Ek "concept" tag ke saath, is hafte ka sequential-rotation pick — aur ek hafta jisme itna bada Q2 results haul aaya ho aur Nifty itne fresh highs chhue ho, options-market positioning (jahan max pain jaisa theory apply hota hai) ko samajhna khaas taur par relevant hai jab index analyst-flagged resistance zones (24,700-25,500) ke itne kareeb trade kar raha ho. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
