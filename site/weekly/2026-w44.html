Explainer

Is hafte Alphabet aur Amazon dono ne strong headline growth report ki — fir bhi stocks gire, kyunki investors "capex" aur "free cash flow" par atak gaye. Capex matlab woh paisa jo company data centers, chips aur infrastructure banane mein kharch karti hai; yeh ek real cash outflow hai, profit-and-loss statement par expense nahi dikhta turant. Jab Alphabet ne $51.4bn aur Amazon ne $77.3bn sirf ek quarter mein capex par kharch kiya — dono ke liye record levels — to unka free cash flow (operating cash minus capex) negative ho gaya, matlab woh business se zyada cash kharch kar rahe hain jitna kama rahe hain, AI infrastructure mein bet lagate hue. Investors ka dar: agar yeh AI investment jaldi revenue mein convert nahi hui, to margins compress honge. Yehi wajah hai ki dono companies' stocks double-digit percentage mein gir gaye, strong revenue growth ke bawajood — aur yehi theme Nikkei se CSI 300 tak poore Asia mein failta gaya.

The Iron Butterfly

Iron condor ka ek tighter cousin: ek at-the-money call aur put bechte hain, aur further out-of-the-money call aur put protection ke liye kharidte hain. Yeh sabse zyada payout deta hai agar underlying exactly middle strike par finish ho — ek condor se zyada reward potential deta hai ek bahut still market ke liye, lekin ek narrower profitable range ki keemat par. Is hafte ka pattern iske "range-bound" tag se poora match nahi karta — net move +2% directional tha — lekin hafte ke beech ke din (Tuesday ka near-flat expiry, Wednesday ka moderate red) is tarah ki ek strategy ke liye zyada fit the, jabki Thursday-Friday ke bade moves ne isse punish kiya hota agar koi trade mein hota.

Yeh digest sirf news summary hai, investment advice nahi. Strategy Corner ek concept explain karta hai, use karne ki recommendation nahi. Numbers multiple sources se liye gaye hain aur revise ho sakte hain — trading se pehle apne broker ya exchange par live prices confirm karein.
