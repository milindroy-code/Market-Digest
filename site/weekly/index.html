Explainer

Jab Infosys jaisi company apna revenue guidance "constant currency" (CC) mein deti hai, to woh currency ke fluctuations ko hata kar dekhti hai — matlab agar rupee ya dollar aapas mein 5% move bhi kar jaaye, CC growth number waisa hi rahega, kyunki woh sirf real, underlying business growth measure karta hai, exchange-rate ka noise nahi. Yehi wajah hai ki Friday ko Infosys ka FY27 CC revenue guidance 1.5-3% se ghatakar 1-2% karna itna bada trigger bana — yeh currency move nahi, genuine demand-softness ka signal tha. IT companies ke liye yeh number khaaskar important hai kyunki unka revenue ka bada hissa dollar, euro, pound jaise multiple currencies mein aata hai; agar sirf reported (currency-sahit) growth dekhein, to ek weak quarter bhi favorable currency moves ki wajah se achha dikh sakta hai, ya ek strong quarter bhi adverse moves ki wajah se kharab. CC guidance hi investors ko asli tasveer deta hai.

Delta Hedging

Delta batata hai ki ek option ki price underlying stock mein ₹1 ke move par kitna badalti hai. Is hafte jaisa ek sustained, crude-driven volatility spike (India VIX ka lagatar upar jaana, Infosys jaisa single-stock shock, aur Nifty ka apna hi breakout zone wapas kho dena) exactly woh environment hai jahan bade options desks apni delta-hedges ko sabse zyada actively adjust karte hain — jaise-jaise underlying move karta hai, unhe apni delta-neutral position maintain karne ke liye underlying buy/sell karte rehna padta hai. Yeh institutional-scale technique hai, retail ke liye seedha replicate karna practical nahi, lekin samajhna zaroori hai: ek volatile hafte mein jo extra volume dikhta hai, usme hedging flows ka bhi bada hissa ho sakta hai, sirf directional buying/selling nahi.
