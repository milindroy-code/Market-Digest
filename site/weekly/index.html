Global Cues

Is hafte ki asli story oil hai, equities nahi. Saudi Arabia ne apni critical 1,200 km East–West crude pipeline band kar di — yeh wahi route hai jo Strait of Hormuz ko poori tarah bypass karta hai — Iraq se launch hue drone attacks ke baad 10 September ko, aur Strait ke paas bhi aur attacks hue. Yeh pipeline normally global oil supply ka roughly 4% carry karti hai.

Brent crude 3.5% se zyada jump kiya, $107–108/barrel se upar; WTI $103 ke paas pahuncha. US diesel prices $6/gallon cross kiye, ek record high. Gold $4,461/oz par tha safe-haven demand ki wajah se, jabki rupee ₹95.56/$ par tha — weak currency ke saath costlier crude, yeh combination usually equities aur inflation expectations dono ko pressure karta hai.

Wall Street Friday ko opposite direction mein gaya: Dow ne 509 points (+0.98%) gain kiya, 52,573.29 tak; S&P 500 0.86% badha, 7,656.98 tak; aur Nasdaq 0.96% chadha, 26,333.04 tak — cooler US inflation data ki wajah se — ek positive cue jo ab yahan ke oil-driven risk-off mood ke saath tension mein hai.
