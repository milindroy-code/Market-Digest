Explainer

Is hafte ka sabse bada single-stock shock — Yes Bank ka Supreme Court mein haarna — ek instrument ke bare mein tha jo zyada retail investors ke radar par nahi rehta: AT1 (Additional Tier-1) bonds. Yeh bank jo special perpetual bonds issue karte hain apna capital-buffer badhane ke liye, aur in bonds mein ek built-in "loss-absorbing" feature hota hai — agar bank ka capital ek critical level se neeche gir jaaye, to regulator in bonds ko fully write-off kar sakta hai, bina shareholders ko pehle wipe-out kiye. Yehi exactly 2020 mein Yes Bank ke rescue ke dauran hua tha — administrator ne ₹8,415 crore ke AT1 bonds ko zero kar diya, bondholders ke objections ke bawajood. Bombay High Court ne 2023 mein yeh decision ulat diya tha, lekin Supreme Court ne ab wahi 2023 verdict hi khaarij kar diya — matlab original write-off hi final rehta hai. Bondholders ke liye yeh ek costly lesson hai: AT1 bonds "bond" kehlate hain, lekin risk-profile mein woh equity ke kaafi kareeb hote hain — higher yield ka vaada karte hain, lekin ek crisis mein sabse pehle wipe-out hone wale instruments mein se bhi hote hain.

Delta Hedging

Delta batata hai ki ek option ki price underlying stock mein ₹1 ke move par kitna badalti hai. Is hafte jaisa ek clean technical breakout (22,747/22,947 zone ka decisively cross hona) exactly woh moment hai jab bade options desks apni hedges ko sabse zyada actively adjust karte hain — jaise-jaise underlying move karta hai, unhe apni delta-neutral position maintain karne ke liye underlying buy/sell karte rehna padta hai. Yeh institutional-scale technique hai, retail ke liye seedha replicate karna practical nahi, lekin samajhna zaroori hai: jab ek breakout ke din volumes achanak badh jaate hain, usme hedging flows ka bhi ek bada hissa ho sakta hai, sirf directional buying/selling nahi.

Yeh digest sirf news summary hai, investment advice nahi. Strategy Corner ek concept explain karta hai, use karne ki recommendation nahi. Numbers multiple sources se liye gaye hain aur revise ho sakte hain — trading se pehle apne broker ya exchange par live prices confirm karein.
