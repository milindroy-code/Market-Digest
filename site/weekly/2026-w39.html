Explainer

Tata Sons ka AGM ek company-law mechanism ko spotlight mein le aaya jo zyada logon ko pata nahi: quorum. Kisi bhi company ka annual general meeting tabhi legally valid hota hai jab minimum number of members (ya unke authorized representatives) present hon. Agar koi bada shareholder khud apna representative nominate nahi kar paata — jaisa Sir Ratan Tata Trust (~23.56% stake) ke saath hua, regulatory restrictions ki wajah se — toh poori meeting quorum ki kami se deferred ho sakti hai, jaisa Tata Sons ke saath 18 August ko hua. Companies Act, 2013 ka Section 97 exactly is situation ke liye ek escape route deta hai: agar koi company required quorum ke saath apna AGM convene nahi kar paati, toh woh National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) se request kar sakti hai ki woh khud meeting call kare ya convene karne ka direction de, kabhi-kabhi reduced quorum requirements ke saath. Yeh isliye matlab rakhta hai kyunki AGM hi wahi jagah hai jahan board reappointments — jaise N Chandrasekaran ka — formally ratify hote hain; jab tak quorum resolve nahi hota, woh appointments technically limbo mein rehte hain.

Position Sizing

Har trade ko same amount of capital nahi milna chahiye. Professional traders position size decide karte hain apne stop-loss ki distance aur total capital ka kitna hissa woh ek idea par risk karna chahte hain us par — often sirf 1-2% per trade — na ki fixed number of shares ya lots par. Strategy khud kam matter karti hai, size zyada — ek great setup bhi, agar position oversized ho, poore account ko wipe kar sakta hai. Is hafte jaisa pattern — ek steady mid-week recovery jo Thursday ke double-shock (crude + IRDAI) mein ek hi din mein reverse ho gayi — exactly wahi risk hai jo disciplined position sizing manage karti hai: ek single bad day poore portfolio ko cripple nahi karna chahiye.
