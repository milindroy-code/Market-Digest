Explainer

RBI ne Wednesday ko sirf rate hi nahi badhaya, apna "stance" bhi "calibrated tightening" tak shift kar diya — aur yehi dusra hissa market ke liye zyada bada signal tha. Rate ek number hai; stance batata hai ki central bank aage kya karega. "Accommodative" ka matlab hai cuts zyada likely hain, "neutral" ka matlab dono directions open hain, aur "calibrated tightening" ka matlab hai ki RBI ne near-term mein rate cuts ko practically table se hata diya hai, chahe agla move hike ho ya na ho. Isiliye ek hike jo poll-consensus ke mutabik hi tha (62% economists ne expect kiya tha), phir bhi rate-sensitive sectors (Realty, Auto, Banks) ko nervous kar gaya — market ne hike khud discount kar rakha tha, lekin "cuts ab jald nahi aayenge" wala confirmation ek fresh, thoda hawkish surprise tha. Yeh forward-guidance ka hi ek version hai jo Fed aur doosre central banks bhi use karte hain — number se zyada, future path ka signal hi price move karta hai.

Delta Hedging

Delta batata hai ki ek option ki price underlying stock mein ₹1 ke move par kitna badalti hai. Bade options traders aur market makers apni underlying position buy/sell karte rehte hain apne poore book ke delta ko offset karne ke liye, taaki woh chhote price moves ke against roughly neutral rahein — yehi tareeka hai jisse institutional desks bina direction par bet kiye bade size mein options sell kar paate hain. Is hafte jaisi volatility (hike-driven dip, oil-driven crash, earnings-driven rebound, sab paanch din ke andar) exactly woh environment hai jahan delta-neutral desks apni hedges ko sabse zyada active rakhte hain — yeh ek institutional technique hai, retail ke liye seedha replicate karna mushkil hai, lekin samajhna zaroori hai ki bade players kaise volatility manage karte hain.

Yeh digest sirf news summary hai, investment advice nahi. Strategy Corner ek concept explain karta hai, use karne ki recommendation nahi. Numbers multiple sources se liye gaye hain aur revise ho sakte hain — trading se pehle apne broker ya exchange par live prices confirm karein.
