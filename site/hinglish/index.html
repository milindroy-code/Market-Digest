Explainer

Agar risks kal jaise hi hain, to aaj market rally kyun hui? Kyunki ek expected event aur ek surprise event market ko alag tarah se move karte hain. Aaj raat ka Fed hike, agar hota hai, to pehle se hi "priced in" hai — yeh bond yields, rupee, aur options prices mein already reflect ho raha hai. Market aksar ek fully-expected event se pehle calm ho jaata hai, kyunki react karne ke liye naya information kuch bacha hi nahi hota. Aaj raat asli baat yeh nahi hogi ki hike hua ya nahi — asli baat hogi Fed ka tone: dot plot kitne aur hikes signal karta hai, aur Fed inflation se zyada worried hai ya growth se. Yehi dekhna zaroori hai, sirf number nahi.

Kya Dekhna Hai

Fed ka tone, sirf number nahi. Dot plot aur press conference ki language usse zyada matter karti hai jitna khud hike matter karta hai.

Kya aaj ki rally kal broaden hoti hai. Midcaps aur smallcaps aaj rally mein nahi the — real conviction ke liye unka bhi involve hona zaroori hai.

Hero Motors ka subscription build-up. 1.05× ka day-one number abhi badh (ya ghat) sakta hai next do din mein.

Kanohar Electricals ka actual listing-day trade. ~38% GMP-implied gain ek signal hai, guarantee nahi.

Crude oil aur Saudi pipeline. Abhi bhi koi restart timeline nahi — koi bhi headline rupee aur rates conversation ko fast move kar sakti hai.

Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Long Straddle

Same strike aur expiry par ek call aur ek put dono khareedo. Agar underlying kisi bhi direction mein bada move karta hai to profit hota hai — aap volatility par bet kar rahe ho, direction par nahi. Cost hai dono options ka combined premium, aur agar stock flat rehta hai to yehi aapka maximum loss hai. Traders yeh strategy binary events se pehle use karte hain — jaise Fed decision ya earnings call — exactly jaisa setup aaj raat ka Fed announcement hai.

Yeh digest sirf news summary hai, investment advice nahi. Strategy Corner ek concept explain karta hai, use karne ki recommendation nahi. Numbers multiple sources se liye gaye hain aur revise ho sakte hain — trading se pehle apne broker ya exchange par live prices confirm karein.
