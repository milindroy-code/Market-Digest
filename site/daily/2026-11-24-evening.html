Calendar Spread

Calendar Spread mein tum same strike par ek near-term option bechte ho aur ek longer-dated option khareedte ho. Near-term option ka time decay expiry ke jitna paas aata hai utni tezi se badhta hai, is liye agar underlying roughly flat rahe, toh yeh decay-rate ke farak se profit banane ki koshish karta hai — ek strategy jo direction par nahi, time par hi based hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "range-bound" tag ke saath — aur aaj se zyada range-bound din ho hi nahi sakta, kyunki poore din koi trading hi nahi hui, Nifty/Sensex literally zero move ke saath. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
