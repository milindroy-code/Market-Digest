Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Covered Call

Agar aapke paas already koi stock hai, to us par ek call option bech sakte hain. Aap premium ko income ki tarah collect karte hain, aur agar stock strike se neeche rehta hai, to aap stock aur premium dono rakh lete hain. Trade-off yeh hai: agar stock strike se kaafi upar rally karta hai, to aap us upar ke gains miss kar dete hain, kyunki aap us price par bechne ke liye obligated hote hain. Aaj ke jaise ek thin-data, wait-and-watch session mein yeh ek "any" condition fit concept hai — sequential rotation se aaj ka pick. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
