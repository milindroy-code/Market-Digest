Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
The Iron Butterfly

Iron Condor ka ek tighter version — at-the-money call aur put sell kiye jaate hain, aur protection ke liye thoda further out-of-the-money call aur put buy kiye jaate hain. Yeh sabse zyada payout tab deta hai jab underlying middle strike par exactly finish ho — ek condor ke comparison mein higher potential reward, lekin narrower profitable range ki cost par. Aaj jaisa din — jab index sharply lower open hone ke baad ek tight range (~22,700-22,900) mein wapas aa gaya hai, pharma ke support se — exactly waisa hi setup hai jahan yeh concept relevant hota hai, halaanki Bank Nifty ke expiry-driven flows range ko kabhi bhi disrupt kar sakte hain.
