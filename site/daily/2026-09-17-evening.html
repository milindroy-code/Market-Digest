Explainer

Trump ka tariff threat toh abhi bhi wahi hai — toh Pharma stocks aaj kyun uchhal gaye? Kyunki price move sirf risk ke hone se nahi, balki risk kitna "priced in" hai usse driven hota hai. Pharma index paanch seedhe din se gir raha tha, US ke proposed generic-drug tariffs ke overhang ki wajah se, jab tak stocks genuinely oversold nahi ho gaye — value buyers ke liye ek entry point ban gaya. Underlying risk khatam nahi hua hai: analysts ne bhi bataya hai ki generic medicines par 0% tariff 31 July 2028 tak hold karta hai, matlab yeh threat abhi bhi years door hai, immediate nahi. Aaj ka bounce is baat ka signal hai ki sentiment ek oversold correction ke baad cool ho raha hai — na ki actual risk resolve ho gaya hai. Yeh farak samajhna zaroori hai: ek stock "cheap ho gaya" isliye upar jaana, aur "problem solve ho gayi" isliye upar jaana — do alag kahaniyan hain.

Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Covered Call

Agar aapke paas already koi stock hai, toh uske against ek call option sell kar dena. Aap premium ko income ki tarah collect karte ho, aur agar stock strike se neeche rehta hai, toh aap stock aur premium dono keep karte ho. Trade-off yeh hai: agar stock strike se kaafi upar rally kar jaaye, toh aap us extra gain se miss ho jaate ho, kyunki aap us price par sell karne ke obligated ho. Aaj jaise flat, rotation-heavy din ke liye — jahan headline index barely move hua — yeh ek popular tareeka hai already-held positions se extra income nikalne ka.
