Explainer

Trump ka ek geopolitical proposal reject karna, seedha Indian GDP forecasts ko kyun touch kar raha hai? Strait of Hormuz duniya ke seaborne oil aur LNG trade ka ek bahut bada hissa carry karta hai — India apni crude requirement ka ~88% import karta hai, aur roughly do-tihai crude ke saath aadha LNG bhi Hormuz se guzarne wale routes se aata hai. Jab is chokepoint ke around talks break down hote hain (jaisa weekend mein Trump ke Iran-proposal reject karne se hua), crude prices turant ek disruption-risk premium price-in kar lete hain — bhale hi abhi tak koi actual physical supply block na hua ho. India ke liye transmission simple hai: costlier crude → wider trade aur current-account deficit → weaker rupee → imported inflation. Analysts ab estimate kar rahe hain ki ek sustained $10/barrel crude rise, India ke CAD ko 40-50bps tak widen kar sakta hai — yehi wajah hai ki Q2 FY27 GDP growth forecasts ~7.7% se ~6.7% taraf trim ho rahe hain. Ek single rejected proposal, oil ke through, poore macro outlook ko reprice kar deta hai.

Delta Hedging

Delta measure karta hai ki ek option ki price, underlying mein ₹1 ke move par kitna move karti hai. Large options traders aur market makers apne book ka delta offset karne ke liye underlying stock buy ya sell karte hain, taaki woh chhote price moves ke against roughly neutral rahein — yehi wajah hai ki institutional desks bina simply direction par bet kiye, size mein options sell kar paate hain. Aaj jaise din — jab Bank Nifty ka monthly F&O expiry hai aur GIFT Nifty ka signal Somwar ke broad rout ke against thoda positive hai — market makers ka delta-hedging flow hi hai jo expiry ke around achanak, sharp intraday swings create kar sakta hai, bina kisi naye fundamental news ke.
