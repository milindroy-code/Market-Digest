Morning Briefing
Wednesday, 16 September 2026 · pre-market Levels below are Tuesday's close — opening watch, not today's result

All eyes are on the Fed tonight, not the tape. Nifty slid to a five-month low on Tuesday as surging crude and bond yields overwhelmed an IT-led rally, and Brent has pushed further to above $108 on the still-unresolved Saudi pipeline outage — but the real catalyst lands at 11:30pm IST tonight, when the Federal Reserve announces its rate decision alongside fresh economic projections.

Tuesday was a round trip: both indices opened sharply higher (Sensex +588, Nifty +178) on IT strength, then reversed hard through the session as oil and yield worries took over, closing at the day's lows.

Global Cues

The Fed decision is tonight's main event. The FOMC's two-day meeting concludes today, with the rate decision and updated Summary of Economic Projections (the "dot plot") due at 2:00pm ET — 11:30pm IST — followed by a press conference. US markets fell Tuesday into the decision: the Dow lost 328 points (−0.63%) to 52,093.11, the S&P 500 slid 0.45% to 7,585.73, and the Nasdaq dropped 0.78% to 25,981.57, as the 10-year Treasury yield surged to a multi-year high.

Brent crude is now at $108.48/barrel (+2.6%), with WTI above $102. Saudi Aramco has suspended operations on the 1,200km East–West pipeline entirely while it assesses drone-strike damage — Riyadh has given no timeline or damage estimate, leaving roughly 4 million barrels a day (about 4% of global supply) in limbo. Gold is at $4,320.80/oz, up $28 on the day. The rupee is holding near ₹95.6–95.9/$, little changed on the month but up over the past week.

Macro & Policy
  • US Fed — rate decision and dot plot due tonight (11:30pm IST); this is the single biggest macro trigger global and Indian markets face this week.
  • RBI held the repo rate at 5.25% at its August review, a fourth consecutive pause. The next MPC meeting runs 5–7 October 2026, with oil-driven inflation risk now firmly on the agenda.
  • SEBI — since 1 September, every new single-holder demat account and mutual fund folio must carry a nomination or a signed opt-out declaration.
  • RBI — the special FCNR(B) dollar-rupee swap window closed 31 August; new NRI deposits no longer get the concessional pricing that had lifted rates to roughly 6–7%.
