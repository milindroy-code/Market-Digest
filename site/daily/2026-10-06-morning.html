Explainer

RBI ka "neutral stance" kya hota hai, aur yeh rate number jitna hi important kyun hai? Jab RBI apni policy announce karta hai, to sirf rate ka number hi nahi, uske saath wala "stance" bhi utna hi important hota hai. "Neutral" ka matlab hai RBI ka agla move dono directions mein ja sakta hai — data par depend karega. "Accommodative" ka matlab hai RBI growth ko support karne ke liye bias kar raha hai (rate cuts ki taraf), jabki "withdrawal of accommodation" ya ek hawkish tilt ka matlab hai RBI inflation-fighting ko priority de raha hai (aur hikes ki taraf bias kar raha hai). Isliye kal (Budhwar) jab outcome aayega, to market sirf "25bp hike hua ya nahi" nahi dekhega — woh RBI ke stance ke exact language ko bhi utni hi closely parse karega, kyunki ek "hike with neutral stance" ka matlab hai ek one-off move, jabki ek "hike with a hawkish tilt" ka matlab hai aur hikes aa sakte hain. Yahi wajah hai ki experienced market-watchers policy statement ka poora text padhte hain, sirf headline number nahi.

Delta Hedging

Delta yeh measure karta hai ki ek option ka price, underlying mein ₹1 ke move par kitna move karta hai. Bade options traders aur market makers apne book ka delta offset karne ke liye underlying stock buy ya sell karte hain, taaki woh chhote price moves ke against roughly neutral rahein — yahi wajah hai ki institutional desks bina simply direction par bet kiye bhi size mein options sell kar sakte hain. Aaj jaisa ek din — RBI MPC ka Day 2, outcome kal — exactly woh type ka setup hai jahan volatility ke against hedge karna ek relevant concept ban jaata hai, ek concept jo educational hai, trade placement ki recommendation nahi.
