Explainer

Aaj ka sabse confusing sawaal yeh hai: agar inflation (core PCE) actually thandi aayi, to US bond yields upar kyun gaye? Bond yields sirf Fed ke overnight rate ka reflection nahi hote — woh investors ki future growth, government borrowing/supply, aur "term premium" (long-dated bonds hold karne ke extra risk ke liye maangi gayi extra return) ki expectations bhi capture karte hain. Jab corporate earnings aur GDP data expectations se behtar aate hain, investors sochte hain ki economy aage bhi "hot" rahegi, isliye woh long-dated bonds hold karne ke liye zyada yield maangte hain — bhale hi near-term Fed-hike odds (jo cooler inflation ki wajah se gire) kam ho jaayein. Yahi wajah hai ki aaj October hike-odds 51% se 38% gir gaye, phir bhi 10-year aur 30-year yields upar gaye. In high US yields ka direct asar yeh hota hai ki risk-free US Treasuries emerging-market equities (jaise India) ke comparison mein zyada attractive ban jaati hain — isliye FIIs apna paisa nikalte hain, jo rupye aur Indian stocks dono par pressure daalta hai. Yahi mechanism hai jo aaj ke crash-then-partial-recovery session ke peeche kaam kar raha tha.

Protective Put

Agar aap pehle se ek stock hold kar rahe hain, to uske against ek put option buy karo — ek insurance ki tarah. Agar stock girta hai, to put ki value badhti hai aur loss offset ho jaata hai; agar nahi girta, to aapne sirf peace-of-mind ke liye ek chhota sa premium pay kiya — bilkul waisi hi logic jaisi car insurance ki, jisse aap use karna nahi chahte.
