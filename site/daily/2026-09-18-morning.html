Explainer

Mangalwar raat Fed ke hawkish hike ne Wall Street ko gira diya tha — toh Thursday raat wahi market itni tezi se wapas kyun aaya, jabki Fed ka message nahi badla? Kyunki stocks sirf policy rate se nahi, balki bond yields se bhi move karte hain. 10-year Treasury yield, jo 2007 ke baad apne highest level tak pahunch gayi thi, Thursday ko apni 8-din ki rising streak todkar neeche aayi — aur yeh exactly wahi cheez hai jo growth aur tech stocks (jinki value future earnings par zyada depend karti hai) ko sabse zyada relief deti hai. Oil ke thanda hone se bhi inflation ka pressure kam hua. Nvidia aur Amazon jaise stocks 2%+ chadhe, aur chipmaker index 3% upar gaya — sabse zyada relief unhi sectors mein dikha jo rates ke sabse zyada sensitive the. Lesson yeh hai: ek hike "priced in" hone ka matlab yeh nahi ki uske baad ka poora path bhi fix ho gaya — yield curve khud agle din ka sabse bada driver ban sakta hai.

Protective Put

Apne paas already hue stock par ek put option khareed lena — bilkul insurance jaisa. Agar stock girta hai, toh put ki value badhti hai aur loss offset ho jaata hai; agar nahi girta, toh aapne bas thoda sa premium peace-of-mind ke liye pay kiya — waise hi jaise car insurance jo aap kabhi use na karna chahen. Is hafte jaisa whiplash — pehle Tuesday ka sharp Fed-hike selloff, phir Thursday ka utna hi sharp rebound — dikhata hai ki kabhi kabhi sabse smart move apni position ko bina exit kiye hue insure karna hota hai.
