Explainer

Crude oil ki price girne se Indian stock markets itna kyun move karte hain? India apni crude oil ki zaroorat ka chaar-panchwaan hissa import karta hai, isliye yahan oil ki price sirf ek energy story nahi hai — yeh ek macro story hai. Sasta barrel India ka oil-import bill chota kar deta hai, jo trade deficit kam karne mein help karta hai aur rupee par se pressure hata deta hai. Yeh directly inflation mein bhi jaata hai: fuel aur transport costs CPI basket ke almost har cheez mein feed hote hain, isliye sasta crude RBI ko accommodative rehne ke liye zyada room deta hai. Aur company level par, oil sectors jaise aviation, paints, tyres aur FMCG packaging ke liye ek direct input cost hai, isliye unke margins almost turant improve hote hain. Isi wajah se Brent crude mein 2% ka move, jaisa aaj hua, poore market ke mood ko move kar sakta hai — chahe kisi bhi individual company ke business mein kuch bhi na badla ho.

Trailing Stop-Loss

Fixed stop-loss ki jagah, ek trailing stop price ke upar jaane ke saath upar move karta hai — gains lock karte hue, trade ko run karne ke liye room bhi deta hai. Yeh "winners ko run karne dena" ka ek tareeka hai — trading wisdom ka ek piece jo aap often quote hote dekhoge — bina yeh predict kiye ki trend exactly kab khatam hoga. Aaj jaise din, jab Nifty aur Sensex apna advance extend karte hue ek flagged resistance band ke bilkul paas band hue, yeh ek reminder hai ki trend ko ride karne ke liye uska top call karna zaroori nahi hai — bas position mein rehte hue already bane gains ko protect karna hai.
