Delta Hedging

Delta measure karta hai ki ek option ka price underlying mein ₹1 ke move par kitna move karta hai. Bade options traders aur market makers apni underlying stock position buy/sell karte rehte hain apne pure book ke delta ko roughly neutral rakhne ke liye, chhote price moves ke against — yeh wahi tareeka hai jisse institutional desks bade size mein options sell kar paate hain bina seedha direction par bet kiye. "High-volatility" tag ke saath yeh pick aaj ke session ke liye particularly relevant hai — crude ka sharp Iran-tension-driven spike aur Nifty/Bank Nifty ka overbought RSI, dono mil kar aaj ek genuinely elevated-volatility backdrop bana rahe hain, jahan market-makers ki delta-hedging flows khud price action ko influence kar sakti hain. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
