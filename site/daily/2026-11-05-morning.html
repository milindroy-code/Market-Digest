Explainer: Girta Crude Oil India Ke Liye "Double" Positive Kyun Hota Hai?

Aaj ka GIFT Nifty ka sharp gap-up sirf Wall Street ki election-rally ki wajah se nahi hai — crude oil ka crash (Brent do sessions mein roughly -8%) iska ek alag, India-specific dusra tailwind hai. India apni crude oil zaroorat ka lagbhag 85% import karta hai, isliye oil crude ka girna do channels se seedha fayda deta hai: pehla, import bill kam ho jaata hai (crude India ka sabse bada single import item hai), jo current account deficit ko narrow karta hai; doosra, transport aur fuel costs kam hone se retail inflation par bhi (thode lag ke saath) downward pressure aata hai. Ek commonly-cited analyst rule-of-thumb ke mutabiq, Brent mein ek sustained $10/barrel ki girawat India ka annual import bill roughly $12-13 billion tak kam kar sakti hai aur current account deficit ko GDP ke ~0.4% tak improve kar sakti hai. Isi wajah se ek global risk-on rally plus ek oil-specific crash, India jaisi net-oil-importer economy ke liye sirf "ek aur upward market move" nahi — balki ek structural macro tailwind hai, jo purely-domestic ya oil-exporter markets ke liye utna relevant nahi hota.

Position Sizing

Position sizing ka matlab hai yeh decide karna ki ek single trade mein apne total capital ka kitna hissa risk par daalna hai — ek fixed percentage rule (jaise per-trade 1-2% risk) follow karke, chahe setup kitna bhi convincing kyun na lage. Yeh sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, "concept" tag ke saath — aur aaj ke din se iska fit khaas taur par relevant hai: ek bahut sharp +1.55% gap-up signal ke baad, jaldi mein bada position le lena tempting hota hai, lekin yeh exactly woh moment hai jab discipline sabse zyada zaroori hoti hai — kyunki itne bade one-directional gaps kabhi-kabhi intraday mein partially reverse bhi ho jaate hain (jaisa is hafte ke midday editions mein already dekha gaya). Sahi position sizing yeh ensure karta hai ki ek acchi khabar wala din bhi, agar woh fade karta hai, aapke overall capital ko seriously damage na kare. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
