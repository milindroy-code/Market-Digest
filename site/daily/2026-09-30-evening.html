Explainer

Aaj ka ek quiet lekin structurally important event tha NSE ka har-6-mahine ka Nifty 50 index reshuffle, jisme BSE Ltd ne Wipro ki jagah li. Yeh ek routine process hai — "reconstitution" — jisme exchange periodically check karta hai ki kaunsi companies index ke eligibility criteria (market cap, liquidity, free float) abhi bhi poori karti hain, aur jo nahi karti unhe replace kar diya jaata hai. Yeh "rebalancing" se different hai, jo sirf existing members ke weights adjust karta hai bina membership badle. Reconstitution ka bada practical impact yeh hota hai ki lakhon crore rupaye ke index funds aur ETFs — jo passively Nifty 50 ko track karte hain — unhe mandatorily apne portfolios adjust karne padte hain: naye member (BSE) ko buy karna, hataye gaye member (Wipro) ko sell karna, exact naye index weights ke hisaab se. Isi wajah se aisi announcement dates par closing auction mein volumes normal se kai guna zyada dekhne ko milte hain — jaisa aaj bhi hua. Interestingly BSE Ltd khud aaj apne "index-entry day" par red band hua, ek reminder ki index mein add hona automatically stock price ko upar nahi le jaata.

Long Strangle

Straddle ki tarah, lekin same strike ke bajaye aap ek call aur ek put ko different (out-of-the-money) strikes par buy karte hain. Yeh straddle se sasta hota hai kyunki dono legs money se aur door hain, lekin profit ke liye underlying ko usse bhi zyada move karna padta hai — bina direction choose kiye ek bade move par bet lagane ka ek lower-cost tareeka. Aaj ka session — jahan dopahar ki sharp recovery final ghante mein reverse ho gayi, aur Nifty apne 200-week MA ko lagatar doosre din test kar raha hai — exactly wahi tarah ki volatility dikhata hai jahan traders directionless big-move bets consider karte hain.
