Explainer

India apna zyada tar crude khud nahi produce karta, toh ek Middle East chokepoint ka news Indian stocks ko kyun hilata hai? Strait of Hormuz se roughly ek-panchwan (fifth) global oil har din guzarta hai, aur India apne crude/LNG imports ka roughly 45-50% is hi route se laata hai. Jab strait ke around military tension badhti hai, toh Brent turant chadhta hai — jaisa kal raat $106+ tak hua. India ke liye iska matlab hai teen simultaneous hits: costlier crude directly refining margins aur inflation par asar dalta hai, oil bill badhne se rupya kamzor hota hai (jo aaj ~₹96/$ ke aaspaas hai), aur paints/tyres/airlines/FMCG jaisi companies ke input costs badh jaate hain jinke margins directly crude se linked hain. Isi liye aaj ka Brent ka $108 peak se ~$107 tak ka pull back — chhota lagne wala move — itna relevant hai: yeh signal hai ki panic thoda kam hua hai, poori tarah khatam nahi.

Calendar Spread

Ek near-term option sell karo aur same strike par ek longer-dated option buy karo. Near-term option time decay se fast girta hai — decay expiry ke close aate hi accelerate hota hai — isliye agar stock roughly flat rehta hai, toh yeh position dono legs ke decay-rate difference se profit kar sakti hai. Yeh ek strategy hai jo direction par nahi, balki time par built hai. Aaj ka bounce Nifty ko ek narrow range mein wapas le aaya hai — 23,000 ka support aur 23,345-23,465 ka resistance zone ke beech — bina kisi clear trend confirmation ke. Aisi range-bound setup mein, jahan koi bada near-term directional move expected nahi hai, Calendar Spread jaisi ek time-based strategy directional bets se zyada relevant ho jaati hai.
