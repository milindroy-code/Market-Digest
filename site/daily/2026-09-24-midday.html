Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Covered Call

Agar aapke paas already koi stock hai, toh uske against ek call option sell karo. Aapko premium income ke roop mein milta hai, aur agar stock strike ke neeche rehta hai toh aap stock aur premium dono keep karte ho. Tradeoff yeh hai — agar stock strike se upar hard rally kare, toh aap us gain se aage ka fayda miss kar dete ho, kyunki aap us price par sell karne ke liye obligated ho. Aaj jaisi choppy, sharply-down session mein — jab bahut se stocks pressure mein hain lekin ek clear directional crash nahi hai — covered call ek tarika hai kuch income generate karne ka jabki aap apni existing holding continue rakhte ho.
