Explainer

Tata Trusts ne 17 September ke board resolution ko "legal nullity" ya "void ab initio" bataya — iska matlab kya hai? Companies Act ke tehat, ek company ka Articles of Association (AoA) uska internal rulebook hota hai — decide karta hai ki board resolutions kaise valid pass hote hain, kaunsi baat par kitne directors ki approval chahiye. Jab koi party kehti hai ki ek resolution "void ab initio" hai, matlab woh yeh keh rahi hai ki resolution shuru se hi legally invalid tha — jaise woh kabhi pass hua hi nahi, sirf isliye ki jis process se pass hua woh company ke apne AoA ko follow nahi karta. Yeh "voidable" se alag hai, jahan resolution valid rehta hai jab tak koi court usse formally cancel na kare. Tata Trusts ka argument hai ki unke do nominated directors Chandrasekaran ki reappointment par 1-1 split ho gaye the, jo AoA ke ek specific quorum/consent clause ko trigger karta hai — agar yeh sahi sabit hota hai, to resolution technically kabhi exist hi nahi kiya, chahe ab tak board isko implement kar raha ho. Yehi wajah hai ki dono sides ab NCLT jaane ki taiyari kar rahe hain — sirf ek governance dispute nahi, balki ek fundamental legal question, ki kya woh resolution kabhi valid tha bhi ya nahi.

Max Pain Theory

Yeh idea hai ki ek underlying apne expiry tak us strike price ki taraf gravitate karta hai jahan option buyers, collectively, sabse zyada paisa lose karte hain — kyunki option sellers (jo often bade, better-capitalized players hote hain) us outcome se benefit karte hain. Yeh ek widely-watched lekin imperfect signal hai, ek guaranteed prediction ki jagah kai inputs mein se ek ki tarah useful.
