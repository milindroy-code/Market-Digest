Explainer

FII aur DII opposite directions mein kyun move karte hain? Budhwar ko yehi dikha — FII ₹10,148.40 cr ke net seller the, DII ₹11,271.70 cr ke net buyer, aur Bank Nifty chadha jabki Nifty gira. FIIs global signals par fast react karte hain — US yields, crude prices, geopolitical risk — aur jab rupya kamzor hota hai, currency-risk ki wajah se unka selling pressure aur badh jaata hai. DIIs ka calculus alag hai: unke paas SIPs ke through steady domestic inflows aate hain, aur jab FIIs sell karte hain tabhi valuations unhe attractive lagne lagte hain — matlab unka buying aksar FII ke selling se hi trigger hota hai, ek natural counter-cyclical force ki tarah. Yehi wajah hai ki DIIs ko "market stabiliser" kaha jaata hai — jab FII selling pressure daalte hain, DII apne steady retail-backed capital se cushion provide karte hain, ek poora freefall rokte hue. Yeh pattern hamesha hold nahi karta — kabhi-kabhi dono ek hi direction mein move karte hain — lekin uncertain periods mein, jaise ki abhi, yeh divergence hi market ko support deta hai.

Ek out-of-the-money call spread aur ek out-of-the-money put spread, dono same time par sell karo.
