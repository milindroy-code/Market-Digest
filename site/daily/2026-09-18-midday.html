Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Bull Call Spread

Ek lower strike par call kharido aur ek higher strike par call becho, same expiry ke saath. Yeh aapki cost aur maximum profit dono ko cap kar deta hai (sirf ek call kharidne ke against) — matlab ek moderately bullish view ko saste mein express karne ka tareeka, jahan aap unlimited upside chhodte ho ek lower breakeven ke badle mein. Aaj jaisa broad-based move — jahan gap-up hold kar raha hai aur banks bhi shaamil ho gaye hain — is tarah ki strategy ka classic use-case hai.
