Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Long Straddle

Same strike aur expiry par ek call aur ek put buy kiya jaata hai. Aapko profit hota hai agar underlying kisi bhi direction mein bada move kare — aap direction par nahi, volatility par hi bet kar rahe hain. Cost dono options ka combined premium hota hai, jo aapka maximum loss bhi hai agar stock chup-chaap pada rahe. Traders is strategy ko binary events (jaise Fed decision ya earnings call) se pehle use karte hain. Aaj ka session exactly waisa hi dikha — subah ka weak open din chadhte-chadhte ek sharp green reversal mein badal gaya, aur PB Fintech jaisa single-stock move bhi utni hi volatility dikhata hai jitni is concept ko relevant banati hai.

Yeh digest sirf news summary hai, investment advice nahi. Strategy Corner ek concept explain karta hai, use karne ki recommendation nahi. Numbers multiple sources se liye gaye hain aur revise ho sakte hain — trading se pehle apne broker ya exchange par live prices confirm karein.
