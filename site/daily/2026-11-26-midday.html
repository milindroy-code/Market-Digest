Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Delta Hedging

Delta measure karta hai ki ek option ka price underlying mein ₹1 ke move ke liye kitna move karta hai. Large options traders aur market makers apne book ka delta offset karne ke liye underlying stock buy ya sell karte hain, choti price moves ke against roughly neutral rehte hue — isi tarah institutional desks bina direction par bet kiye size mein options sell kar paate hain. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "high-volatility" tag ke saath — jo aaj ke actual conditions (VIX stable, koi panic nahi, modest gains) se ek loose fit hai, flag kiya jaa raha hai rather than force-fit kiya jaaye. Phir bhi relevant hai: calm-looking din par bhi, institutional desks background mein apna delta-hedging continue karte hain, chahe headline-level volatility na dikhe. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
