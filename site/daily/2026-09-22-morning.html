Explainer

US 10-year Treasury yield ka 5% ke neeche girna Nasdaq ko record high tak kaise pahuncha deta hai — aur ismein AI stocks ka itna bada role kyun hai? Bond yield asal mein ek "discount rate" hai jisse investors kisi company ke future earnings ki aaj ki value calculate karte hain. Jab yields girti hain, to future mein aane wale profits ka aaj ka present value badh jaata hai — aur yeh effect un companies ke liye sabse zyada strong hota hai jinki zyada tar valuation door future ke growth par based hai, na ki aaj ke earnings par. AI aur chip stocks exactly isi bucket mein aate hain: unki valuations mostly ismein baked-in hain ki aane wale saalon mein AI-driven demand kitna bada hoga, isliye jab yields girti hain, unki price sabse zyada move karti hai — jaisa aaj Intel, AMD aur Arm ke double-digit percentage moves mein dikha. Yehi wajah hai ki ek hi din, falling oil, falling yields aur AI-optimism teeno saath milkar Nasdaq ko ek fresh record tak le gaye, chahe pichle hafte ka Fed hike abhi bhi technically "hawkish" tha.

Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur paisa daalna, apni average cost kam karne ke liye — risky hai, kyunki aap ek aise trade mein add kar rahe hain jo already aapke against ja raha hai. Pyramiding iska ulta hai: ek winning position mein add karna jab woh aapke favor mein move kar rahi ho. Zyada tar experienced traders averaging into weakness ke bajaye pyramiding into strength ko prefer karte hain, kyunki isme market khud aapki thesis ko confirm kar raha hota hai, aap market se lad nahi rahe hote. Aaj jaise din, jab Wall Street ka overnight rally ek naya resistance test la sakta hai, yeh discipline especially matter karta hai.
