Explainer

GMP — Grey Market Premium — asal mein hai kya, aur ise kitna seriously lena chahiye? GMP ek IPO ke shares ka unofficial, unregulated price hai jo listing se pehle hi ek informal (grey) market mein trade hota hai — ek tarah se market ka "guess" ki stock listing ke din kitne upar ya neeche khulega. Yeh sirf demand-supply sentiment capture karta hai: zyada demand, zyada GMP. Lekin iski teen badi limitations hain — pehla, yeh ek illegal/unregulated market hai jiska koi official settlement nahi; doosra, GMP listing ke din tak badalta rehta hai, kabhi-kabhi sharply; aur teesra, yeh overall market mood (jaise aaj ka weak-week sentiment) se bhi influence hota hai, sirf company ke fundamentals se nahi. Isi wajah se Orient Cables ka strong ₹107 GMP aur AceVector ka near-zero GMP ek saath exist kar sakte hain — dono alag business, alag investor-perception, lekin dono hi 5 October ko actual listing din tak sirf ek indicative signal hain, ek guarantee nahi.

Calendar Spread

Ek near-term option becho aur same strike par ek longer-dated option khareedo. Near-term option zyada tezi se time-decay hota hai (expiry jitni paas aati hai, decay utni tez hoti hai), isliye agar stock roughly flat rehta hai, to yeh position in dono decay-rates ke farq se profit kar sakta hai — ek strategy jo direction par nahi, time par based hai. Market holiday jaisa ek quiet stretch — jahan koi fresh directional trigger nahi hai — exactly woh type ka setup hai jahan traders range-bound, time-based strategies explore karte hain.
