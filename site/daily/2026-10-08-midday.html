Protective Put

Agar aapke paas already koi stock hai (jaise ek IT holding), to usi par ek put option khareedna — bilkul insurance ki tarah. Agar stock girta hai, to put ki value badhti hai aur loss offset ho jaata hai; agar nahi girta, to aapne thoda sa premium "peace of mind" ke liye diya — wahi logic jo car insurance ke peeche hota hai jise aap kabhi use na karna chahein. Aaj jaisa din — jahan TCS ke results raat ko aane wale hain aur poora IT pack unke reaction ka wait kar raha hai — exactly woh scenario hai jahan investors apni existing holdings ko bina beche protect karna chahte hain.
