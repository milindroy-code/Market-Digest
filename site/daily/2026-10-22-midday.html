Sector-wise, Nifty FMCG -1%+ se zyada gira hai, aur Chemical, Auto aur Metal bhi underperform kar rahe hain; Power Grid din ka sabse bada loser hai (~-2.5%), sath mein Mahindra & Mahindra aur Nestle India bhi laggards hain. Calendar Spread

Ek near-term option becho aur same strike par ek longer-dated option khareedo. Near-term option fast decay karta hai (expiry ke kareeb time decay accelerate hota hai), toh agar stock roughly flat rehta hai, yeh position us decay-rate ke difference se profit kar sakti hai — ek strategy jo time ke around built hai, direction ke nahi.
