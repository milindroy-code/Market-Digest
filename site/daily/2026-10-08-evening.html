Explainer

IPO ka subscription number aakhri din itna dramatically kyun badal jaata hai? Vishal Nirmiti ka hi example lo: bidding ke beech ke dino mein overall subscription sirf ~0.57x-0.73x dikh raha tha, lekin aakhri din band hote-hote woh 1.71x tak pahunch gaya. Iski wajah IPO ke teen investor categories ka alag-alag behavior hai. Retail aur NII (non-institutional) investors apni bids aksar pehle 2-3 dino mein hi daal dete hain, demand dekh kar. Lekin QIB (Qualified Institutional Buyers) — mutual funds, insurance companies, FIIs — jaanbujhkar apni bulk ki bidding aakhri din, aksar aakhri ek-do ghanton mein hi daalte hain, kyunki woh pehle retail/NII ka demand-read dekhna chahte hain aur apna pricing-risk minimize karna chahte hain. Yehi wajah hai ki QIB category ka number akela subscription-window ke aakhri ghanton mein sabse zyada move karta hai — Vishal Nirmiti mein QIB 1.27x tak pahuncha, jo ek healthy institutional interest dikhata hai, bhale hi shuru ka retail/NII demand muted dikh raha tha.
