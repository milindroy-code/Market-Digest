Explainer: Fed Ka Faisla Aaj Raat — Toh Asar Kab Dikhega?

Aaj raat US Federal Reserve apna rate decision announce karega — US Eastern Time 2:00 PM par, yaani IST mein raat ~11:30 baje ke aas-paas, press conference ~12:00 baje ke baad. Lekin Indian stock market toh usse kaafi pehle, shaam 3:30 baje hi, band ho chuka hoga. Toh kya aaj ka decision kal tak ke liye completely irrelevant hai Indian investors ke liye? Nahi — bas uska asar seedha nahi, ek transmission chain ke through aata hai. Sabse pehle US stock market khud react karta hai (raat ko hi), phir woh move Asian markets mein carry hota hai jab woh subah khulte hain, aur phir GIFT Nifty — jo almost 24 ghante trade hota hai — us poore overnight sentiment ko absorb karke India ke apne open se pehle ek directional signal de deta hai. Isi wajah se is digest ki playbook kehti hai ki Fed decisions ka "poora market reaction" aksar Indian market ke agle poore trading session mein hi dikhta hai, same-din mein nahi — jaisa aaj ke liye kal (29 October) hoga.

Protective Put

Agar aap pehle se koi stock hold karte hain, toh usi stock par ek put option khareedo — bilkul insurance ki tarah. Agar stock girta hai, toh put ki value badhti hai aur loss ko offset kar deti hai; agar nahi girta, toh aapne bas thoda-sa premium diya, bilkul usi tarah jaise car insurance ke liye paisa dete hain jise use karne ki ummeed nahi karte. Yeh ek common tarika hai kisi uncertain event — jaise aaj raat ka Fed decision — ke through poori tarah exit kiye bina invested rehne ka.
