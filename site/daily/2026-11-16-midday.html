Friday ka confirmed close tha Nifty 25,144.30, Sensex 81,592.45. Early trade mein (~9:25-9:26 AM IST) Upstox aur HDFC Sky dono cross-confirm karte hain Sensex ~81,984-82,011 (+392 se +418 points/+0.48% se +0.51%) aur Nifty 25,227.40-25,227.60 (+83 points/+0.33%), jo briefly 25,250 cross kar gaya tha. Gains uske baad aur widen hue — Business Standard ke ~10:00 AM IST read ke mutabik Sensex 82,292.86 (+700.41 points/+0.86%) tak pahuncha, aur Zee Business ka early-trade read bhi similar range (~82,195) confirm karta hai. Nifty ka koi later cross-confirmed read is run mein nahi mila — flagged rather than guessed at. Driver mixed hai: ek taraf capital rotation hai — FIIs weak Asian markets (Taiwan -3.33%, Kospi -2.29%) se nikal kar Indian stocks mein aa rahe hain, November mein ab tak $8 billion+ ka FII inflow (Dec 2020 ke baad ka highest monthly total, HDFC Sky), aur crude bhi thoda aur ease hua hai (Brent ~$95.12/-0.75%, WTI ~$88.11/-0.51%) jo import bill/inflation/rupee ke liye helpful hai. Doosri taraf strong Q2 earnings hain — Eicher Motors (consolidated PAT +14.9% YoY to ₹1,573.37 crore) aur Godrej Industries (consolidated profit +130.8%). IT (+1.04%) aur PSU Bank (+1.15%) sabse aage rahe sectors mein. Lekin do genuinely sharp moves bhi hain jo "no circuit filter, bas ek routine gap-up din" wali simple story se alag hain: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles ek bada single-stock drag raha — Q2 consolidated net loss ₹5,531 crore (exceptional items ki wajah se) ke baad stock early trade mein hi 8.73% tak gir gaya (10:39 AM update), headline-level reads ise ~9% bata rahe hain. Aur Eicher Motors khud ek sharp intraday reversal dikha gaya — results ke baad stock +4% (₹7,947 high) tak gaya tha, lekin phir pare karke 11:15 AM tak -0.28% par ₹7,625.50 tak aa gaya, kyunki analysts (Citi, CLSA, Nomura, Jefferies) ne higher other-expenses/staff-costs se margin pressure flag kiya. Sabse bada "breaking" item hai ek macro surprise — MCX gold December futures ~11:43 AM tak -1.39% (₹1,34,802), intraday low -1.7% (₹1,34,385) tak gira, aur silver -3.75% (₹1,69,700), ek record-low intraday -4.04% (₹1,69,194) tak — stronger US dollar, elevated US Treasury yields, aur October US CPI ke +0.4% MoM print ke baad, jisne CME FedWatch ke mutabik December Fed rate-hike (sirf hold nahi) ki odds ko 92% tak push kar diya hai. Yeh aaj subah ki edition ke "hot PPI, rate-uncertainty" framing se ek genuine step aage hai — ab market ek actual 25bp hike ko hi base case maan raha hai.

Iron Condor

Ek hi samay par ek out-of-the-money call spread aur ek out-of-the-money put spread sell karna. Aapko upfront premium milta hai aur aap use rakh lete hain agar underlying expiry tak ek range ke andar rahe — yeh woh trade hai jo market ke "kahin na jaane" se profit karta hai. Risk dono sides par capped hota hai, isi liye yeh sideways markets mein ek popular income strategy hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "range-bound" tag ke saath — lekin aaj ka session genuinely ek trending gap-up-aur-extend session raha hai, range-bound nahi, toh yeh ek acknowledged mismatch hai (jaisa pichle kuch editions mein bhi hua hai). Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
