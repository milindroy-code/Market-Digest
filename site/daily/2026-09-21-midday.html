Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Risk-Reward Ratio

Koi bhi trade lene se pehle compare karo ki aap kitna lose kar sakte ho (apne stop-loss ki distance) versus kitna gain kar sakte ho (apne target ki distance). Ek 1:3 risk-reward ratio wala trade overall profitable reh sakta hai chahe aap zyada baar wrong ho jao correct hone se — isi liye professional traders sirf win-rate ki baat nahi karte, "expectancy" ki baat karte hain. Aaj jaisa divergent din — jahan index up hai lekin Tata Group down hai — dono taraf ke setups ka risk-reward alag-alag hoga, aur woh hi decide karega ki trade lena sensible hai ya nahi.
