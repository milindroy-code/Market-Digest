Explainer

Tata Trusts ne kaha ki Chandrasekaran ki reappointment "void ab initio" hai — iska matlab kya hota hai, aur ek board vote jo already ho chuka hai, hafton baad kaise undo ho sakta hai? "Void ab initio" ek legal Latin term hai jiska matlab hai "shuruaat se hi void" — yeh nahi ki resolution valid tha aur baad mein cancel ho gaya, balki yeh ki woh us moment se hi legally invalid tha jab pass hua, jaise woh kabhi hua hi na ho. Tata Trusts ka argument procedural/quorum technicalities par based hai: unka kehna hai ki Chandrasekaran ki reappointment kabhi validly pass hi nahi hui, kyunki yeh sawaal abhi tak unresolved hai ki Tata Sons mein Tata Trusts ke 66% stake ko vote mein kaun represent karta hai. Yeh ek valid resolution se bahut alag hai jo court mein challenge ho aur baad mein overturn ho — agar Tata Trusts ka legal stance hold karta hai, to Chandrasekaran ki doosri term simply kabhi legally exist hi nahi ki. Yehi wajah hai ki yeh dispute litigation ke bajaye seedha Tata Trusts ki apni AGM mein ek shareholder vote ki taraf badh raha hai, jahan unka 66% stake directly decide kar sakta hai — aur yehi wajah hai ki volatility hamesha listed group companies (Tata Chemicals, TCS) par hi aakar girti hai, na ki khud Tata Sons par, jo listed hi nahi hai.

Aaj Ka Concept
Position Sizing

Har trade ek jaisa capital deserve nahi karta. Professional traders ek position ka size decide karte hain apne stop-loss ki distance aur apne total capital ka kitna hissa ek idea par risk karne ko tayyar hain — often sirf 1-2% per trade — ek fixed number of shares ya lots ke bajaye. Strategy khud size se kam matter karti hai; ek great setup bhi, ek oversized position ke saath, poora account wipe out kar sakta hai. Chahe headline index aaj gap-up ke saath khule ya Tata-linked volatility wapas laute, ek professional trader ka reflex sirf "kya trade karu" nahi hota — "kitna size lu" bhi hota hai.
