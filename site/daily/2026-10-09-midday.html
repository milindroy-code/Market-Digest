Kal (8 October) ke bull-trap crash ke ek din baad, aaj dopahar tak market ek sharp relief-rally dikha raha hai — Sensex 12 baje tak 704.40 points (+0.98%) uchal kar 72,297.64 par, aur Nifty 220.10 points (+0.99%) chadh kar 22,451.90 par trade kar raha tha; intraday high par dono indices ne aur zyada upar jaake dikhaya (Sensex +854 points to 72,447.61, Nifty +284 points to 22,515.95). Rally ka sabse bada driver hai IT pack — Nifty IT index apna 4-session ka losing run reverse kar chuka hai, TCS khud is move ko lead karte hue Nifty50 ke top gainers mein shaamil hai, saath mein Apollo Hospitals Enterprise aur Max Healthcare Institute bhi. Yeh TCS ke raat ko aaye strong Q2 FY27 results (revenue ₹73,188cr, +11.2% YoY; net profit ₹13,884cr, +~15% YoY) ka market ka pehla real verdict hai — is subah ki edition ka sabse bada open sawaal — aur market ne isse clearly positively liya hai, bhale hi TCS ka exact percentage move is run mein independently confirm nahi ho paaya. Ek doosra tailwind: Asia session mein crude oil thoda soften hua hai. Broader market bhi upar hai — Nifty Midcap 100 +1.18%, Smallcap 100 +0.16%. Is likhe jaane tak koi circuit filter, surprise policy move, ya koi naya negative shock kahin report nahi hua — yeh ek seedha, clean relief-bounce session hai. Ek zaroori caveat: yeh abhi bhi kal ke crash ka sirf partial recovery hai — Nifty 22,451 par hai, jo apne toote hue 22,747/22,947 resistance zone se kaafi neeche hai; asli test abhi bhi baaki hai ki kya yeh bounce shaam tak tikta hai ya ek short-covering move ki tarah fade ho jaata hai.

Trailing Stop-Loss

Ek trailing stop-loss ek stop order hai jo stock ki price badhne ke saath automatically upar move karta hai — gains ko lock karte hue, lekin position ko run karne ki room bhi deta hai; ek fixed stop ke ulat, yeh kabhi peeche nahi jaata. Aaj jaisa din — jahan market ek oversold low se sharply bounce kar raha hai lekin abhi tak genuine trend-reversal confirm nahi hua — exactly woh tool hai jo ek trader is bounce ko ride karte hue use kar sakta hai: agar rally extend hoti hai to position mein rahiye, lekin agar yeh sirf ek short-covering move nikle jo shaam tak fade ho jaaye, to automatically exit ho jaaye.
