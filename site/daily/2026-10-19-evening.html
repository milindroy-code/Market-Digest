Explainer: Profit Badhne Ke Baad Bhi Stock Kyun Girta Hai

Aaj HDFC Bank, Axis Bank aur ICICI Bank — teeno ne year-on-year profit growth report ki, phir bhi teeno ke stocks gire. Wajah simple hai: market sirf headline profit number nahi dekhta, woh profit ki "quality" aur aage ka guidance dekhta hai. Sabse important metric hai Net Interest Margin (NIM) — woh spread jo bank loans par kamata hai minus deposits par chukata hai. Agar NIM squeeze ho raha hai (jaisa Axis Bank mein 3.36% par aur ICICI mein sequentially 12 bps neeche 4.18% par hua), toh market isse future earnings ke liye ek warning sign ki tarah leta hai, chahe is quarter ka profit number kitna bhi accha ho. Isi tarah, agar profit growth mein one-off items ya weak treasury income (jaisa Axis Bank mein dekha gaya) chhupe hon, toh analysts "core" earnings ko discount kar dete hain. Yeahi wajah hai ki experienced investors sirf "profit up X%" headline padhkar react nahi karte — woh NIM trend, guidance aur earnings quality dekhte hain.

Max Pain Theory

Max Pain Theory kehti hai ki ek underlying (jaise Nifty) expiry ke paas us strike price ki taraf gravitate karta hai jahan option buyers, collectively, sabse zyada paisa gawate hain — kyunki option sellers (jo aksar bade, better-capitalized players hote hain) usi outcome se benefit karte hain. Yeh ek widely-watched lekin imperfect signal hai, ek guaranteed prediction nahi balki ek input hai jo doosre factors ke saath dekha jaata hai. Aaj ke Technicals section mein jo max-pain reading flag ki gayi hai (13 October ka stale baseline, 22,550) — wahi concept yahan explain ho raha hai.
