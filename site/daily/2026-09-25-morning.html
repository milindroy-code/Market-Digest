Explainer

GMP flat ₹0 par hone ka kya matlab hai? Grey Market Premium (GMP) ek unofficial, off-exchange price hai jispar traders IPO shares allotment se pehle hi buy/sell karne ka commitment karte hain — yeh retail investors ko ek rough sense deta hai ki listing day par stock kitne premium (ya discount) par khul sakta hai. Jab GMP ₹0 par flat ho jaata hai — jaisa aaj Varmora Granito aur Anand Seamless dono ke saath ho raha hai — iska matlab hai ki grey market participants ko listing par koi meaningful gain ki ummeed nahi hai, na hi koi bada loss ki. Yeh weak subscription numbers (Varmora ka sirf 0.27x tak) ke saath bhi align karta hai: jab demand hi tepid ho, toh market listing ko roughly issue price ke aas-paas hi price karta hai. GMP ek unofficial, unregulated indicator hai — kabhi-kabhi galat bhi hota hai — lekin ek quick, directional read ke liye useful hai ki ek IPO ko kaisa reception mil raha hai, bina allotment ka wait kiye.

Bull Call Spread

Ek lower strike par call buy karo aur ek higher strike par call sell karo, same expiry ke saath. Yeh outright call buy karne ke comparison mein, aapka cost aur maximum profit dono cap kar deta hai — matlab ek moderately bullish view ko sasta expressed kiya ja sakta hai, unlimited upside chhodkar ek lower breakeven ke badle mein. Aaj Nifty ka daily RSI ~31 par hai — near-oversold — aur US-Iran phased Hormuz-deal talks ek relief-bounce scenario ka rasta khol sakti hain. Agar aisa bounce materialize hota hai, toh ek Bull Call Spread poori selloff ko erase hone ki zaroorat ke bina, ek moderate recovery move par position lene ka ek cost-efficient tareeka hai.
