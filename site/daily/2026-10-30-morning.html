Explainer: Index Gir Sakta Hai Jabki Zyadatar Stocks Chadh Rahe Hon — Aisa Kaise?

Raat ko S&P 500 aur Nasdaq dono gire — lekin asal mein S&P ke 500 stocks mein se 406 upar band hue, aur equal-weight S&P index khud +1.2% tha. Toh phir index khud kyun gira? Jawab hai market-cap weighting — Nifty aur S&P jaise index apne member stocks ko unki company size (market capitalization) ke hisaab se weight dete hain, flat-equal nahi. Iska matlab: agar koi ek-do trillion-dollar company bahut zyada gir jaaye, woh akeli hi poore index ko neeche khींch sakti hai, chahe baaki 495+ companies upar hi kyun na ho. Yahi raat ko hua — sirf Alphabet ke 12.6% girne se (jiska market cap trillions mein hai), Nasdaq aur S&P dono red mein chale gaye, jabki market ka asli "breadth" (kitne stocks upar vs neeche) positive tha. Indian investors ke liye yeh samajhna zaroori hai: jab bhi "Nifty/Sensex gira" sunte hain, hamesha yeh check karna chahiye ki kya yeh ek-do heavyweight stocks (jaise Reliance, HDFC Bank, ya IT majors) ki wajah se hai ya sach mein broad-based selling hai — dono ka matlab bahut alag hota hai.

Trailing Stop-Loss

Ek fixed stop-loss ki jagah, ek trailing stop price ke upar jaane ke saath khud bhi upar move karta hai — gains ko lock karte hue, trade ko aage chalne ki room bhi deta hai. Yeh "winners ko run karne do" wali trading wisdom ka ek practical tareeka hai, bina yeh predict kiye ki trend exactly kab khatam hoga. Guruwar ke strong +1.24%/+1.05% rebound ke baad, jo traders is move mein ride kar chuke hain, unke liye yeh ek fitting concept hai — khaaskar aaj jab global cues mixed-to-soft hain aur Nifty apna naya support test kar sakta hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
