Long Strangle

Ek Long Strangle, Straddle jaisa hi hota hai, lekin same strike ki jagah alag-alag (out-of-the-money) strikes par ek call aur ek put buy kiya jaata hai. Straddle se sasta hota hai banana, kyunki dono legs money se aur door hain, lekin profit ke liye underlying ko aur bhi bada move karna padta hai. Direction pick kiye bina ek bade move par bet lagane ka ek lower-cost tarika — bilkul aaj jaisa din, jahan overnight Asia ka sharp selloff aur aaj ka sharp HCLTech-led upside dikhata hai ki is hafte volatility kitni elevated hai, chahe direction kuch bhi ho.
