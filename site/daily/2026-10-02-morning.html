Explainer

Thursday ka sabse confusing data point yeh tha: Fed ke October mein rate-hike na karne ke odds gir gaye (51% se 38%), phir bhi long-term US yields upar chadh gaye. Normally yeh ulta hota hai — agar market ko lagta hai ki central bank rates nahi badhayega, toh long-term bonds par yield girni chahiye. Jawab hai "term premium": ek extra compensation jo investors long-dated bonds hold karne ke liye maangte hain, short-term policy-rate expectations se alag. Yeh premium tab badhta hai jab investors government ke fiscal deficit aur upcoming bond-supply (US debt $40 trillion se upar hai) ko lekar nervous hote hain, ya jab AI-boom-driven corporate debt issuance bond market mein competition badhata hai. Is hafte yahi hua — core inflation data acha tha, lekin fiscal/supply worries ne usse overshadow kar diya, aur higher Treasury yields ne FIIs ko Indian equities se dollar-safe, ab-zyada-attractive US bonds ki taraf khींचा, jo is hafte ke record FII outflow ka ek bada reason hai.

Bull Call Spread

Ek lower strike par call kharido aur ek higher strike par call becho, same expiry ke saath. Yeh seedhe call kharidne ke comparison mein aapka cost aur maximum profit, dono cap karta hai — ek moderately bullish view ko sasta mein express karne ka tarika, jahan aap unlimited upside ke badle ek lower breakeven lete hain. Is hafte jaisa downtrend jab eventually reverse hota hai, toh traders aksar is tarah ke defined-risk, bullish setups ki taraf dekhte hain — full-conviction call-buying se pehle, oversold bounce par limited capital risk karne ke liye.
