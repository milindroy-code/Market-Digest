Explainer: Girta Crude Oil India Ke Liye Itna Bada Deal Kyun Hai

India apni crude oil zaroorat ka lagbhag 85% import karta hai — matlab global oil prices mein har move seedha desh ke import bill, current account deficit (CAD), rupee aur inflation par asar daalta hai. Jab Brent jaisa aaj Mangalwar ki do-mahine ki low se neeche gaya (Trump ke Iran-conflict comments ke baad), toh iska matlab hai ki India ko apna same crude import karne ke liye kam dollars kharch karne padte hain — jisse CAD improve hota hai aur rupee par pressure kam hota hai. Ek analyst ka rule-of-thumb estimate (official government figure nahi) kehta hai ki Brent mein har $1/barrel ki girawat se India ka CAD lagbhag $1.5-1.6 billion tak improve ho sakta hai — scale samajhne ke liye ek useful number, chahe exact ho ya na ho. Yehi wajah hai ki aaj Wall Street ki overnight girawat (hot US CPI print par) ke bawajood Indian markets ne apni rally jaari rakhi — cooling crude aur continued FPI buying ($5.68bn is mahine, Sept 2024 ke baad ka best) ne global headwind ko offset kar diya. Agar crude yeh recovery hold karta hai ya phir se neeche jaata hai, dono hi cases Indian market ke liye directly relevant honge.

Position Sizing

Har trade ko same amount of capital nahi deserve karta. Professional traders apna position size is baat par decide karte hain ki unka stop-loss kitna door hai aur woh apne total capital ka kitna hissa ek single idea par risk karne ko ready hain — often sirf 1-2% per trade — na ki ek fixed number of shares ya lots. Strategy kitni acchi hai, usse zyada important yeh hai ki position kitna bada hai — ek great setup bhi, agar oversized position ke saath liya jaaye, poore account ko wipe out kar sakta hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, aur yeh aaj jaise ek "extend-then-fade" session ke liye ek genuinely relevant reminder bhi hai — jo log midday ke peak momentum par chase karke bade size mein ghuse honge, unhe shaam tak ka giveback zyada hurt karta hai agar position sizing disciplined na ho. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ya strike selection ki recommendation nahi.
