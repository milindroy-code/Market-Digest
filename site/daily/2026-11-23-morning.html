Explainer: Ek "Correction" Ka Matlab Kya Hota Hai — Aur India Usi Scale Mein Kyun Nahi Girta

Raat ko "S&P 500 ne correction territory enter kar liya" wali headline bahut dikhi — yeh ek specific technical term hai: kisi recent peak se 10% ya usse zyada ki giravat ko "correction" kehte hain, jabki 20%+ ki giravat ko "bear market." Lekin iska yeh matlab nahi ki Indian investors ko bhi aaj ek matching 3-5% single-day crash expect karna chahiye. Teen wajah hain: pehla, composition — US ka yeh selloff heavily AI/mega-cap-tech-concentrated hai (Nasdaq -5.3% vs Dow sirf -1.9%, khud hi is divergence ko dikhata hai), jabki Nifty financials, FMCG aur domestic-consumption names mein anchored hai, jinki direct AI-capex exposure kaafi kam hai. Doosra, flows — FIIs poore November mein Indian equities ke net buyers rahe hain (~₹94,230cr, pehle 13 sessions mein), ek structural tailwind jo US market ke paas nahi hai. Teesra, valuation reset — Nifty ka apna 2026 ka gain US ke AI-driven Nasdaq rally jitna extreme nahi raha, isliye "hawa nikalne" ke liye utni jagah bhi nahi hai. Iska yeh matlab bilkul nahi ki India immune hai — ek sharp global repricing hamesha short-term mein sab jagah se risk-capital kheenchta hai — lekin girne ki scale mein jo gap dikhta hai, woh khud bata deta hai ki yeh kis type ki story hai.
