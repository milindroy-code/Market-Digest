Explainer: RBI Ke Naye Domestic Money Transfer Rules — Cash-Payout Caps Aur Mandatory KYC Kyun

Domestic money transfer (DMT) services — jaise ki migrant workers apne gaon cash remittances bhejte hain — ek long time se ek loophole rahe hain: ek remitter bina full KYC ke, multiple chhote-chhote cash transactions kar sakta tha, jo money-mule networks aur layering (money laundering ka ek stage, jaha illegal paise ko chhote-chhote legitimate-looking transactions mein todkar trace karna mushkil banaya jaata hai) ke liye easily exploit ho sakta tha. RBI ke naye Directions is gap ko band karte hain: cash payouts par ₹10,000/transaction aur ₹25,000/beneficiary-per-month ki cap, saath mein mandatory KYC aur har transaction par additional-factor authentication. Isse genuine chhote remitters (jaise ek daily-wage worker apna salary ghar bhejta hai) par almost koi impact nahi padta — unki typical transaction size caps se kaafi neeche hoti hai — lekin ek bade volume mein cash route karne ki koshish karne wale bad actors ke liye yeh significantly harder ho jaata hai. Yeh ek routine compliance tightening hai, na ki koi crisis response — isliye market ne isse largely ignore kiya hai, jaisa aaj bhi dikha.

Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur buy karna taaki average cost kam ho jaaye — risky, kyunki aap ek trade mein aur add kar rahe hain jo already aapke against ja raha hai. Pyramiding iska opposite hai: ek winning position mein add karna jab woh aapke favor mein move kar rahi ho. Zyada experienced traders pyramiding ko prefer karte hain, kyunki isme market khud thesis ko confirm karta hai, na ki aap market ke against fight kar rahe hote hain. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, concept tag ke saath — aur aaj jaisa ek choppy, round-trip session isko ek genuinely relevant frame deta hai: jo traders subah ke session-high (82,013/25,316) par chase karke ghuse the, unhe dopahar ka give-back zyada hurt karta — averaging down karke us position ko "bachane" ki koshish aksar aur risk add kar deti, jabki pyramiding ek confirmed move par hi depend karta hai, hope par nahi. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
