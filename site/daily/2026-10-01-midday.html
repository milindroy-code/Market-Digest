Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Covered Call

Agar aap pehle se ek stock hold kar rahe hain, to uske against ek call option sell karo. Aap premium ko income ki tarah collect karte ho, aur agar stock strike ke neeche rehta hai, to aap stock aur premium dono rakh lete ho. Tradeoff yeh hai: agar stock strike se kaafi upar rally kar jaaye, to aap strike se aage ke gains miss kar dete ho, kyunki aap us price par sell karne ke liye obligated ho. Aaj jaisa ek sector-rotation session — jahan Kotak Mahindra Bank jaise names firm hain lekin broader index flat-to-down hai — exactly woh type ka market hai jahan traders existing holdings par covered calls se extra income generate karne ko explore karte hain, ek concept jo educational hai, trade placement ki recommendation nahi.
