Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Bear Put Spread

Bull Call Spread ka mirror image: ek higher strike par put buy karo aur ek lower strike par put sell karo, same expiry ke saath. Yeh ek bearish bet ka cost outright put buy karne ke comparison mein kam kar deta hai, is cost par ki agar stock sharply gire toh aapka maximum profit bhi cap ho jaata hai. Aaj ka modest bounce dikhata hai ki panic thoda kam hua hai, lekin crude abhi bhi ~$107 par hai aur Hormuz-deal talks abhi finalized nahi hain — agar koi yeh maanta hai ki downside risk poori tarah khatam nahi hua, toh ek Bear Put Spread ek cost-efficient tareeka hai us view ko express karne ka, bina ek naked put ka poora premium risk liye.
