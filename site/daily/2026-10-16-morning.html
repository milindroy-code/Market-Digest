Explainer

Rupya FII selling ke bawajood kaise mazboot ho raha hai? FIIs lagatar 15 sessions se Indian stocks bech rahe hain — yeh aam taur par rupye par dabaav daalta hai, kyunki FIIs jo stocks bechte hain, uska paisa aksar dollars mein wapas le jaate hain, jisse rupye ki demand kam hoti hai. Phir bhi rupya Guruwar ko lagatar paanchwe session gain karke ~₹95.05/USD par band hua. Kaise? Teen aur factors FII-selling ke asar ko overpower kar rahe hain: pehla, crude oil prices soft ho rahe hain (Brent ~$104-105/barrel) — India apna zyadatar oil import karta hai, toh sasta crude seedha rupye ke demand-supply ko help karta hai, kyunki importers ko kam dollars khareedne padte hain. Doosra, dollar khud globally weak ho raha hai, jaise traders apni "dollar-long" positions unwind kar rahe hain. Teesra, RBI khud bhi intervene karke rupye ko support de raha hai. Matlab: koi bhi currency sirf ek factor se move nahi hoti — FII flows sirf ek input hain, crude aur global dollar-trend utne hi important hain.

Bull Call Spread

Buy ek call option lower strike par, aur sell ek call higher strike par, same expiry ke saath. Yeh seedha ek call khareedne ke comparison mein cost aur maximum profit dono cap kar deta hai — ek moderately bullish view ko sasते mein express karne ka tarika, jahan aap unlimited upside ke badle ek lower breakeven lete hain. Aaj jaisa setup — jahan Nifty ne apna long-watched 22,747/22,947 resistance decisively cross kar diya hai aur GIFT Nifty bhi ek firm continuation ka signal de raha hai, lekin global chip-stock picture abhi bhi mixed hai — ek classic Bull Call Spread scenario hai: ek capped, near-term bullish target express karne ke liye, poora directional risk liye bina.
