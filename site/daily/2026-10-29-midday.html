Position Sizing

Har trade ko same amount of capital nahi milna chahiye. Professional traders ek position ka size decide karte hain is hisaab se ki unka stop-loss kitna door hai aur total capital ka kitna hissa woh ek idea par risk karne ko taiyaar hain — often sirf 1-2% per trade — fixed shares ya lots ki jagah. Strategy se zyada important size hai; ek great setup bhi, agar oversized position ke saath liya jaaye, to poora account wipe out kar sakta hai. Yeh sequential rotation se aaj ka concept pick hai, ek safe "concept" tag ke saath. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
