Explainer

GIFT Nifty kya hai, aur woh Asia ke actual trading se divergent kyun ho sakta hai? GIFT Nifty — GIFT City, Gujarat mein trade hone wala Nifty futures contract — do sessions mein chalta hai: subah 6:30 se 3:40 PM IST, phir shaam 4:35 se agli subah 2:45 AM IST tak, jisse yeh US market hours ke dauraan bhi live rehta hai aur overnight global cues ko turant price mein absorb kar leta hai. Yehi wajah hai ki traders ise Nifty ke agle open ka sabse accurate same-morning proxy maante hain. Lekin woh perfect nahi hai: yeh ek thin, derivatives-only market hai, aur agar iska last trade kisi specific, short-lived news-flow par based ho, to woh poori Asia session ke broader mood se divergent dikh sakta hai — jaisa aaj hua, jab ek single GIFT Nifty read +1.91% positive dikha raha tha jabki KOSPI 7.4% crash ho chuka tha. Asli test hamesha actual cash-market open par hota hai, jab poori liquidity aa jaati hai.

Delta Hedging

Delta yeh measure karta hai ki ek option ka price, underlying mein ₹1 ke move par kitna badalta hai. Bade options traders aur market-makers apni book ka delta neutral rakhne ke liye underlying stock ko khud buy ya sell karte rehte hain — isi tarah institutional desks bina kisi direction par bet kiye, bade size mein options sell kar paate hain. Yeh concept khaaskar aisi din ke liye relevant hai jaisa aaj hai — ek taraf strong domestic uptrend momentum, doosri taraf ek sharp overnight global risk-off move, aur market-makers jaise players apni positions ko kisi bhi direction mein bina bet kiye hedge karna chahenge. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
