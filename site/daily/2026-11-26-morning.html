Explainer: Section 301 "Excess Capacity" Tariff Kya Hai, Aur Yeh Pehle Wale Trade-Deal Se Alag Kyun Hai

Aaj subah ki badi khabar — US ka India par naya 25% tariff proposal — confusing lag sakti hai, kyunki humne already February 2026 mein ek India-US trade-deal dekha tha jisne tariffs ghata kar 18% kiya tha. Woh deal ek "reciprocal tariff" mechanism (IEEPA ke tehat) se tha, jise baad mein US Supreme Court ne legally invalid declare kar diya — matlab woh poora framework ab limbo mein hai, aur dono desh ek naya interim agreement negotiate kar rahe hain. Yeh naya proposal ek bilkul alag legal tool se aaya hai: Trade Act ka Section 301, jo USTR ko kisi doosre desh ki "unfair trade practice" ki investigation karne aur usके jawab mein tariff lagane ki power deta hai — is case mein, 16 economies ki "structural excess manufacturing capacity" (yaani woh apni domestic demand se zyada banate hain aur surplus global market mein dump karte hain) ki probe ke through. India ke liye USTR ne specifically pharma, steel aur ICT goods mein US ki "dependence" flag ki hai. Dono tools alag hain, lekin result investors ke liye same hai: ek fresh uncertainty jab tak final rate (25% ya 17%) aur timeline clear nahi hoti — jo months door hai, is liye abhi ke liye yeh headline-risk hai, immediate tariff nahi.

Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur buy karna apna average cost kam karne ke liye — risky, kyunki aap ek aise trade mein add kar rahe hain jo already aapke against ja raha hai. Pyramiding iska ulta hai: ek winning position mein add karna jab woh aapke favor mein move kare. Zyadatar experienced traders pyramiding ko prefer karte hain averaging-into-weakness ke mukable, kyunki ismein market aapki thesis ko confirm kar raha hota hai, na ki aap market se fight kar rahe hote hain. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "concept" tag ke saath — aur aaj ka din is distinction ke liye ek acha example hai: GIFT Nifty ek doosra gap-up signal de raha hai (jo already-rallying position mein pyramid karne ka temptation bana sakta hai), lekin raat ke tariff-proposal headline ek fresh, abhi-unresolved risk bhi layi hai. Jo trader kal ki rally mein already hain, unke liye yeh ek acha moment hai yeh sochne ka ki woh strength mein add kar rahe hain ya sirf ek move ko chase kar rahe hain. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
