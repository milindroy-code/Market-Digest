Explainer

LTCG tax aur STT exactly kya hain, aur ek FM ka "hum sun rahe hain" comment itna bada rally kyun la sakta hai? LTCG (Long-Term Capital Gains) tax woh tax hai jo aap equities ek saal se zyada hold karne ke baad profit par dete hain — abhi 12.5% (Budget 2024 mein 10% se badha ke). STT (Securities Transaction Tax) har trade par lagta hai, chahe profit ho ya loss, isliye yeh trading ki har layer par cost add karta hai. Dono mile kar equity investing ka "friction cost" decide karte hain. Market itna forward-looking hota hai ki ek confirmed policy change ka intezaar nahi karta — sirf ek senior minister ka "hum open hain" signal bhi future cost-of-capital ke expectations ko shift kar deta hai, kyunki agar LTCG ya STT kam hoti hai, to equities har investor ke liye relatively zyada attractive ho jaati hain, aaj hi se. Yehi wajah hai ki aaj ka move sirf ek speculative comment se aaya, koi actual Budget announcement se nahi.

Bull Call Spread

Ek Bull Call Spread mein ek lower-strike call buy kiya jaata hai aur ek higher-strike call (same expiry) sell kiya jaata hai. Yeh aapki cost aur maximum profit, dono ko cap kar deta hai, ek outright call buy karne ke comparison mein — aap unlimited upside chhod dete hain, badle mein ek lower breakeven milta hai. Yeh ek moderately-bullish view ko cheaply express karne ka tarika hai — bilkul aaj jaisa din, jahan Nifty ek clear trend mein hai aur ek specific resistance level (22,747) ki taraf push kar raha hai, lekin woh level clear hoga ya nahi, yeh abhi uncertain hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, trade placement ki recommendation nahi.
