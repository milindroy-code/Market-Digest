Explainer

Ek "resistance breakout" asal mein kya hota hai, aur ek single close usse kyun confirm nahi karta? Resistance ek price level hota hai jahan baar-baar selling pressure ne rally ko rok diya ho — jaise Nifty ka 22,747/22,947 zone, jo kai hafton se index ko upar nahi jaane de raha tha. Jab price is zone ko strong volume ke saath clear kar deta hai, use "breakout" kehte hain, aur classic technical theory kehti hai ki jo resistance tooti hai woh aksar naya support ban jaati hai — purani ceiling, nayi floor. Lekin ek akela session ka close isse "confirm" nahi karta: traders ek retest ka wait karte hain — jahan price wapas us level tak aaye aur wahan se bounce kare (support ki tarah), na ki wapas us zone ke andar chala jaaye. Agar retest fail ho jaaye, use "false breakout" ya "fakeout" kehte hain, jo ek bahut common trap hai. Aaj ka close ek genuinely bullish signal hai, lekin agle 1-2 sessions ka price action hi is breakout ko "confirmed" banayega.

Bull Call Spread

Ek in-the-money (ya at-the-money) call buy karna aur sath hi ek higher strike ka out-of-the-money call sell karna, same expiry par. Sold call ka premium bought call ki cost ko partially offset karta hai, jisse trade cheaper ban jaata hai bina ek naked long call jitna capital lagaye — lekin profit bhi sold strike ke upar cap ho jaata hai. Yeh ek classic "moderately bullish" structure hai: jab aapko lagta hai underlying upar jaayega lekin ek specific level tak — exactly jaisa aaj ka resistance-breakout scenario, jahan agla target ek naya resistance level ban sakta hai, na ki unlimited upside.
