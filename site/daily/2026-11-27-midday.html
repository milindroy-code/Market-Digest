Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Ek fixed amount regular intervals par invest karna, price se independent, ek single lump-sum entry ko time karne ki koshish karne ke bajaye. India mein SIP ke naam se jaana jaata hai — yeh "kya abhi sahi time hai buy karne ka" wala emotional guesswork hata deta hai, aur historically long-term investors ke liye returns ko smooth kiya hai volatile stretches mein, bilkul aaj jaise din mein. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "concept" tag ke saath — ek safe default jo khaaskar aaj relevant hai: jab headline indices achanak reverse ho jaate hain (jaisa aaj hua), ek disciplined SIP investor ko intraday swings ko time karne ki koshish nahi karni padti. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
