Trailing Stop-Loss

Ek fixed stop-loss ki jagah, ek trailing stop price ke upar jaane ke saath-saath upar move karta hai, gains ko lock karte hue trade ko abhi bhi run karne ki room deta hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, "uptrend" tag ke saath — aur aaj ke session se ek relevant fit bhi: market subah ek sharp high tak gaya, phir dopahar tak kuch gains wapas diye, bilkul wahi scenario jahan ek trailing stop ek fixed target se better kaam karta — na bahut jaldi exit, na saare gains wapas dena, jab tak trend genuinely reverse na ho. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
