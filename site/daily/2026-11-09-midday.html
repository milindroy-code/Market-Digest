Long Strangle

Long Straddle jaisa hi, lekin aap ek call aur ek put alag (out-of-the-money) strikes par buy karte hain, same strike par nahi. Yeh straddle se sasta hota hai kyunki dono legs money se aur door hain, lekin profit karne ke liye underlying ko aur zyada move karna padta hai. Direction ke bina ek bade move par bet karne ka ek lower-cost tareeka. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, "high-volatility" tag ke saath — aur aaj ka session isi theme ko continue karta hai: ek fresh high se sharp give-back, aur Data Patterns/Finolex Cables jaise odd earnings-vs-stock-reaction divergences, dikhate hain ki direction abhi bhi genuinely two-way hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
