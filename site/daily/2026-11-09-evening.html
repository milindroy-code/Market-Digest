Explainer: Lock-In Period Expiry Ka Matlab Kya Hai?

Jab koi company IPO laati hai, toh SEBI ke rules ke mutabik uske promoters, anchor investors aur kuch pre-IPO shareholders ko apne shares ek fixed period (typically 6, 12 ya 18 mahine) tak bechne ki ijaazat nahi hoti — isey lock-in period kehte hain. Iska maqsad hai ki yeh log listing ke turant baad apne shares dump na kar dein aur retail investors ko confidence mile ki insiders ka skin-in-the-game genuinely long-term hai. Lekin jab yeh lock-in expire hota hai, utne shares achanak market mein freely tradable ho jaate hain — matlab ek sudden supply increase, jo stock par selling pressure daal sakta hai agar bade holders cash-out karna choose karein (chahe woh karein ya na karein, yeh guaranteed nahi hai). Aaj ki ek NSE data point isi concept ko scale par dikhati hai — agle kuch mahino mein (26 February 2027 tak) 116 companies ke lock-ins expire honge, jisse lagbhag $35 billion worth shares supply mein aa sakte hain. Ek savvy investor ke liye yeh ek useful calendar-based risk hai jo watch karna chahiye — kisi particular stock mein lock-in-expiry ki date ke aas-paas volatility ka chance badh jaata hai, chahe company ke fundamentals khud na badle hon.

Iron Condor

Iron Condor mein aap ek hi samay par ek out-of-the-money call spread aur ek out-of-the-money put spread bechte hain. Aap upfront premium collect karte hain aur usey rakh lete hain agar underlying expiry tak ek range ke andar rehta hai — yeh ek aisi trade hai jo market ke "kahin nahi jaane" se profit karti hai. Risk dono taraf capped hota hai, isi liye yeh sideways markets mein ek popular income strategy hai. "Range-bound" tag ke saath sequential pick, aur aaj ka session isi theme se genuinely resonate karta hai — Nifty ne intraday mein ~245 points ka round-trip kiya (25,084 high se 24,838 low tak) sirf wapas usi flat-to-marginally-positive zone mein band hone ke liye jahan se din shuru hua tha, aur poore din ka net move sirf +0.23% tha. Ek disciplined range-bound trader ke liye yehi "intraday volatile, net flat" pattern exactly woh setup hai jahan Iron Condor jaisi strategy consider ki jaati hai.
