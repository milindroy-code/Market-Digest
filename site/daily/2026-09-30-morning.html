Explainer

Nifty ka 200-week moving average test itna bada deal kyun hai? Ek "moving average" bas ek index ke pichle X sessions ka average closing level track karta hai — 200-week wala, roughly pichle saade-3 saal ka average. Jab tak index is level ke upar trade karta hai, long-term trend "healthy" mana jaata hai; jab woh decisively neeche toot jaata hai, technical analysts ise ek structural warning sign maante hain, sirf ek short-term dip nahi. Yehi wajah hai ki Mangalwar ka event itna discuss ho raha hai: Nifty ne apna 200-week MA (~22,600) test kiya, jo March 2020 ke Covid crash ke baad pehli baar hua — matlab 2022 ka bear phase ho ya koi bhi correction, market kabhi is level tak wapas neeche nahi aaya tha. HDFC Securities ke Nagaraj Shetti is bounce ko 22,900-23,000 tak "sell-on-rise" maan rahe hain, jabki SBI Securities ke Sudeep Shah 22,550-22,570 ko agla immediate support bata rahe hain — aur 22,400 tak neeche jaane ka risk agar yeh bhi toot jaaye. Concrete number: Nifty apni worst September F&O series 25 saal mein dekh chuka hai, series mein ~6.7% neeche.

Max Pain Theory

Yeh idea hai ki ek underlying, expiry tak us strike price ki taraf gravitate karta hai jahan option buyers, collectively, sabse zyada paisa gawate hain — kyunki option sellers (aksar bade, better-capitalized players) us outcome se benefit karte hain. Yeh ek widely watched lekin imperfect signal hai, ek guaranteed prediction ki jagah ek useful input hai kai mein se. Mangalwar ke session mein yehi dikha — max pain 22,600 se 22,750 tak shift hua jab options positioning ne closing hours mein reprice kiya, aur Nifty ka final close (22,716.20) us naye max-pain level ke bahut kareeb tha. Aaj bhi, jab market apne 200-week MA test ke baad direction dhoondh raha hai, max pain ka level ek useful reference point rahega — ek trading signal ki tarah nahi, balki ek input ki tarah jo batata hai ki options market kahan settle hone ki expect kar raha hai.
