Technicals & Positioning

Aaj ka close (22,716.20) is se zyada significant hai ki Nifty ne apna 200-week moving average (~22,600) test kiya hai — Covid crash (2020) ke baad pehli baar. Yeh ek major long-term support zone hai; agar yeh decisively toot jaaye to agla support ~22,222 tak dekha ja sakta hai. Explainer

Aaj ka sabse bada technical story headline number se bhi zyada important hai: Nifty ne apna 200-week moving average (200-WMA) — ~22,600 ka level — test kiya, jo Covid crash (March 2020) ke baad pehli baar hua hai. 200-WMA ek long-term trend indicator hai: pichle ~4 saalon ke weekly closing prices ka average, jo short-term noise filter karke ek bade-picture support/resistance zone dikhata hai. Jab tak price is line ke upar rehta hai, long-term bull trend technically intact mana jaata hai; is line ka pehla decisive test ek warning sign hota hai ki bahut saare long-term holders apna cost-basis ke kareeb aa gaye hain, jisse selling pressure badh sakta hai agar yeh level toot jaaye. Yeh koi guaranteed prediction tool nahi hai — 2020 mein bhi Nifty ne isi level ko touch kiya tha aur phir sharply recover kiya — lekin traders aur analysts ise closely watch karte hain kyunki historically, is tarah ke levels par institutional buying interest aata hai.

Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Fixed amount ko regular intervals par invest karna, price se irrespective, ek single lump-sum entry ka sahi time dhoondhne ki koshish karne ke bajaye. India mein SIP ke naam se jaana jaata hai, yeh "kya yeh sahi time hai buy karne ka" wale emotional guesswork ko remove karta hai, aur historically long-term investors ke returns ko smooth kiya hai is tarah ke volatile stretches mein jaisa markets abhi guzar rahe hain. Aaj jaisa session — jahan Nifty apna 200-week moving average test kar raha hai geopolitical uncertainty ke beech — exactly wahi moment hai jahan yeh discipline sabse zyada value deta hai: ek single din ke move par react karne ke bajaye, ek consistent, rule-based approach follow karna.
