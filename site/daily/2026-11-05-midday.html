Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Trailing Stop-Loss

Ek fixed stop-loss ki jagah, trailing stop price badhne ke saath upar move karta hai — isse gains lock hote jaate hain, lekin trade ko chalte rehne ka room bhi milta hai. Yeh "winners ko run karne dene" ka ek practical tareeka hai, bina yeh predict kiye ki trend kab khatam hoga. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai, "uptrend" tag ke saath — aur aaj jaise sharp, broad-based rally wale din ke liye yeh ek bahut hi sahi fit hai: jaise-jaise Nifty naye highs banata hai, stop ko bhi upar move karte rehna ek disciplined tareeka hai gains protect karne ka bina jaldi exit kiye. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
