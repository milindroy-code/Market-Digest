Explainer

US 10-year Treasury yield ka 5.11% tak chadhna Indian markets ke liye kyun matter karta hai? 10-year Treasury yield duniya ka "risk-free" benchmark hai — matlab, har doosri investment (stocks se lekar mortgages tak) ka valuation isi ke against measure hota hai. Jab yeh yield chadhti hai, toh US bonds — jo already sabse safe asset maane jaate hain — ek aur zyada attractive return dene lagte hain, bina koi extra risk liye. Isi wajah se global investors, especially emerging markets se, apna paisa nikalkar wapas US Treasuries mein daalte hain — dollar strengthen hota hai, aur EM stocks/bonds dono se outflows badh jaate hain. Yeh India ke liye directly relevant hai: FII selling ka pressure badh sakta hai, rupya weak ho sakta hai, aur cost of capital (borrowing costs) globally reset ho jaata hai, jo stock valuations par bhi weigh karta hai — especially un sectors par jinka business US clients ya US-dollar-denominated debt se juda hai, jaise IT. Analysts ab dekh rahe hain ki kya 5% ka level ek "ceiling" tha ya sirf ek "waypoint" — kuch investors already 6% ki baat kar rahe hain.

Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
The Iron Condor

Ek out-of-the-money call spread aur ek out-of-the-money put spread, dono ek saath sell karo. Aapko premium upfront milta hai, aur agar underlying expiry tak ek range ke andar rehta hai toh woh poora premium aapka reh jaata hai — yeh ek aisi trade hai jo market ke "kahin nahi jaane" se profit karti hai. Risk dono taraf capped hota hai, isliye sideways markets mein yeh ek popular income strategy hai. Aaj India VIX apne multi-month lows (10.34) ke aaspaas hai aur Nifty options ka max pain (23,450) spot ke bahut paas hai — dono hi ek tight, range-bound setup dikhate hain. Lekin caution: aaj ka Trump-Xi summit ek genuine two-sided event risk hai, jo range ko kisi bhi taraf break kar sakta hai — isliye range-bound strategies ko event ke outcome ke against carefully weigh karna padta hai.
