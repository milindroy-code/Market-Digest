Explainer

Agar hike priced in tha, to Wall Street news par kyun gira? Kyunki "priced in" sirf headline number ko cover karta hai — market Fed ki guidance par bhi trade karta hai ki aage kya hoga, aur wahi is baar ka surprise tha. Meeting se pehle, traders broadly is saal ek aur hike expect kar rahe the; Fed ke apne dot plot ne dikhaya ki 18 mein se 16 officials kam se kam utna hi expect karte hain, aur chaar log do aur hike expect karte hain. Yeh bond aur equity markets ne jo already price kiya tha, usse zyada hawkish path hai — isliye yields jump hui aur stocks gire, jabki 25bp ka move khud koi surprise nahi tha.

Long Strangle

Straddle jaisa hi, lekin aap same strike ki jagah alag (out-of-the-money) strikes par call aur put khareedte ho. Yeh straddle se sasta padta hai kyunki dono legs money se zyada door hain, lekin profit ke liye underlying ko aur zyada move karna padta hai — kal raat jaisa move, jab ek widely-anticipated event ne bhi bade swings de diye.
