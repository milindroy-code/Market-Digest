Explainer: IPO Ek Fixed Price Ke Bajaye Ek Price Band Kyun Deta Hai

Jio Platforms kal ₹1,065-1,119 ke price band par khulega — ek fixed number par nahi. Yeh "book building" process ka hissa hai, jo ab bhara IPOs ka standard tareeka hai. Company aur uske merchant bankers ek range tay karte hain — floor price (band ka sabse neeche wala number) aur cap price (sabse upar wala) — aur phir investors is range ke andar apni bids lagate hain, exact price ke saath, apni demand batate hue. Jaise-jaise bidding aage badhti hai, exchange ek "cut-off price" calculate karta hai jahan total demand total available shares ke barabar ho jaati hai — yeh final issue price ban jaata hai, jo band ke kisi bhi point par ho sakta hai. Yeh fixed-price IPOs se better hai kyunki real demand price discover karti hai, company ko guess nahi karna padta ki market kitna paisa dene ko taiyaar hai. Retail investors ko "cut-off price" par bid karne ka option milta hai — matlab final price jo bhi ho, unhe automatically allot hoga agar unki bid band ke andar hai. Jio jaisa mega-IPO mein yeh mechanism especially important hai kyunki demand ka scale — anchor book mein hi $20 billion+ ka interest — final price ko cap ke bahut kareeb push kar sakta hai.
