Explainer

Bank stocks aaj rate-hike ke darr ke bawajood kyun chadhe? Kotak Mahindra Bank aur Axis Bank ne aaj apne Q2 ke "business updates" diye — yeh full quarterly results se pehle release hone wale operational numbers hote hain (deposits, advances, loan book growth), jo company khud voluntarily share karti hai taaki market ko full results tak wait na karna pade. Yeh numbers ek company-specific fundamental signal hain — "humara business kitna badh raha hai" — jo RBI ke macro rate decision se bilkul alag cheez hai. Isliye aap ek hi din mein yeh dekh sakte hain ki banking stocks ek aane wale rate hike (jo unke margins ko squeeze kar sakta hai) ke bawajood chadh jaate hain, agar unki apni business growth itni strong ho ki woh us macro headwind ko outweigh kar de. Market dono signals ko alag-alag price karta hai — ek company ka "kitna business kar raha hai" uske "us business par kitna margin milega" se ek alag sawaal hai, aur aaj Kotak/Axis ke liye pehla sawaal doosre par haavi raha.

Max Pain Theory

Yeh idea hai ki ek underlying expiry tak us strike price ki taraf gravitate karta hai jahan option buyers, collectively, sabse zyada paisa gawate hain — kyunki option sellers (aksar bade, better-capitalized players) us outcome se benefit karte hain. Yeh ek widely watched lekin imperfect signal hai, kai inputs mein se ek ke taur par useful, ek guaranteed prediction nahi. Kal ke binary RBI event se pehle, options positioning (max pain sahit) par nazar rakhna ek extra lens de sakta hai ki market kya price kar raha hai.
