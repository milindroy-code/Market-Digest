Explainer

Ek "resistance breakout" fail ho kar "bull trap" kaise ban jaata hai? Jab price ek established resistance zone ke paar close hota hai, toh traders usse ek bullish signal maante hain — ummeed yeh hoti hai ki jo kabhi resistance thi, woh ab support ban jaayegi. Lekin yeh sirf tabhi confirm hota hai jab price us level ko "retest" kare aur wahan se bounce kare. Agar retest fail ho jaaye — matlab price wapas us poori zone ke neeche chala jaaye, jaisa Nifty ke saath kal hua, 22,747/22,947 se seedha 22,231.80 tak — toh yeh ek "bull trap" ban jaata hai: jo traders breakout dekh kar late mein bullish positions mein ghuse the, woh ab loss mein phase hote hain, aur unki forced selling hi price ko aur neeche dhakka de sakti hai. Yehi wajah hai ki experienced traders sirf ek close ko breakout confirmation nahi maante — unhe ek successful retest chahiye hota hai, aur jab woh fail ho, toh jaldi exit lena hi sahi strategy hoti hai.

Bear Put Spread

Bull call spread ka mirror-image: ek higher-strike put buy karna aur sath hi ek lower-strike put sell karna, same expiry par. Yeh ek naked put buy karne ke comparison mein cost kam kar deta hai, cost ki tarah maximum profit bhi cap ho jaata hai agar stock sharply gire. Kal Nifty ka apna breakout-level reverse ho jaana, FII selling ka 10-session streak, aur global AI-stock jitters — sab ek saath ek clear downtrend setup banate hain. Jo traders further downside par bearish view rakhte hain lekin ek naked put ki poori cost nahi dena chahte, unke liye yeh ek fit structure hai.
