Explainer

Ek well-expected event ke baad bhi market sharp move kyun kar sakta hai? Jab RBI ka rate decision jaise ek event hafton se expected hota hai, to market usko dheere-dheere apne prices mein "price in" kar leta hai — matlab outcome aane se pehle hi stocks/index us expectation ko reflect kar chuke hote hain. Mangalwar ka confident close (Nifty apne resistance zone ke kareeb) khud isi ka signal tha. Lekin "priced in" hona guaranteed muted-reaction nahi deta — agar actual outcome bilkul expectation jaisa hi aata hai, to move halka ho sakta hai (classic "buy the rumour, sell the news"); lekin agar RBI expectation se zyada hawkish ya dovish surprise deta hai — ya stance ki language alag hoti hai — to move sharp ho sakta hai, chahe direction kuch bhi ho. Yahi wajah hai ki aaj jaisa scheduled, binary din options traders ke liye implied volatility ka classic example hai: asli uncertainty rate ke number mein nahi, uske saath aane wali language aur guidance mein hoti hai.

Long Straddle

Same strike aur expiry par ek call aur ek put dono buy karna — aap profit karte hain agar underlying kisi bhi direction mein bada move karta hai, aap direction par nahi, volatility par hi bet laga rahe hote hain. Cost dono options ke combined premium ka hota hai, jo stock ke still rehne par aapka maximum loss bhi hota hai. Traders is strategy ki taraf exactly aaj jaise binary events ke time jaate hain — ek RBI rate decision, jahan outcome dono directions mein ja sakta hai — ek concept jo educational hai, trade placement ki recommendation nahi.
