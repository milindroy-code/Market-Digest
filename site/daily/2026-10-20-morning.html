Explainer: IPO Mein Anchor Book Se Public Subscription Tak — Kya Badalta Hai

Jio Platforms ke IPO ka agla step hai public subscription khulna — yeh anchor bidding se kaafi alag process hai. Anchor investors (bade institutional players) issue khulne se ek din pehle hi apna allocation lock kar chuke hote hain, fixed price par, aur unka lock-in period hota hai (30-90 din) — unhe fauran bechne ki ijazat nahi hoti. Public subscription window mein teen categories alag-alag bid karti hain: QIB (Qualified Institutional Buyers) — mutual funds, insurance companies, FPIs — jinke liye Jio ke issue mein 50% net-issue reserved hai; NII (Non-Institutional Investors) — HNIs aur corporates, 15% reserved; aur Retail individual investors, 35% reserved, jo minimum lot size mein apply karte hain. Har category ka apna subscription multiple track hota hai (kitna applied vs kitna available) — yeh number hi batata hai ki demand kitni strong hai, aur GMP (grey market premium) is demand ka ek unofficial, pre-listing signal deta hai. Jio jaisa mega-IPO mein anchor book ka strong demand already ek achha signal hota hai, lekin asli test public subscription ke numbers hi denge, jo Budhwar se Shukrawar tak aayenge.

Long Straddle

Ek Long Straddle mein same strike aur expiry par ek call aur ek put dono kharide jaate hain. Aap profit karte hain agar underlying kisi bhi direction mein ek bada move kare — aap volatility par bet kar rahe hain, direction par nahi. Cost dono options ka combined premium hota hai, jo aapka maximum loss bhi hai agar stock sthir raha. Is hafte jaisa setup — Jio ke mega-IPO ka public subscription khulna, Infosys ke Q2 results, aur HDFC/Axis/ICICI mein follow-through ka sawaal — traders ke liye exactly woh binary-event window hai jahan straddle jaisi high-volatility strategies discuss hoti hain, chahe actual direction clear na ho.
