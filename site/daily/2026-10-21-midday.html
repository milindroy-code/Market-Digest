Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Protective Put

Agar aap pehle se koi stock hold karte hain, toh uske against ek put option khareed sakte hain — ek insurance ki tarah. Agar stock girta hai, toh put apni value gain karta hai aur loss ko offset kar deta hai; agar nahi girta, toh aapne sirf peace of mind ke liye thoda sa premium pay kiya — bilkul waise jaise aap car insurance pay karte hain jo hopefully kabhi use nahi hoga. Aaj jaisa din — jab ek strong gap-up morning dopahar tak clean reversal mein badal jaye — exactly woh scenario hai jahan existing holdings ko bina fully exit kiye uncertainty ke against hedge karne ke liye protective put jaisi strategy discuss hoti hai.
