Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Long Straddle

Ek Long Straddle mein same strike aur same expiry par ek call aur ek put dono buy kiye jaate hain. Isse trader ko ek badi move se profit milta hai — chahe woh move kisi bhi direction mein ho — bas move itni badi honi chahiye ki dono premiums ka total cost cover ho jaaye. Yeh strategy tab kaam aati hai jab direction clear na ho lekin volatility ka spike expected ho, jaisa aaj India VIX ke 14% se zyada uchalne mein dikh raha hai — crude aur geopolitical headlines ke saath, aaj ka din exactly waisa hi uncertain, high-volatility setup hai jahan yeh concept relevant hota hai.
