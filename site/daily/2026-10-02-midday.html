Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Bear Put Spread

Bull call spread ka mirror image: ek higher strike par put kharido, ek lower strike par put becho, same expiry ke saath. Yeh seedhe put kharidne ke comparison mein ek bearish bet ka cost kam karta hai, lekin agar stock sharply girta hai toh aapka maximum profit bhi cap ho jaata hai. Is hafte jaisa clean four-session downtrend jab traders dekhte hain, toh woh aksar aise defined-risk, bearish setups explore karte hain — unlimited-downside put-buying ke badle ek lower cost ke liye.
