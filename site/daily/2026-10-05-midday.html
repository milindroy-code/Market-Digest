Trailing Stop-Loss

Ek fixed stop-loss ki jagah, ek trailing stop price ke upar jaane ke saath khud bhi upar move karta hai — gains lock karte hue bhi trade ko chalne ki room deta hai. Yeh "winners ko run karne dena" ka ek practical tareeka hai, bina yeh predict kiye ki trend exactly kab khatam hoga. Aaj ka din exactly yeh concept illustrate karta hai — subah ka strong open (jisne apna gain largely gawa diya hai) ya Orient Cables jaisa stock (jo apne intraday high se profit-booking mein kaafi neeche aa gaya) dono hi dikhate hain ki gains ko protect karna, sirf unka peecha karna, kitna zaroori ho sakta hai.
