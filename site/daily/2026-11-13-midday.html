Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
The Iron Butterfly

Iron condor ka ek tighter cousin: ek at-the-money call aur put sell karna, aur protection ke liye further out-of-the-money call aur put buy karna. Isme sabse zyada payout tab milta hai jab underlying exactly middle strike par finish ho — ek condor se zyada potential reward deta hai un markets ke liye jo bahut still rehne wale expect kiye jaate hain, lekin ek narrower profitable range ki cost par. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "range-bound" tag ke saath — lekin aaj ka session genuinely trending/declining raha hai, range-bound nahi, to yeh ek acknowledged mismatch hai (jaisa pichle kuch editions mein bhi hua hai). Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
