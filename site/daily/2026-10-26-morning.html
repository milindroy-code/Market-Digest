Explainer: IPO Allotment Kaise Hota Hai — Lottery, Oversubscription Aur Refunds

Jab ek IPO apne offered shares se zyada demand paata hai (jaise Jio Platforms ka ~22x oversubscription), toh har applicant ko shares nahi mil sakte — exchange (NSE/BSE) ek computerised, SEBI-regulated lottery system use karta hai jisse "basis of allotment" kehte hain. Retail investors ke liye yeh lot-based hota hai: agar aapne ek lot ke liye apply kiya aur aapka category 5x oversubscribe hua, toh aapke allotment ka chance roughly 1-in-5 hota hai, chahe aapne kitne bhi extra lots ke liye bid kiya ho — zyada lots sirf chances thoda improve karte hain, guarantee nahi dete. QIB aur NII categories mein bada allotment proportional basis par hota hai, full lottery nahi. Jinhe shares nahi milte, unka poora blocked amount (ASBA ke through) kuch dino mein automatically unblock ho jaata hai — koi manual refund request ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Yahi wajah hai ki ek IPO "22x subscribed" hone ka matlab yeh nahi ki aapko 22 guna paisa milega ya lagega — iska matlab hai ki aapke allotment ka chance sirf ~1-in-22 ke aas-paas reh gaya hai, oversubscription jitna zyada, odds utni hi kam.

The Iron Butterfly

Yeh sabse zyada payout deta hai agar underlying exactly middle strike par finish hota hai — condor ke muqable higher potential reward deta hai jab market ke very still rehne ki expectation ho, lekin profitable range kaafi narrow hoti hai.
