Explainer

WPI aur CPI mein farak kya hai, aur market WPI ke jump par kyun react karta hai jab RBI khud CPI ko target karta hai? WPI (Wholesale Price Index) wholesale-level prices track karta hai — woh prices jo producers/wholesalers ek-doosre se charge karte hain, consumer tak pahunchne se pehle. CPI (Consumer Price Index) retail level par track karta hai — jo end-consumer dukaan mein actually pay karta hai. RBI apni repo-rate decisions ke liye officially CPI ko hi target karta hai, WPI ko nahi. Phir bhi market WPI ke jump par react karta hai kyunki yeh ek leading indicator ki tarah kaam karta hai — wholesale prices aaj badhte hain to woh cost aane wale mahinon mein retail prices (CPI) tak pass-through ho sakti hai, khaaskar jab fuel/crude jaisa input cost directly WPI mein bada weight rakhta hai. Aaj ka 11.39% ka print isi wajah se chinta ka sabab bana — fuel-and-power ka 25.85% YoY jump aane wale mahinon mein retail inflation ko bhi upar push kar sakta hai, jo RBI ke rate-cut expectations ko aur door kar deta hai.

Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Investing ek fixed amount, fixed intervals par, price ki parwaah kiye bina — bajaye single lump-sum entry ka "sahi time" guess karne ki koshish karne ke. India mein ise SIP kehte hain, aur yeh "abhi sahi time hai kya" wali emotional guesswork hata deta hai. Aaj jaisa mixed-signal din — WPI jump aur FII selling se broader index neeche, lekin IT sector phir bhi rally continue kar raha hai — exactly wahi scenario hai jahan ek single entry ka timing decide karna sabse mushkil hota hai. SIP/DCA ne historically long-term investors ke returns ko aise hi volatile stretches mein smooth kiya hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, trade ya investment placement ki recommendation nahi.
