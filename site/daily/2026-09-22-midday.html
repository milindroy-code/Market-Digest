Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Delta Hedging

Delta yeh measure karta hai ki underlying mein ₹1 ke move par ek option ka price kitna move karta hai. Bade options traders aur market makers apne book ka delta roughly neutral rakhne ke liye underlying stock buy ya sell karte rehte hain — isi tarah institutional desks bina directional bet liye bade size mein options sell kar paate hain. Aaj jaisa expiry-cum-reversal din — jahan Nifty subah 23,466 tak chadhkar phir dopahar tak 23,350 ke aaspaas fisal gaya — is discipline ka importance especially dikhta hai.
