Explainer

RBI ka rate decision stock market ko kyun move karta hai? Jab RBI repo rate badhata hai, banks ke liye RBI se borrow karna mehenga ho jaata hai — yeh cost aage companies aur consumers tak pass ho jaati hai, EMIs badh jaati hain aur loans lena mehenga ho jaata hai. Companies ke liye, higher borrowing costs ka matlab hai lower future profits — aur stock prices largely ek company ke future cash flows ka hi present value hote hain, isliye higher rates = lower valuations, khaas taur par rate-sensitive sectors (banks, NBFCs, auto, realty) mein. Higher rates fixed-income instruments (bonds, FDs) ko bhi relatively zyada attractive bana dete hain equities ke comparison mein, jo stocks se paisa khींच sakta hai. Yahi wajah hai ki is hafte ka RBI decision itna closely watch ho raha hai — ek Reuters poll mein 62% economists (38 of 61) ek 25bp hike expect kar rahe hain, jo Feb 2023 ke baad RBI ka pehla hike hoga. Agar yeh ho jaata hai, to market ka reaction sirf us ek number se zyada is baat par depend karega ki RBI inflation-fighting ko kitna seriously le raha hai vs growth ko support karna.

Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur paisa daalna, apna average cost lower karne ke liye — yeh risky hai, kyunki aap ek aise trade mein aur add kar rahe hain jo already aapke against ja raha hai. Pyramiding iske opposite hai: ek winning position mein add karna jab woh aapke favour mein move kare. Zyadatar experienced traders pyramiding ko prefer karte hain averaging-into-weakness ke comparison mein, kyunki ismein market aapke thesis ko confirm kar raha hota hai, aap market se fight nahi kar rahe hote.
