Explainer

Aaj ek din mein ₹6-7 lakh crore wipe out ho gaya — lekin Hormuz se ek barrel crude bhi kam nahi guzra. Toh market itna hila kyun? Stock markets actual events ko nahi, balki un events ki future probability ko price karte hain. Jab Iran ne Friday ko 7-din ka Hormuz-reopening proposal offer kiya, market ne already ek partial de-escalation ko price-in kar liya tha — isi liye pichle hafte crude thoda cool hua aur Nifty ne ek bounce dikhaya. Jab Trump ne weekend par yeh proposal reject kar diya, aur aides ko bataya ki midterms ke baad strikes resume ho sakte hain, toh market ko apna poora "de-escalation" assumption reverse karna pada — sirf ek headline ki wajah se, koi actual supply disruption ke bina. Yehi wajah hai ki aaj Brent $108 tak chadha aur FIIs ne selling continue ki: unhe ab agle kayi hafton tak elevated geopolitical risk price karna padega, na ki sirf aaj ka fear. Is tarah ke "expectation reversal" moves aksar actual physical disruptions se bhi zyada sharp hote hain, kyunki poori market ek saath apna assumption reprice karti hai.

Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur buy karna, taaki average cost kam ho jaaye — risky hai, kyunki aap ek aise trade mein aur add kar rahe hain jo already aapke against move kar raha hai. Pyramiding iska opposite hai: ek winning position mein add karna, jab woh aapke favor mein move kar rahi ho. Zyadatar experienced traders pyramiding ko prefer karte hain averaging-into-weakness ke against, kyunki isme market thesis ko confirm karta hai, na ki aap market ke against fight karte hain. Aaj jaise sharp fall ke din, jab kayi investors apne favorite stocks mein "average karne" ka soch rahe honge, yeh distinction especially relevant hai — ek falling position mein add karna aur ek confirmed trend mein add karna, do bilkul alag decisions hain.
