Explainer: Ek US Inflation Number Indian IT Stocks Ko Kyun Hila Deta Hai

Friday ko US ka October PPI (producer price inflation) 6.5% YoY aaya — Nov 2022 ke baad sabse hot print — aur iske chalte AMD 8.75% aur Nvidia bhi gira. PPI ek leading indicator hai consumer inflation ka, toh ek hot print December mein Fed ke rate-hold/hike ke odds badha deta hai, jisse US bond yields upar jaate hain (aaj ~5.15%). AI/tech stocks sabse zyada sensitive hote hain is move ke liye, kyunki unki valuation bahut door ke future earnings par based hoti hai — higher discount rate unke present value ko sabse zyada ghatata hai, isliye yeh stocks disproportionately gir jaate hain. Yeh effect India tak do raaston se pahunchta hai: pehla, global fund managers apna tech-sector risk har jagah trim karte hain, including Indian IT majors (TCS, Infosys, HCL Tech, Wipro) jinka revenue bhi unhi US corporates se aata hai. Isi chain ki wajah se Friday ka ek US inflation number, aaj ke "IT under pressure" sector-watch note tak pahuncha hai.

Long Straddle

Ek hi strike aur expiry par ek call aur ek put dono buy karna. Aapko profit hota hai agar underlying kisi bhi direction mein ek bada move kare — aap volatility par hi bet kar rahe hain, direction par nahi. Cost hai dono options ka combined premium, jo aapka maximum loss bhi hai agar stock flat reh jaaye. Traders is strategy ko binary events se pehle use karte hain — jaise ek Fed decision ya earnings call — bilkul waisa hi setup jaisa aaj ka session hai: hot US PPI print, Fed-rate uncertainty, aur global AI/tech-stock selloff, teeno mil kar ek genuinely high-volatility backdrop bana rahe hain, saath mein is hafte ke FOMC minutes aur Nvidia earnings bhi aane wale hain. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
