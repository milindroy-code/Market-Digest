Explainer: India VIX Kya Measure Karta Hai, Aur Aaj Ka +6% Jump Kyun Matter Karta Hai

India VIX Nifty ke out-of-the-money options ke premiums se calculate hota hai — yeh batata hai ki options market agle 30 din mein Nifty mein kitna percentage move expect kar raha hai, chahe woh move upar ho ya neeche. Yeh koi directional prediction nahi hai, sirf expected-magnitude ka ek number hai. Ek quick rule-of-thumb: VIX level ko roughly 3.46 se divide karo (ek saal mein ~12 mahine ke square root ke barabar), toh ek rough monthly expected-move % milta hai — matlab agar VIX 14 hai, toh market roughly ~4% ka monthly swing price kar raha hai. Aaj VIX ~6% chadha, crude ke $114 tak pahunchne aur Infosys ke mixed Q2 guidance-cut ke saath hi — dono hi fresh uncertainty add karte hain. VIX ka chadhna market crash ki guarantee nahi hai, lekin yeh batata hai ki options buyers protection ke liye zyada pay karne ko taiyar hain, jo traders ke liye ek useful sentiment-gauge hai.

Averaging Down vs. Pyramiding

Averaging down ka matlab hai ek losing position mein aur paisa lagana apni average cost kam karne ke liye — risky hai, kyunki aap ek aise trade mein aur add kar rahe hain jo pehle se aapke against ja raha hai. Pyramiding iska ulta hai: ek winning position mein add karna jab woh aapke favor mein move kar raha ho. Zyadatar experienced traders weakness mein average karne ke bajaye strength mein pyramid karna prefer karte hain, kyunki isme market hi thesis ko confirm kar raha hota hai, na ki aap market se fight kar rahe hote hain. Aaj jaisa chautha-lagatar-weak-session din — jahan Nifty apne support ki taraf aur neeche ja raha hai — exactly woh scenario hai jahan yeh distinction matter karti hai.
