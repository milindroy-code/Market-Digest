Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Long Straddle

Ek hi strike aur expiry par ek call aur ek put buy karna. Aap profit karte hain agar underlying kisi bhi direction mein bada move kare — aap volatility par bet kar rahe hain, direction par nahi. Cost dono options ka combined premium hota hai, jo aapka maximum loss bhi hai agar stock flat reh jaaye. Traders isse binary events se pehle reach karte hain — jaise kal ka Trump-Xi summit, jiska outcome market ke liye ek genuine two-sided risk hai, chahe aaj ka move mild aur positive raha ho.
