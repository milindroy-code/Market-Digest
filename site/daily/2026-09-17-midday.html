Iron Condor

Ek out-of-the-money call spread aur ek out-of-the-money put spread ek saath sell karna. Aap upfront premium collect karte ho aur use rakhte ho agar underlying expiry tak ek range ke andar rehta hai — yeh trade tab profit karta hai jab market kahin nahi jaata. Risk dono taraf capped hota hai, isliye yeh sideways/range-bound din — jaisa aaj ka fade-then-hold move — ke liye ek popular income strategy hai.
