Explainer: Anchor Investor Bidding Kya Hai?

Jab Jio Platforms jaisa ek mega-IPO public subscription se do din pehle apni "anchor investor bidding" karta hai, toh asal mein kya ho raha hota hai? Anchor investors bade institutional players hote hain — mutual funds, insurance companies, sovereign wealth funds — jinhe SEBI rules ke tehat IPO khulne se ek din pehle hi shares allot kiye ja sakte hain, ek fixed price par, jo baad mein issue price ka hissa banta hai. Isका fayda do-tarfa hai: company ko ek bada chunk demand pehle hi lock ho jaata hai (jo IPO ki credibility badhata hai), aur retail investors ko ek signal milta hai ki smart money is issue ko kaise dekh raha hai — agar bade names jaise top mutual funds ya sovereign funds anchor round mein participate karte hain, toh woh aam taur par ek positive sentiment indicator maana jaata hai. Lekin yeh guarantee nahi hai — anchor interest strong hone ke bawajood listing-day performance alag ho sakti hai, jaisa kai recent IPOs mein dekha gaya hai. Jio Platforms jaisa ₹30,213 crore ka mega-issue ke liye, aaj ka anchor book hi pehla real-world signal hoga ki institutional appetite kaisa hai.

The Iron Butterfly

Iron Condor ka ek tighter cousin: at-the-money call aur put dono sell karo, aur ek further out-of-the-money call aur put protection ke liye buy karo. Yeh sabse zyada payout deta hai agar underlying middle strike par exactly settle ho — ek condor ke comparison mein higher potential reward deta hai, lekin ek narrower profitable range ke cost par, ek market ke liye jo bahut still rehne wala expected ho.
