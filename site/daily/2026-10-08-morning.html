Explainer

Bond yields chadhne se stocks neeche kyun aate hain, bhale hi koi "bad news" na ho? Stocks aur bonds investors ke paise ke liye compete karte hain. Jab Treasury yields chadhte hain (jaisa Budhwar raat hua, 10-year ~5.31% aur 30-year ~5.68% tak), to bonds ek safer, guaranteed return offer karne lagte hain — jisse stocks, jinka return uncertain hota hai, relatively kam attractive ho jaate hain. Doosra mechanism hai "discounting": kisi company ki value aaj uske future earnings ke present value ke barabar hoti hai, aur higher yields ka matlab hai un future earnings ko ek higher rate se discount karna — jisse unki aaj ki value kam ho jaati hai. Yeh effect sabse zyada un stocks par padta hai jinki value zyadatar distant-future growth par depend karti hai (tech/high-growth names) — exactly jaisa Budhwar raat Nasdaq aur S&P 500 dono mein dikha, dono hi -0.22% neeche band hue bina kisi specific negative headline ke.

Covered Call

Agar aapke paas already ek stock hai, to usi stock par ek out-of-the-money call option sell karna — "covered" isliye kyunki aapke paas underlying shares hain to cover the risk agar option exercise ho jaaye. Yeh ek income-generating strategy hai: aapko premium milta hai, lekin agar stock sold strike ke upar chala jaaye to aapka upside wahan cap ho jaata hai. Yeh exactly aisa din fit karta hai jaisa aaj hai — kal ek strong rally ke baad market ek pause/consolidation mode mein lag raha hai (TCS results ka wait karte hue), jahan ek pure directional bet se zyada, existing holdings par thoda extra income generate karna zyada sensible lag sakta hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, trade placement ki recommendation nahi.
