Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Max Pain Theory

Yeh idea ki ek underlying expiry tak us strike price ki taraf gravitate karta hai jahan option buyers, collectively, sabse zyada paisa lose karte hain - kyunki option sellers (jo aksar bade, better-capitalized players hote hain) usi outcome se benefit karte hain. Yeh ek widely-watched lekin imperfect signal hai, ek input ke roop mein useful, guaranteed prediction nahi. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "concept" tag ke saath - aaj jaisa ek do-tarfa, volatile session (jahan early-morning crash ka ek partial reversal ho chuka hai) exactly woh scenario hai jahan traders max-pain levels ko ek extra data point ke roop mein watch karte hain, expiry ke paas. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
