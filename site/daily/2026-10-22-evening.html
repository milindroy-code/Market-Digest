Explainer: Midterm-Timed Geopolitical Risk Crude Oil Ko Kyun Hila Deta Hai

9 October ko Brent ~$103/barrel tak soften ho gaya tha jab Trump ne kaha tha ki US midterm elections (3 November) se pehle Iran par koi attack nahi hoga — markets ne is "election-timing" ko ek temporary de-escalation signal maana. Lekin fresh reports ab keh rahi hain ki US aur Israel midterms ke baad renewed strikes consider kar rahe hain, jisne Brent ko wapas ~$110-112/barrel tak pahuncha diya hai — sirf do hafte mein ~$7-8/barrel ka jump. India apni crude zaroorat ka ~85% import karta hai, isliye yeh $7-8/barrel ka move seedha roughly ~$35-40 million/din extra import cost (illustrative estimate, India ke ~4.5-5 million barrels/din ke import volume par) jaisa asar daalta hai — jo current account deficit ko widen karta hai, rupee par pressure daalta hai aur retail inflation (petrol-diesel ke through) ko upar dhakelta hai. Yahi wajah hai ki geopolitical headlines jo "kuch hafton baad" ki timeline par hain, bhi aaj hi market ko hila sakti hain — risk-premium future ke event ka hi hota hai, sirf uske hone ka nahi.

Position Sizing

Har trade ko same amount ka capital nahi milna chahiye. Professional traders apni position size apne stop-loss ki distance aur total capital ke us hisse par decide karte hain jo woh ek idea par risk karne ko taiyar hain — aksar sirf 1-2% per trade — na ki ek fixed number of shares ya lots par. Strategy kitni achhi hai yeh utna matter nahi karta jitna size karta hai — ek great setup bhi, agar position oversized ho, poore account ko wipe out kar sakta hai. Yeh rotation ka sequential pick hai aur sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
