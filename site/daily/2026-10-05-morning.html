Explainer

Shukrawar ko ek weak jobs report aaya — to Wall Street girne ke bajaye chadha kyun? Yeh counter-intuitive lagta hai, lekin market ka logic simple hai: investors sirf "kitni jobs bani" nahi dekhte, woh yeh dekhte hain ki is data ka Fed ke agle move par kya asar padega. Jab jobs data weak aata hai (29,000 jobs vs 84,000 ka estimate, unemployment 4.2% tak chadhte hue), to ek weakening labour market signal milta hai — jo Fed ko rate hike se door aur rate cut/hold ki taraf push karta hai. Kyunki stocks, especially rate-sensitive tech names, lower rates se directly benefit karte hain (cheaper borrowing, higher present-value of future earnings), market weak jobs data ko "good news for rates" ki tarah treat karta hai, bhale hi woh "bad news for the economy" ho. Yehi wajah hai ki Nasdaq ne Shukrawar ko apna sabse bada gain banaya — ek chhota, counter-intuitive mechanism jo almost har jobs-report-day par market mein kaam karta hai. India ke liye relevance seedha hai: agar US rates lower-for-longer ki taraf ja rahe hain, to woh widening yield-gap thoda narrow hota hai jo abhi FIIs ko Indian equities se dollar assets ki taraf khींच raha tha.

Risk-Reward Ratio

Koi bhi trade lene se pehle, yeh compare karo ki tumhara downside kitna hai (stop-loss tak ki distance) versus tumhara upside kitna hai (target tak ki distance). Ek 1:3 risk-reward ratio wala trade overall profitable reh sakta hai, bhale hi tum zyada baar galat ho — yahi wajah hai ki professional traders "win rate" se zyada "expectancy" ki baat karte hain. Aaj jaisa ek din — RBI MPC ka Day 1 aur ek triple IPO listing dono ek saath — exactly woh type ka setup hai jahan naye positions lene se pehle apna risk-reward carefully define karna zaroori ho jaata hai, ek concept jo educational hai, trade placement ki recommendation nahi.
