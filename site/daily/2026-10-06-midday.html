Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Ek fixed amount regular intervals par invest karna, price dekhe bina, instead of ek single lump-sum entry ka timing try karna. India mein ise SIP kehte hain — yeh "is this the right time to buy" wale emotional guesswork ko hata deta hai, aur historically long-term investors ke liye volatile stretches (jaisa pichle hafte ka four-session losing streak) ke through returns ko smooth kar chuka hai. Yeh ek educational concept hai, trade placement ki recommendation nahi.
