Protective Put

Agar aapke paas pehle se ek stock hai, toh us par ek put option khareedna ek insurance jaisa kaam karta hai. Agar stock girta hai, toh put ki value badhti hai aur loss offset ho jaata hai; agar nahi girta, toh aapne bas thoda sa premium diya hai peace of mind ke liye — bilkul waisa hi jaisa aap car insurance ke liye paisa dete hain jise aap kabhi use na karne ki ummeed rakhte hain. Kisi bhi uncertain event — jaise aaj shaam ka US CPI print — se pehle apni position ko fully exit kiye bina invested rehne ka ek tareeka. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ya strike selection ki recommendation nahi.
