Long Strangle

Straddle jaisa hi, lekin aap same strike ke bajaye call aur put dono ko alag-alag (out-of-the-money) strikes par kharidte hain. Yeh straddle se sasta padta hai kyunki dono legs money se aur door hoti hain, lekin profit ke liye underlying ko aur bhi bada move karna padta hai. Is hafte jaisa setup — Budhwar ko khulne wala Jio ka mega-IPO subscription, Shukrawar ke Infosys results, aur bank-stocks mein follow-through ka sawaal — traders ke liye exactly woh binary-event window hai jahan strangle jaisi lower-cost, high-volatility strategies discuss hoti hain, chahe direction clear na ho. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
