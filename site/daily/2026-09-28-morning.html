Explainer

Ek US president ka geopolitical proposal reject karna, Indian stock market ko kaise touch karta hai? Jawab hai crude oil ke "geopolitical risk premium" ke through. Strait of Hormuz duniya ke seaborne oil trade ka ek bahut bada hissa carry karta hai — jab isko band hone ya restrict hone ka khatra badhta hai (jaisa Trump ke Saturday ke rejection ke baad hua), traders turant crude prices mein ek extra premium price-in kar dete hain, bhale hi abhi actual supply disrupt na hui ho. India ke liye yeh doubly important hai kyunki desh apni crude oil requirement ka ~85% import karta hai — har $1/barrel ka sustained rise, India ke annual import bill ko roughly $2-2.5 billion tak badha sakta hai, jo current account deficit aur rupee dono par pressure daalta hai. Yehi wajah hai ki GIFT Nifty jaisa indicator, jo raat bhar ke global news ko absorb karta hai, aaj subah crude ke upar jaane par hi neeche signal de raha hai — bina kisi India-specific bad news ke.

Risk-Reward Ratio

Koi bhi trade lene se pehle, jo aap lose kar sakte hain (aapke stop-loss tak ki distance) uski comparison karo jo aap gain kar sakte hain (aapke target tak ki distance) ke saath. Ek 1:3 risk-reward ratio wali trade, overall profitable reh sakti hai chahe aap zyada baar galat hi kyun na ho — yehi wajah hai ki professional traders "win rate" ki baat kam, "expectancy" ki baat zyada karte hain. Aaj jaise crude-driven, headline-sensitive session mein — jahan GIFT Nifty ek raat mein hi tepid se negative ho gaya — apna risk pehle se define karna aur usse stick karna, kisi bhi single news headline ke overreact karne se zyada important hai.

Yeh digest sirf news summary hai, investment advice nahi. Strategy Corner ek concept explain karta hai, use karne ki recommendation nahi. Numbers multiple sources se liye gaye hain aur revise ho sakte hain — trading se pehle apne broker ya exchange par live prices confirm karein.
