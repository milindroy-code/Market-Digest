Explainer

Ek Trump-Xi summit Indian markets ko kyun move karta hai? Rare earths — 17 chemical elements jo smartphones, EV motors aur military radar mein use hote hain — globally abundant hain, lekin China inki mining ka ~70% aur processing ka ~90% control karta hai. Pichle saal, US tariffs ke jawab mein China ne rare-earth exports curb kar diye the, jisse global supply chains mein shockwaves aayi thi. Isi vulnerability ki wajah se Trump administration ab ek zyada warm tone apna raha hai — kyunki predictable Chinese rare-earth access milne se expensive alternative supply chains banane ki urgency kam ho jaati hai. Yeh India ke liye directly relevant hai: agar summit se koi concrete tariff ya rare-earth deal nikalta hai, toh woh "China+1" supply-chain shift ki speed ko slow ya fast kar sakta hai — jo India ke manufacturing aur export ambitions ke liye ek bada factor hai, sirf ek headline geopolitical event nahi.

The Long Strangle

Ek straddle jaisa hi, lekin isme aap same strike ki jagah alag (out-of-the-money) strikes par ek call aur ek put buy karte hain. Yeh straddle se cheaper hota hai kyunki dono legs money se aur door hoti hain, lekin profit mein aane ke liye underlying ko aur zyada move karna padta hai. Ek cheaper tareeka hai bina direction pick kiye ek bade move par bet lagane ka. Kal ka Trump-Xi summit — jiska outcome tariffs se lekar rare earths tak kuch bhi ho sakta hai — exactly waisa hi ek genuine two-sided event hai jahan traders is strategy ko consider karte hain.
