Explainer: Ek IPO +38% Khul Kar Bhi Poore Market Ko Kyun Gira Sakta Hai

Jab koi highly-awaited IPO apne issue price se upar khulta hai, pehli nazar mein yeh ek bullish signal lagta hai. Anthropic ka ANT stock $83 ke IPO price se intraday $115 tak — yaani +38% — bhi gaya tha. Lekin jo asal mein matter karta hai woh hai open ke baad ka poora trade, aur yahan stock apna pop fade karte hue sirf ~$97 (+17%) par close hua. Isse "sell the news" kehte hain: jab koi event itna zyada anticipated ho chuka hota hai ki saari positive expectation pehle se price mein ho, to actual event khud profit-booking ka trigger ban jaata hai — early allocation-holders apne gains lock karte hain, aur naye IPO mein capital lock hone se baaki AI-linked stocks se liquidity bhi khinchti hai. Yeh sirf ek stock ki kahani nahi thi — investors ne ise poori "AI trade" ke health ka litmus test maana: agar saal ka sabse bada, sabse anticipated AI-IPO bhi apna intraday momentum hold nahi kar paaya, to yeh sector-wide overvaluation concerns ko reinforce karta hai. Yehi wajah hai ki ek single stock ka intraday fade poore Dow/S&P/Nasdaq ko 2%+ neeche le gaya, aur Nvidia tak ko fresh 52-week low tak.

Aaj Ka Concept
Dollar-Cost Averaging (SIP)

Ek fixed amount ko regular intervals par invest karna, price ki parwah kiye bina, bajaye ek single lump-sum entry ka right time dhoondhne ke. India mein isse SIP kehte hain — yeh "kya yeh sahi time hai buy karne ka" wali emotional guesswork hata deta hai, aur historically long-term investors ke liye volatile stretches (jaisa aaj Wall Street ke overnight-crash-driven risk-off mein dikh raha hai) mein returns ko smooth karta aaya hai. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "concept" tag ke saath — ek safe default jo khaas kar aaj jaisi volatile/uncertain opening ke liye fit baithta hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
