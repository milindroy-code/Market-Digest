Budhwar, 18 November ka session November ki ab tak ki >10% rally ko rok gaya. Sensex 500.38 points (-0.61%) gir kar 82,031.81 par band hua, Nifty 159.45 points (-0.63%) gir kar 25,227.95 par — Business Standard, Free Press Journal, Daijiworld aur Zee Business sab is figure ko cross-confirm karte hain — kal (17 Nov) ke confirmed close Nifty 25,387.40/Sensex 82,532.19 ke against. Dono indices Mangalwar ko nine-month highs ke paas band hue the, November mein >10% ki rally ke baad — aaj sirf ek natural pause tha, koi specific trigger nahi. Nifty intraday low 25,141.80 tak gaya, phir close tak ~86 points recover kar gaya. Broader market bhi weak raha — Nifty Midcap 100 -1.22%, Smallcap 100 -0.35%.

Is selloff ke piche koi circuit filter ya policy-surprise nahi tha — ek classic profit-booking day, overnight Nvidia ke blockbuster Q3 FY27 result ke bawajood jo abhi Indian market mein price-in nahi hua hai (neeche ka Explainer isi par hai). Nvidia ne Mangalwar US close ke baad $115.8 billion revenue (+103% YoY) report kiya, ~$108-109bn consensus se upar, Data Center revenue $107.6bn (+110% YoY) ke saath — adjusted EPS $2.68 bhi beat hua, aur Q4 guidance $130bn (±3%) diya, China compute revenue assume kiye bina. Stock after-hours trading mein regular-session close $155.46 se recover kar ke ~$163.29 (~+4-5%) tak gaya — Jensen Huang ke resignation ke baad ka pehla earnings print. Iska full Wall Street/Asia reaction abhi aana baaki hai, kyunki yeh number Indian market band hone ke kaafi der baad aaya tha.

Commodities mein gold/silver ka crash continue hua — MCX December gold ₹2,601 neeche khula ₹1,31,622/10g par, silver bhi sharply neeche (~₹1,63,775 tak, kal ke close ~₹1,69,597 se) — ek stronger dollar aur December Fed-hike ki badhti odds par. India VIX thoda badh kar 15.56 (+0.26%) par band hua, kal 15.52 se. Brent crude ~$91.55-92.92/barrel range mein raha (sources ke beech halka variance), rupee ~₹93.3-93.6/USD range mein — in sabka exact end-of-day confirmed print is run mein nahi mila, isliye range di ja rahi hai.

Explainer: Nvidia Ka Blockbuster Q3 Aaj Indian Market Mein Kyun Nahi Dikha

Aaj Nifty/Sensex gire, jabki Nvidia ne raat ko estimates se kaafi upar results diye — yeh contradiction nahi hai, sirf ek timing mismatch hai. Nvidia ke numbers Mangalwar US market close ke baad aaye, jabki Indian market aaj apna poora session chala aur Wall Street ke khud ke full-day reaction se kaafi pehle band ho gaya — iska asli test kal hoga, jab Asia aur GIFT Nifty is print ko price karenge. Doosra factor: Indian IT majors jaise TCS, Infosys aur HCLTech staffing-aur-services companies hain, GPU ya chip makers nahi — Nvidia ke data-center capex boom ka ek mechanical lift inko seedhe nahi milta jaisa US ke semiconductor-adjacent stocks ko mil sakta hai. Aaj ka selloff apne hi domestic logic se chala — ek overbought rally ka profit-booking pause, plus gold/silver ka crash jo Consumer Durables ko neeche le gaya — Nvidia se largely decoupled, same-day ke liye.

Risk-Reward Ratio

Koi trade enter karne se pehle, compare karo ki aap kitna lose kar sakte hain (apne stop-loss tak ki distance) against kitna gain kar sakte hain (apne target tak ki distance). Ek 1:3 risk-reward ratio wala trade overall profitable reh sakta hai chahe aap zyada baar galat ho — isi liye professional traders "win rate" ki jagah "expectancy" ki baat karte hain. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "concept" tag ke saath — khaas kar relevant ek profit-booking din par jaisa aaj tha, jab decide karna padta hai ki winners ko hold karna hai ya exit karna hai.
