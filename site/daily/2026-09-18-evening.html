Explainer

Same Tata Sons news pe stocks kal upar gaye, aaj neeche — aisa kyun? Kyunki jo actually move karta hai woh "kya hua" nahi, balki "kaun control karta hai" ka certainty hai. Tata Sons khud listed nahi hai, lekin Tata Chemicals, Tata Investment Corporation jaisi listed companies ke paas Tata Sons mein direct stakes hain — ek balance-sheet asset jiski value Tata Sons ki apni valuation se judi hai. Jab board ne kal Chandrasekaran ko doosra term diya, market ne use "resolved" maana aur woh embedded value re-price ho gayi — stocks upar. Jab aaj Tata Trusts (jo khud Tata Sons ka ~66% maalik hai) ne usi resolution ko "void" declare kar diya, control ka sawaal wapas khul gaya — aur woh hi stocks, jinke paas sabse zyada direct Tata Sons exposure hai (Tata Chemicals sabse zyada), sabse zyada gir gaye. Yeh ek reminder hai ki holding-company structures mein, uncertainty khud ek price hai — chahe underlying business kuch na badle.

Bear Put Spread

Bull call spread ka mirror image: ek higher strike par put kharido aur ek lower strike par put becho. Yeh ek bearish bet ki cost kam kar deta hai (sirf ek put kharidne ke against), us cost par ki agar stock sharply gir jaaye to aapka maximum profit bhi cap ho jaata hai. Aaj jaise din — jahan headline index up tha lekin specific names (jaise Tata Group stocks, Tata Sons governance rift ki wajah se) sharply neeche the — is tarah ki strategy ek concept hai jo kisi single stock ya sector par bearish view express karne ke liye use hota hai, poore index par nahi.
