Strategy Corner
Aaj Ka Concept
Risk-Reward Ratio

Koi trade enter karne se pehle, compare karo ki aap kitna lose kar sakte ho (stop-loss ki distance) us against ki aap kitna gain karne ki ummeed rakhte ho (target ki distance). Ek 1:3 risk-reward wala trade overall profitable reh sakta hai chahe aap right se zyada baar wrong ho — isi liye professional traders "expectancy" ki baat karte hain, sirf win-rate ki nahi. Sequential rotation se aaj ka pick, "concept" tag ke saath — khaas taur par relevant aaj jaise ek sharp rally ke din, jab FOMO mein chase karne se pehle apna risk-reward define karna hi sabse zaroori discipline hai. Yeh sirf ek educational concept hai, kisi specific trade ki recommendation nahi.
